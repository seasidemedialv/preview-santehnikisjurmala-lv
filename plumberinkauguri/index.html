Plumber in Kauguri - risers, water heaters and new connections

A plumber in Kauguri works in the western part of Jūrmala between Sloka and Kaugurciems, where 16,821 people lived at the start of 2024, or a third of Jūrmala’s residents.

The territorial plan describes this side as a 1960s and 1970s multi-storey estate and uniform detached-house building. So work in Kauguri splits into two parts: risers and water heaters in flats, service pipes and sewerage in detached houses.

Six boreholes, 11.5 km of rising main and Jūrmala's largest water plant

In the late 1960s and early 1970s, six artesian boreholes were put into operation in Kauguri. In 1974–1976 an 11.5 km sewage rising main was built from Kauguri to Vaivari, through which waste water is sent to the Sloka treatment plant. In 2010, 97% of Kauguri’s residents used town water, but in neighbouring Kaugurciems only 11%.

The Kauguri plant is the largest of the four treatment plants of Jūrmalas ūdens: 3,222 m³ a day. The water is treated with iron-removal pressure filters and nanofiltration, and in 2026 (until 19 August) the average hardness of the Kauguri plant’s water was 2.68 mmol/l. According to a WHO document, water above 1.8 mmol/l already counts as very hard, and Jūrmalas ūdens does not plan to supply moderately hard or soft water, so softening is up to the consumers themselves.

On the detached-house streets the network is newer. For phase IV, in 2018 networks were designed in Kauguri on streets such as Dambja, Jurģu, Kauņas, Pērļu, Rūpniecības, Staburaga, Tīruma, Uzvaras and Vētras iela. There the network is brought to the plot boundary, and the owner builds the yard part alone or with a builder.

From the blocks on Talsu highway to the Kaugurciems boundary

Multi-storey buildings between Sloka and Kaugurciems began to be built in the 1960s, first along the Talsu highway, then towards Sloka, and the 1971 general plan made Kauguri Jūrmala’s main multi-storey residential district. In such a building the riser is common property, and replacing it is usually agreed with the building manager. In a flat, your part usually begins after the riser branch and the first valve, unless the building’s documents say otherwise: taps, mixers, the water heater, the washing machine connection.

In 2010 two of Jūrmala’s six public drinking-water taps were in Kauguri. A public tap is part of the town network, but for a detached house Jūrmalas ūdens' responsibility for the water supply ends at the last gate valve before the property boundary. Everything beyond it up to the house tap is the owner’s network, and its repair is the owner’s matter too.

Kauguri’s neighbour is Kaugurciems, the urban planning monument of local importance “Kauguru zvejniekciems” No. 6086, with a 10 m height limit. Majori and Kaugurciems are the only places in Jūrmala where houses were built not only on the shore dune but right down to the beach. Repair of a historic building that can change it may be carried out there only with the written permission of the Jūrmala Cultural Space and Environmental Design Centre.

A riser section and a water heater tank in a Kauguri flat

Riser replacement. “Water riser replaced on one floor” costs from €120 per floor, and likewise “Soil stack replaced on one floor” from €120 per floor, labour price, materials not included. In January 2024 one Jūrmala block of flats was left without cold water for 10 days, because part of the frozen water pipe ran under the floor of an unheated, uninhabited flat. If a riser passes through an empty flat, make sure in winter that it is warm there.

Water heater cleaning. “Water heater clean-out with an anode check” costs from €75 per water heater, and “Wall-mounting a water heater” from €65 per water heater. In water of average hardness 2.68 mmol/l, limescale builds up on the heating element and at the bottom of the tank faster than in soft water. So in Kauguri cleaning the tank and checking the anode is planned work, not an emergency.

A basement after the storm of 29 July 2024 and the points of regulations No. 38

In the storm of 29 July 2024, Kauguri was among the parts of Jūrmala with many reports of damage, and LSM wrote that streets and yards flooded, especially in Kauguri. Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 (point 61) oblige the user to protect their basement from flooding by water and waste water, by keeping the networks watertight in their own zone of responsibility. Water on the street is a different system: since January 2023, rainwater and land drainage systems in Jūrmala have been managed by the municipality, not by Jūrmalas ūdens. If water rises through a basement floor drain, look at the house sewer and its shut-offs.

If a basement has a sink, toilet, shower, floor drains or drainage, point 34.4 requires automatic shut-offs or gate valves on the sewer. If shut-off fittings are missing, the rodding eyes are not closed airtight or there are no non-return valves, the user is responsible for any consequences (point 53). In a block of flats the basement is usually common property, so discuss this first with the building manager; in a detached house it is the owner’s decision.

Often asked in Jūrmala

Why does a water heater in a Kauguri flat need cleaning if the water plant is right here in Kauguri?

The Kauguri plant treats the water for iron and removes sulphates with nanofiltration, but the average hardness of its water in 2026 was 2.68 mmol/l, above the WHO document’s limit of 1.8 mmol/l for very hard water. Jūrmalas ūdens does not plan to supply softer water. So limescale builds up in a water heater just as elsewhere in Jūrmala. How often to clean it is set first of all by your water heater manufacturer’s instructions.

Our detached house is on Uzvaras iela in Kauguri. Is there already a town sewer there?

Uzvaras iela is among the Kauguri streets for which water and sewer networks were designed in phase IV in 2018. Jūrmalas ūdens can say for certain whether construction on your section is finished. If the network is at the boundary, the yard service pipe is built by the owner, and before backfilling the trench is shown to a company specialist.

Can pipes in a Kaugurciems fisherman's house be changed without permission?

Kaugurciems is an urban planning monument of local importance, No. 6086. A repair that can change a historic building needs the written permission of the Jūrmala Cultural Space and Environmental Design Centre; for buildings built after 1940, no design brief is needed for reconstruction. Whether your pipework needs permission, ask the centre before the work.

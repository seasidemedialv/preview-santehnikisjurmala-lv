Plumber in Vārkaļi - new homes on low ground either side of the A10

Vārkaļi is a village of Sala parish in Mārupe Municipality on both sides of the A10 road, between the Lielupe and the northern shore of Lake Babīte, 23 km from the centre of Riga.

The village lies only about 2 m above sea level, so on level ground the fall of pipes and drains has to be planned before digging.

From the A10 to the yacht port on the Lielupe bank

The A10 road runs through the village, and houses stand on both sides of it. It is 8.5 km to Spuņciems. All the land here is low and level, about 2 m above sea level, lower than in the other villages of Sala parish. The building code LBN 222-15 (point 154) requires a water pipe to be laid with a fall of at least 0.001 towards the drain-off end, and on level ground allows 0.0005. Only with such a fall can the pipe be emptied completely.

In 1988 the Varkaļi Canal was dug at the north-eastern end of Lake Babīte to the Lielupe, and the lake’s water now drains through it. The canal shows how little fall there is here for everything, drains included. For a gravity sewer from the house to the street network or to your own installation in the yard, every centimetre matters, so rodding eyes should be provided at bends, through which a blockage can be reached without digging.

On the Lielupe bank in the north-east of the village is the Varkaļi yacht port. Houses by the river and by gardens often have an outside tap, and it is the part of the pipe closest to frost. The city of Toronto, for example, advises before frost to disconnect garden hoses, close the separate shut-off valve of the outside tap and then open the tap itself so that the remaining water runs out.

Where the water starts in a Vārkaļi house: the street main or your own system

In the regulator’s 2020 decision on the water utility tariffs of “Babītes siltums”, centralised water supply and sewerage in Sala parish is named only in Spuņciems. Vārkaļi is not named on that list. So in Vārkaļi each house has its own question: where the water comes from and where the waste goes.

In the request form write what your house has: a service pipe from the street, your own well or borehole, a septic tank or a holding tank. We do not service well and borehole pumps, and we do not pump out tanks either. Our work starts beyond them: pipes in the house, water heater, taps, toilet, shower and waste pipes up to the outlet from the house.

Mārupe regulations No. 16/2025, when a network is built in the street

Mārupe Municipality binding regulations No. 16/2025 provide that the municipality co-funds connecting a residential house to centralised water supply and sewerage if the main network is built in the street by the house. Until then the municipality’s requirements for emptying septic tanks apply, namely at least twice a year.

How large a share of the costs is covered and which documents must be submitted is set by the municipality, so ask the Mārupe Municipality council. Once that is clear, you go through the yard work and its price with the plumber during the call before the work.

If you are building a house in Vārkaļi now, while the street has no network yet, it is worth positioning the waste outlet from the house so that it can later be extended to the street without rebuilding the indoor drains. The same goes for the water supply inlet: a place for the meter and shut-off valve that is easy to reach saves digging later.

Mārupe Municipality binding regulations No. 16/2025 on co-funding for connecting residential houses to centralised water supply and sewerage (likumi.lv), checked 19 November 2026.

Two jobs for a new house in Vārkaļi

Plumbing a new house. In 1989, 46 people lived in Vārkaļi, and by 2016 already 101. The village more than doubled, and in a new house all the plumbing has to be run: each sink, shower, toilet and washing machine is a separate point. One plumbing point in a new house: from €55 per point. If a finished house needs one more place, Extra water or waste outlet: from €35 per point.

Connection to the street networks. When the main network reaches your street, a new service pipe and outlet have to be dug in the yard to the junction point, and on the level ground of Vārkaļi the fall of the outlet is the main question. Yard connection to the town network: the price depends on length, depth, restoring the surface and permits. It is a labour price, materials not included.

Vārkaļi lies outside Jūrmala, in Mārupe Municipality. Even so, getting to Vārkaļi is included in the labour price, and you go through the work and its price with the plumber during the call before the work.

Often asked in Jūrmala

Should I write Vārkaļi or Varkaļi in the form?

Both work. On Wikipedia the village is written “Varkaļi”, and it used to be called Vārkaļciems too. In the form field choose “Varkaļi”, and in the notes write the street or the nearest landmark by the A10.

How much fall does a 20 m water pipe need in a Vārkaļi yard?

LBN 222-15 requires a fall of at least 0.001, on level ground not less than 0.0005. For a 20 m pipe that is a fall of 2 cm, or at least 1 cm, towards the drain-off end. With such a fall the pipe can be emptied if the house is left unheated, and on level ground it has to be planned before the trench is dug.

We are building a house in Vārkaļi with one bathroom and a kitchen. How many plumbing points is that?

A basin, shower and toilet in the bathroom, a kitchen sink and a dishwasher: five points. At the One plumbing point in a new house price from €55 that is at least €275 of labour, materials not included. If a dishwasher is connected to an already finished kitchen, Hooking up a washing machine or dishwasher: from €30 per appliance. The exact number of points and the price you go through during the call.

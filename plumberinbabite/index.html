Babīte village has centralised sewerage, so drain work here usually concerns the stretch from the house to the street network, not a septic tank.

From Babīte station along roads that meet

Babīte village grew up around the old Pūpe inn and Pūpe railway station on the line that ran from Riga to the Jūrmala resorts. The historic roads still meet in the centre by the station and form a distinct star-shaped layout. When you book work, it helps to write which of these roads the house is on and whether the yard has an inspection chamber: this helps the call to establish where your service pipe begins.

The village has two kindergartens, the private primary school “Vinnijs”, a family doctor’s practice and a post office. In buildings where many people use the toilets and sinks every day, a running cistern or a slowly draining sink becomes a problem on the first day. For such a property too, you can request the work through the form, describing in which room and at which fixture the water does not drain away.

In 2025, 1,191 people lived in Babīte in an area of 1.54 km², so the village is compact and the houses stand close to one another. If a yard pipe has to be dug up, it matters to know where the neighbour’s service pipe runs, where the inspection chamber is and whether a paved driveway lies over the route. The more precisely the route is known, the less has to be dug.

Who has served water and sewerage in Babīte since 2024

Water supply and sewerage in Babīte are provided by “Mārupes komunālie pakalpojumi”. The earlier provider, “Babītes siltums”, was merged into it on 6 September 2024, so older contracts may still show the old name.

Babīte village was among the villages for which the regulator approved a centralised sewerage tariff in 2020, together with Piņķi, Priežciems and Spuņciems. From 1 July 2026 there is a single water and sewerage tariff across the whole of Mārupe Municipality where “Mārupes komunālie pakalpojumi” provides the service. In Babīte parish, tariffs had not been reviewed since 2020 before that.

For plumbing a simple conclusion follows: water that runs away day and night through a running cistern or a dripping tap passes through the meter just like water you drink. If the meter reading keeps rising even when nobody in the house is using water, it is worth looking for a leak.

A house with no connection that has a septic tank or holding tank in the yard must, under Mārupe Municipality binding regulations No. 13/2023, be entered in the municipality’s register of decentralised systems. It is exactly such a house that can use the municipality’s co-funding for a connection, covered at the end of the page.

Two jobs in Babīte homes and yards

Cleaning the yard drain line. In Babīte village, wastewater runs from the house along a yard pipe to the street network. If water drains slowly from all fixtures at once, the blockage is usually in the yard pipe or the inspection chamber, not under one sink. Cleaning the yard drain line costs from €50 per job, and Clearing a blocked indoor drain from €40.

Replacing water pipes in a private house. From 1965 (640 residents) to 2000 (1,544), the population of Babīte more than doubled. If your house was built in that period, its water pipes may be just as old. Replacing water pipe costs from €10/m, and Repiping a whole flat or house from €500. For example, replacing 8 m of pipe in a basement at the per-metre price is at least €80 of labour, materials not included.

Babīte lies outside Jūrmala, about 13 km from the centre of Riga and 4.7 km from the parish centre, Piņķi. Even so, Babīte is on our list of places we work, and the prices of both jobs already include getting to Babīte. If the plumber comes, inspects and does no other work, you pay “On-site inspection”, from €40.

For both jobs it helps if you write in the form what the pipe is made of (steel, copper or plastic) and where the main valve is. During the call we go through the job and its price, and this information helps there.

Mārupe Municipality co-funding for a connection

Mārupe Municipality binding regulations No. 16/2025 provide that the municipality co-funds building a private house’s connection to the centralised water main and sewer. There are two conditions: the main network is in the street by the property, and the connection was built after 1 July 2022.

Before work starts, ask the municipality which documents the co-funding needs. You can request the building of a yard service pipe through the form; for the content of the work see the page connection to the networks. The price of a yard connection to the town network depends on length, depth, restoring the surface and permits.

Three things help before you apply: a plot plan showing the house, a note of which side of the street the main network runs on, and the place where the pipe has to enter the house. With them, the call can go through the route and the scope of the work before anyone starts digging.

Mārupe Municipality binding regulations No. 16/2025 on co-funding for connecting residential houses to centralised water supply and sewerage. Checked 19 November 2026.

Often asked in Jūrmala

The contract still says “Babītes siltums”. Who should you ask in Babīte about a fault in the street network?

“Babītes siltums” was merged into “Mārupes komunālie pakalpojumi” on 6 September 2024, and it now provides water supply and sewerage in Babīte. Ask this company about a fault in the street network. A fault in the house or yard can be requested through the form.

We built the connection to the street network in 2021. Does the municipality's co-funding apply to it?

Mārupe Municipality binding regulations No. 16/2025 provide co-funding for connections built after 1 July 2022, if the main network is in the street by the property. Ask the municipality about your own case.

Does a plumber in Babīte work on both sides of the railway?

Yes, the village lies on both sides of the Riga-Tukums railway, and you can request work in any part of it. In the form choose Babīte and describe the work. On both sides, getting there is included in the labour price.

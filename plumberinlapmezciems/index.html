Plumber in Lapmežciems - homes between the sea and Lake Kaņieris

Lapmežciems is the centre of Lapmežciems parish in Tukums Municipality, by the P128 road, between the Gulf of Riga and Lake Kaņieris, 48 km from Riga.

In 2025, 1,293 people lived in the village, almost as many as in 1989, so drains and water heaters here work every day all year round, not only in summer.

From the net sheds by the sea to the shore of Lake Kaņieris

On the sea side stands the Lapmežciems net-shed group, five net sheds. It is an architectural monument of national importance, No. 6138, with a 500 m protection zone. If a house is in this zone and the work needs digging in the yard or bringing a new pipe through an outer wall, first ask the Tukums Municipality council what the zone means for your particular plot.

The village has a community hall, a primary school, a library, a sports complex, a doctor’s practice, a post office and the Lapmežciems museum. Since 1989 the population has stayed between 1,213 and 1,298. In a house lived in all year, the kitchen drain works every day, and fat builds up in the pipe gradually. In such a house a blockage usually starts slowly: water leaves the sink more and more sluggishly until one evening it no longer leaves at all. If water drains slowly in several places at once, the blockage is usually in the shared pipe under the floor or in the yard, not in one trap.

Behind the village is Lake Kaņieris, a shallow lagoon-type lake of 11.3 km² with an average depth of 0.6 m. It is part of Ķemeri National Park and a Ramsar site since 1995, and the national park covers most of the parish. In such a place both the yard connection to the street network and your own tank have to be sealed.

Two jobs that year-round homes in Lapmežciems need

Drain cleaning. Drains are most often blocked by everyday things: fat, hair and wet wipes, even those marked “flushable”. Clearing a blocked indoor drain: from €40 per job. Cleaning the yard drain line: from €50 per job.

Check before buying a house. The Tukums Municipality coastal plan sets a historic development area in Lapmežciems, taken over from the Engure Municipality plan. When buying an older house in this part, it is worth finding out before the deal which pipes are in the walls and floor, where the drains run and what condition the water heater is in. Plumbing check before the purchase: the price depends on the size of the house, the number of systems and the depth of the check.

If an indoor blockage turns out to be in the yard pipe, the two jobs together are at least €90, materials not included. Lapmežciems lies outside Jūrmala, 28 km from Tukums, but here too getting there is part of the labour price.

Tukuma ūdens tariffs from 1 December 2026 and houses with their own tank

The regulator’s decision of 29 October 2026 states that the “Tukuma ūdens” tariffs from 1 December 2026 also apply to Lapmežciems parish. If the house is connected to the network, ask “Tukuma ūdens” about the tariffs and the connection point. The plumber’s work is the pipes in the house and yard.

Houses with no connection are covered by Tukums Municipality binding regulations No. 16 (23 February 2022). A septic tank must be emptied at least once a year, a holding tank often enough to prevent overflow, and the register of these systems is kept by the municipality’s water utility service provider. Pumping out is not the plumber’s work: the plumber works with the pipe from the house to the tank and with everything in the house.

If a holding tank fills faster than it used to, the reason is often in the house itself: a toilet flush mechanism that lets water run into the pan continuously, or a dripping tap, sends litres into the tank day and night that nobody uses. Repairing the flush mechanism: from €35 per job.

Tukums Municipality binding regulations No. 16 (23 February 2022) and Public Utilities Commission decision No. 85 (29 October 2026), likumi.lv, checked 19 November 2026.

Often asked in Jūrmala

The house documents say Engure Municipality. Does that change anything?

Lapmežciems parish was Lapmežciems Municipality in 2006–2009, part of Engure Municipality in 2009–2021, and since 2021 has belonged to Tukums Municipality. Older documents may therefore say Engure Municipality. Since 2021 the sewerage rules have been set by Tukums Municipality, and the water tariffs by the Commission’s decision on “Tukuma ūdens”.

May pipes be changed in a house in the net shed protection zone?

The zone is 500 m around monument No. 6138, and it follows from the monument’s use and preservation instructions published on likumi.lv. The monument itself may be repaired only with written permission from the National Heritage Board (Article 21 of the law “On Protection of Cultural Monuments”). What the zone means for your house and yard, the municipality can tell you.

Do the water tariffs in Lapmežciems change from 1 December?

Public Utilities Commission decision No. 85 of 29 October 2026 sets the “Tukuma ūdens” tariffs from 1 December 2026, and they also apply to Lapmežciems parish. The amounts are in the decision and on your “Tukuma ūdens” bill. Our prices are plumbing labour prices, and they are not linked to the water tariff.

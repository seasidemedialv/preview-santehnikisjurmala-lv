Plumber in Bražciems - Jūrmala's gateway on the right bank of the Lielupe

A plumber in Bražciems works at the eastern end of Jūrmala, on the right bank of the Lielupe by the road bridge, between Priedaine and Egļuciems in Mārupe Municipality; the A10 road runs through.

In 2010 Bražciems had neither town water supply nor sewerage, and houses took water from their own boreholes. So for a Bražciems house the main plumbing question is the move from its own system to the phase IV network.

The “Bražciems - Bulduri” contract and the meter chamber in the first winter

Connecting to the town networks. In phase IV, on 25 September 2020 a construction contract “Bražciems - Bulduri” was signed, which also covers Priedaine. The network is brought to the plot boundary for properties with at least one registered resident. “Yard connection to the town network” is charged per metre; the price is set by the length, the depth, restoring the surface and the permits needed. Before backfilling, the trench is shown to a Jūrmalas ūdens specialist.

The service pipe and outlet up to the connection point are built by the user according to the technical conditions of Jūrmalas ūdens, and the meter is installed afterwards by the company (regulations No. 38, point 11). Jūrmalas ūdens prepares the technical conditions within 10 working days free of charge, and they are valid for two years. In the house, each new connection point is an “Extra water or waste outlet”, from €35 per point, labour price, materials not included; the on-site inspection is already part of that price. “On-site inspection” (from €40) is the price when the plumber comes, looks and does no other work.

Frozen pipes. For the item “Thawing out frozen pipes” the price is set by the length of the frozen section, access and splits. If the meter is in a chamber in the yard, Jūrmalas ūdens asks you to check before winter that the chamber lid is intact and closes tightly, and, where needed, to insulate the chamber with moisture-resistant materials, keeping access to the meter. If a meter frozen in the chamber was not protected from frost, the replacement is paid by the user.

The Lielupe road bridge, the forest by the bypass and Babīte hill fort

After the storm of 1824 the mouth of the Lielupe widened, and a new ferry with an inn was set up near Brāži. Since 1959 Bražciems has been in Jūrmala, and building grew quickly after the new Lielupe road bridge opened in 1962. The territorial plan calls Bražciems the “gateway of Jūrmala” and limits building height to keep the historic scale of detached houses. In a detached house, everything after the last gate valve and inspection chamber before the boundary is the owner’s network.

The plan also mentions low-rise building by the railway and unauthorised building in the forest. A detailed plan document describes the plot Bražciems 0701, between the Jūrmala bypass, the Riga–Jūrmala highway and the Lielupe, as a pine forest with single-storey timber-frame camping-type cabins. In a light building without winter heating, pipes must be drained or insulated: binding regulations No. 38 (point 36) oblige the user to prepare their networks and fittings for winter conditions.

Bražciems has the Babīte hill fort, an archaeological monument of national importance; in 1219–1228 the Babīte (Holy Mary) castle stood here. For repairs to a monument of national importance, Article 21 of the law “On Protection of Cultural Monuments” requires the written permission of the National Heritage Board. Before groundwork near the hill fort, ask this board which conditions apply to your plot. According to the 2012 study of Lielupe flood risk, a small part of the plot Bražciems 0701 lies in a flood-risk area.

From no connections in 2010 to a network at the boundary

In 2010, none of the 195 residents of Bražciems used centralised water and sewerage, and houses had individual water supply from boreholes. At the start of 2024, 202 people lived here. Once all the phase IV contracts are completed, centralised networks were planned for 98% of Jūrmala’s residents. Whether the network is already on your stretch of street, Jūrmalas ūdens can tell you.

If a borehole stays after connecting, the two systems must not be joined directly: LBN 222-15 (point 150) prohibits combining a drinking-water system with a system whose water does not meet drinking-water requirements. After construction the owner submits an as-built survey plan and confirms that their own well or driven well is not connected to the centralised system and that rain and drainage water is not discharged into the sewer (regulations No. 38, point 13).

In the LVĢMC flood-risk plan, the protected areas “Lielupes grīvas pļavas” and “Darmštates priežu audze” near Bražciems are named among those in Jūrmala that flood in sea storm surges. If a house has a basement with a sink, toilet, shower or floor drain, regulations No. 38 (point 34.4) require automatic shut-offs or gate valves on the sewer. After connecting, your own waste water storage structures must be eliminated (point 15).

Jūrmala City Council binding regulations No. 38, points 11, 13, 15, 34.4 and 36; LBN 222-15, point 150; law “On Protection of Cultural Monuments”, Article 21. Checked on 19 November 2026.

Often asked in Jūrmala

Is Bražciems in Jūrmala or in Mārupe Municipality?

Bražciems has been part of the city of Jūrmala since 1959, at its eastern end on the right bank of the Lielupe. The neighbouring Egļuciems is already in Mārupe Municipality. For water supply and sewerage, Jūrmala City Council regulations No. 38 are binding in Bražciems, and the network operator is “Jūrmalas ūdens”.

Is connecting to the town water supply compulsory for a Bražciems house with a borehole?

No. Jūrmalas ūdens writes about the phase IV networks that connecting is not compulsory. If you connect and the borehole stays, the joint needs an air gap of at least 50 mm or a special non-return valve (regulations No. 38, point 22), so that borehole water does not get into the town network.

What should be done with the meter chamber in the first winter after connecting?

Check that the lid is intact and closes tightly, and, where needed, insulate the chamber with moisture-resistant materials, leaving access to the meter. Without agreement with Jūrmalas ūdens, you must not leave indoor taps before the meter open to protect the pipe from frost (regulations No. 38, point 51.6).

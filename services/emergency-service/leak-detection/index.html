Water leak - pressure, hoses and the pressure-reducing valve

A water leak in Jūrmala often starts with pressure. The building code LBN 221-15 allows no more than 6 bar in a domestic water supply, even at the lowest tap, and if the incoming supply has more, every flexible hose and joint carries a greater load.

In Dzintari, while the new yard service pipe has not yet been backfilled

The construction contract for phase IV, “Dzintari and Majori”, was signed on 30 March 2020. It provided for 11.6 km of water main and 12.1 km of sewer in streets that had no networks before, and the service pipe up to the property boundary is often seen as a pipe with a cap above the ground.

The owner builds the yard connection personally or with a builder, and before backfilling shows it in the open trench to a “Jūrmalas ūdens” (Jūrmala’s water utility) specialist. This is the only moment when the whole pipe and its joints can be seen.

After construction, Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 (point 13) require an as-built survey plan to be submitted. It shows the route of the pipe, and if a leak ever has to be found in the yard, digging starts where the pipe really runs, not across the whole lawn.

Two assumptions about the meter chamber in winter

Often thought: the meter belongs to Jūrmalas ūdens, so a frozen meter in the chamber is not the owner’s loss.

In fact: Jūrmalas ūdens states that if an inspection finds a frozen and damaged meter in the chamber that was not properly protected from frost, the user pays for replacing the meter and the related work.

Often thought: to stop the service pipe freezing in a frost, you can leave a tap before the meter slightly open.

In fact: Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 (sub-point 51.6) prohibit this unless it has been agreed with Jūrmalas ūdens.

Checked 19 November 2026: Jūrmala City Council binding regulations No. 38 of 25 July 2024, points 13, 32, 33 and sub-point 51.6.

Pressure after the meter: 6 bar at the lowest tap and a pressure-reducing valve at 4 bar

The building code LBN 221-15 sets 0.6 MPa, or 6 bar, as the upper limit at the lowest water outlet. Pressure is measured with a pressure gauge after the meter; if it shows more, flexible hoses and joints work under a greater load than the code intends.

A pressure-reducing valve is then fitted after the meter. For example, the manufacturer SYR gives a factory setting of 4 bar for its Drufi+ DFR filter and valve, adjustable within 1.5–6 bar. The inlet pressure must be at least 1 bar higher than the desired outlet pressure. Other models may have different figures.

Checked 19 November 2026: Cabinet Regulations of 2015 No. 332, LBN 221-15 “Internal Water Supply and Sewerage of Buildings”.

Tracing a water leak in a house with the diagrams under points 32 and 33

In a house the pipe often runs inside a wall or under the floor. Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 (points 32 and 33) require the user to keep diagrams and as-built drawings of the water supply and drains, showing pipe diameters, depth, fittings and meter points.

If such diagrams exist, they show where the supply runs to the bathroom or kitchen, and less needs to be opened up. If they do not, the regulations require inventory drawings to be prepared, and after a repair the new section is worth adding to them.

The price of tracing a hidden leak is affected by the place of the leak, access and the checking method. You go through this during the call, once it is known where the damp is and what the diagram shows. If water is already running at the neighbour’s, see what to do when a flat is flooded.

A water leak protection system: sensor, valve and 8 years

Usually such a system consists of sensors on the floor by the water heater, the washing machine and under the sinks, and a motorised valve on the incoming supply after the meter. When a sensor gets wet, the valve shuts off the water to the whole house.

Leak detectors have a service life too. For example, the manufacturer ACO requires the leak detector in its ShowerDrain Public X shower channel with heat exchanger to be replaced every 8 years. A house system likewise needs to be known for when to change batteries and sensors, and the valve must be where it can be reached.

Worked example in a flat in Kauguri

In a block of flats in Kauguri a flexible hose under the kitchen sink bursts, and the pressure gauge shows more than 6 bar. Repairing a leaking spot from €35 and fitting a pressure-reducing valve as hourly work at €25/h: in total at least €60 labour price, materials not included.

The hose and the pressure-reducing valve are materials, and you go through their price during the call before the work.

Often asked in Jūrmala

How often should the filter before the pressure-reducing valve be flushed?

For the Drufi+ filter, the manufacturer SYR requires back-flushing when the flow drops, and at least once every six months. It advises checking the valve setting once a year. For other models, see their instructions.

What pressure is the SYR 315 pressure-reducing valve set to?

The manufacturer SYR states that the pressure must be set at static pressure, when no water is running. At maximum flow the pressure after the valve drops by 1.3 bar, and the valve is well protected by the filter before it.

What pressure must the pipes and fittings in a house be designed for?

LBN 221-15 requires the pipes and fittings of a domestic water supply to be designed for a working pressure of not less than 600 kPa, or 6 bar. For a flexible hose, check the pressure on its marking or packaging.

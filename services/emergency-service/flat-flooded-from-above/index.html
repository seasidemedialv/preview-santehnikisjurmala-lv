Flooded flat - where the water starts and what to close

A flooded flat in Buļļuciems or Bulduri usually means the water comes from the floor above, from the riser between floors or from your own supply pipe.

First close your own shut-off valve, switch the water heater off at its fuse and tell the neighbours above and below. We contact you within 5 minutes of your request, and during the call we go through the job and its price with you.

In a 1960s block in Buļļuciems, when a wet patch appears on the ceiling

Jūrmala’s territorial plan states that the multi-storey housing in the eastern part of Buļļuciems arose in the 1960s, together with the growth of the “Uzvara” fishermen’s collective farm. In such blocks the water and soil risers usually run through all floors in one vertical line.

The first question is whether clean water or waste water is leaking. If it drips even when nobody upstairs is using water, it is usually the water pipe. If it drips only when the bath or toilet is used upstairs, the fault is usually in the waste pipe.

The next step depends on that. A shut-off valve stops a water pipe leak, but a waste pipe leak stops only when people upstairs stop using water.

Two assumptions in the first hour after the flooding

Often thought: Jūrmalas ūdens (Jūrmala’s water utility) will come and repair the pipe that is leaking.

In fact: Jūrmalas ūdens explains that its responsibility for the water supply ends at the last gate valve before the property boundary. All networks on the plot and in the buildings belong to the owners and are repaired at their expense.

Often thought: while the riser is shut off, Jūrmalas ūdens will bring water to the whole building.

In fact: according to LSM, the company takes part in fixing faults only when the emergency is outside the territory next to the building. In a crisis it may bring a water barrel, but in a frost the water in the barrel freezes too.

Three points where the user answers for the consequences: point 53

Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 (point 53) state that if the user’s system lacks shut-off fittings, the rodding eyes are not tightly closed or non-return valves are not fitted, the user answers for the possible consequences. A plumbing check after flooding therefore starts with these three points.

Shut-off fittings
whether the tap on the flat’s incoming supply closes fully and then does not drip along the stem.
Rodding eyes
whether the cleaning hatch of the soil stack in the bathroom is closed and its seal is intact.
Non-return valves
whether water cannot flow back where the system needs a valve, for example in the safety group of a water heater.

The aim of the check is to find the place the water came from: a joint, a hose, an appliance connection or the riser. If the riser is at fault, see riser replacement in a flat.

Checked 19 November 2026: Jūrmala City Council binding regulations No. 38 of 25 July 2024, sub-point 34.5 and point 53.

The neighbours flooded you and the riser was shut off: water heater before power

When the water is restored to the whole riser, a water heater may be partly empty or full of air. For its electric water heater, the manufacturer Lochinvar requires the tank to be filled completely and the air released before the power is connected.

To do that, the hot tap has to run at full flow for at least three minutes. If the power is switched on earlier, the upper heating element may burn out. For water heaters from other manufacturers, see their instructions.

Worked example in a block of flats in Bulduri

In Bulduri, between the Lielupe and Dzintari, south of the railway, there are also Soviet-era multi-storey blocks. In such a building the water riser leaks between the second and third floors, and a leak is found at the same time on the supply to the neighbour’s sink.

Replacing a water riser on one floor from €120 per floor and one leaking spot from €35: in total at least €155 labour price, materials not included. If the visit is at night or at the weekend, the price of an urgent visit is affected by the time, the distance and the urgency.

Often asked in Jūrmala

The bath upstairs overflowed because the water would not drain. Should the drains be checked too?

Yes, because the cause may be a blocked waste pipe rather than a burst pipe. Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 (sub-point 34.5) require the user to clean the drains in their service zone preventively at least twice a year.

A ground-floor flat flooded in a downpour from the street side. Who should I ask about it?

In the storm of 29 July 2024, streets and yards flooded in several parts of the city. Rainwater and drainage systems have been managed by Jūrmala city municipality since January 2023, so ask it about the street side. If the water came through a floor drain, the building’s drains are checked.

How do I know whether it is leaking from the water riser or from the soil stack?

A water riser leaks even when nobody is using water, and the water is clean. A soil stack leaks only when water is used on the floors above, and it often smells. Replacing a soil stack on one floor is also from €120 per floor.

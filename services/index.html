Opened in May, shut up in October: when work piles up in Jūrmala

In October the work is set by the city’s rules: Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 (point 36) require the user to prepare the water pipe, fittings and meter assembly on their side for winter. Without agreement with “Jūrmalas ūdens”, leaving taps open before the meter so the pipe does not freeze is forbidden (point 51.6).

In May, when summer houses and rented flats open up, the Health Inspectorate advises running off the cold and hot water after a long absence, raising the flow gradually over ten minutes. The hot water temperature advice for guest houses is on the page guest houses and lets. For both cases: draining a summer house for winter and prices.

Often asked in Jūrmala

Do you install pressure booster sets or borehole pumps?

No. We do not offer pressure booster sets, well and borehole pumps, water filters or gas equipment. Basement drainage and sewage pumps, though, are on the page drainage and basement pumps.

Which page should I choose if the water heater is leaking?

If water is running onto the floor, it is an emergency: close the cold water supply to the water heater and switch it off at the fuse. If the water heater only fails to heat or smells, the page water heaters fits.

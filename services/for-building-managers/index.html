In January 2024 a Jūrmala block of flats was left without cold water for 10 days, and Cabinet Regulation No. 906 requires the manager to keep hot water at the heat exchanger outlet at no less than +55 °C.

In January, when the incoming supply freezes under an unheated flat

In that building part of the frozen water pipe ran under the floor of an unheated, unoccupied flat. “Jūrmalas ūdens” gets involved in putting a fault right only if the failure is outside the territory adjoining the building; in a crisis it can bring a water barrel, but in frost the water in a barrel freezes too.

For the building manager this means a task before the frost: know through which empty or unheated rooms the incoming supply and the risers run, and look at those places together with a plumber. If the incoming supply has already frozen, see emergency plumber.

Two assumptions that the regulations change

Often assumed: a building’s drains are cleaned when they block.

In fact: Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 (point 34.5) require the user to clean the sewer networks in their service zone preventively at least twice a year.

Often assumed: the town water pipe is the town’s concern right up to the wall of the building.

In fact: “Jūrmalas ūdens” states that its responsibility ends at the last gate valve before the property boundary for the water pipe, and at the last inspection chamber before the boundary for the sewer. The networks on the plot and in the building belong to the owners.

Incoming supply under an empty flat: points 36 and 51.6 of regulations No. 38

Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 (point 36) oblige the user to prepare the water pipe networks, fittings and meter assembly in their service part for winter conditions. In a block of flats this also means the pipes that run through the basement and unoccupied rooms.

The simplest “solution” is forbidden: leaving internal taps open before the meter so the pipe does not freeze is not allowed without agreement with “Jūrmalas ūdens” (point 51.6). If the meter is in a chamber, before winter the company advises checking the chamber cover and, if necessary, insulating the chamber with moisture-resistant materials.

If an inspection finds that a meter that froze in its chamber and was damaged was not properly protected from frost, the user, not the company, pays for the meter replacement and the related work.

An association takes over a building: diagrams and a CCTV drain survey

A plumber’s first job for a flat owners' association can start with documents. Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 (points 32 and 33) require keeping the water and sewer diagram and as-built drawings with pipe diameters, depth, fittings and meter assemblies; if they do not exist, survey drawings must be drawn up.

During reconstruction or on a change of owner, if there is no as-built documentation for the sewer outlet, the owner must carry out a CCTV pipe survey at their own cost and submit it to “Jūrmalas ūdens” (point 57). After that, riser replacement can be planned.

The yearly plan: April and October in a Dubulti multi-storey block

In Dubulti, blocks of multi-storey housing were built in the 1970s between the railway and Strēlnieku prospekts. For such a building the yearly plan can be tied to point 34.5 and to the January lesson.

In April: pressure flushing of a soil stack, at least €80. In October: the same flushing, at least €80, and replacing a water riser on one floor under the empty flat, at least €120 per floor. In total at least €280 a year, labour price, materials not included. Replacing a soil stack on one floor costs the same: from €120 per floor.

Often asked in Jūrmala

May residents' belongings be stored in the basement of a block of flats that has drainage?

Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 (point 59) forbid storing food and other goods in basements where sanitary fixtures and drainage systems are installed. How this applies to a particular building, ask “Jūrmalas ūdens”.

What type of non-return valve does a basement with a toilet need?

If the basement has a toilet or floor drains, point 34.4 of regulations No. 38 requires automatic shut-offs on the sewer. For anti-flood valves the standard is EN 13564: for example, the ACO Quatrix is a type 3 device for grey and black wastewater, while the Junior (type 5) and Triplex (type 2) suit grey water only.

What hot water temperature must the building manager provide?

At least +55 °C at the heat exchanger outlet, which Cabinet Regulation No. 906 has required since 9 March 2018. If the hot water at the tap in a flat is noticeably cooler, the cause may be in the circulation or the riser, and it is checked on site.

In July, when everyone showers one after another in a guest house in Majori

If several people shower one after another in the evening in a guest house or rented flat in Majori, the water heater is chosen by flow, not by capacity alone. The manufacturer Rinnai explains it with an example: a 50 l tank can be enough even for 75 l of daily use, if the water is used in three draws of 25 l and heats up again in between.

The other side is the showers themselves. Hamworthy notes that push-button showers that switch off after 20–30 seconds use less hot water than ordinary ones, which are often left running. In a rental property the size of the water heater and the type of shower are therefore one decision, taken together.

Low temperature and a big tank: two assumptions

Often assumed: a water heater can be kept at a low temperature to save electricity.

In fact: the Health Inspectorate states that Legionella in a water supply is practically impossible to destroy, and that the technically simplest way to limit it is a constantly high hot water temperature. For blocks of flats, Cabinet Regulation No. 906 requires hot water leaving the heat exchanger to be no lower than +55 °C.

Often assumed: the bigger the tank, the better.

In fact: Hamworthy recommends sizing a hot water system by the largest simultaneous load and the recovery time. A bigger tank takes longer to heat up again, while a tank that is too small will not meet demand. The right size lies between those two limits.

Above 49 °C at the shower: a thermostatic mixing valve

A high temperature in the tank and a safe temperature at the tap are two different things. In the instructions for its electric water heater, the manufacturer Lochinvar warns that above 120 °F (about 49 °C) the risk of scalding rises, and recommends thermostatic mixing valves at the outlets.

A mixing valve blends hot water with cold to a set temperature. That way the water heater can hold a high temperature, while in the shower a child or an older guest will not get a scalding jet. In a rented flat it is worth planning this valve straight away, while the pipework is open.

Replacing a water heater in Jūrmala's hard water

Since 2023 “Jūrmalas ūdens” (Jūrmala’s water utility) has been gradually replacing the nanofiltration membranes at all its stations, and the hardness of the treated water in 2024 was 3.5–4.0 mmol/l. In water this hard, limescale builds up in the tank and on the heating element.

The new water heater is usually put in the old one’s place. At the moment of replacement it is worth thinking about servicing: whether there is room to drain the tank and open it for cleaning without taking it off the wall, and whether the pipework can be reached.

Connecting an electric water heater in a basement or utility room

The State Fire and Rescue Service (VUGD) lists the basement among the places with a raised risk of electric injury, because of conductive floors and metal pipes. A water heater is water and electricity in one casing. The plumber connects the water side, but a separate electrical line or a new socket in a damp basement is an electrician’s job. In the call before the work, you go through what is already at the water heater’s location.

For a summer house that stands empty in winter: Vacation mode

The manufacturer Lochinvar provides its electric water heater with a “Vacation” mode that keeps the tank at about 10 °C: it saves energy and protects the water heater from freezing during a long absence. For a summer house in Jaunķemeri or Vaivari, such a function is one of the criteria when choosing a model, if the power in the house stays on in winter.

In a guest house with several bathrooms: 50–60 °C to the farthest shower

ECDC recommends that in tourist accommodation hot water be kept circulating at 50–60 °C throughout the system, and cold water below 20 °C right up to all the outlets. In a long guest house that means a hot water circulation loop returning to the water heater, so it is planned together with the water heater, not afterwards.

ECDC explains that Legionella also multiplies in pipes with little flow, including in unoccupied rooms. Hot water in the system and a thermostatic mixing valve at the shower together make it possible to meet both conditions.

ECDC guidance for owners of tourist accommodation on Legionella control, checked 19 November 2026.

Worked example: two water heaters in a guest house in Majori

In a guest house in Majori two old water heaters are to be replaced by two new ones, and a thermostatic mixing valve is to be fitted at the shower. Often asked in Jūrmala

Can a new water heater be heated to 60 °C if the hot water smells?

In its instructions Lochinvar mentions that to reduce the smell the tank can be heated to 140 °F (60 °C). Above 120 °F (about 49 °C) the risk of scalding rises, so the manufacturer recommends thermostatic mixing valves at the outlets.

Can a water heater be put on the floor in a basement in Kauguri that floods?

Better not. In flood-prone places BALTA recommends placing water pumps and heating appliances at least 10–20 cm above the floor, and the same principle suits a water heater. A water heater hung on the wall is higher than the floor.

Can an indirect water heater be connected if there is antifreeze in the heating circuit?

Yes, if the heating circuit and the drinking water stay separate: heat passes through the coil and the liquids do not mix. LBN 222-15 point 150 prohibits connecting a drinking water system to a system whose water does not meet drinking water requirements.

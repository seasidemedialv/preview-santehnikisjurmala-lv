When the water heater leaks or does not heat after winter

If the water heater leaks, switch it off at the fuse, close its cold water supply and fill in the form; water heater repair helps from there.

If the water heater does not heat on the very first day after winter, the cause is often switching it on with an empty tank. The manufacturer Lochinvar requires the tank to be filled and the air let out first, otherwise the upper heating element can burn out.

Jobs you can plan

Water heater fitting starts with the load: how many showers run in an hour and how quickly the tank heats up again. For a rented flat or a guest house it is worth working this out before choosing a model.

Water heater cleaning in Jūrmala is tied to the water itself: it is very hard, and limescale builds up in the tank faster than in soft water.

In October, when summer houses in Asari and Melluži are closed up

“Jūrmalas ūdens” (Jūrmala’s water utility) states that the number of residents and guests in Jūrmala changes considerably between summer and winter. In autumn some houses stand empty, and a decision has to be made about the water heater: drain it or leave it running.

Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 (point 36) require the service user to prepare the water supply networks and fittings in their part of the system for winter conditions. A water heater in an unheated house is part of that.

A full but switched-off tank faces another problem. The trade magazine Water Technology notes that any water heater left unused for a long time can start to smell, because odour-causing bacteria multiply in a standing tank. That is one more reason to drain the tank in October rather than leave it full until May. For the plan for the whole house, see the page on draining a summer house for winter.

Worked example: an outdoor shower at a summer house in Vaivari

At a summer house in Vaivari, an outdoor shower with an instantaneous heater is set up against the house wall. The shower needs two new outlets: one for water and one for waste. Wall-mounting a water heater is from €65, an extra water or waste outlet from €35 per point, so €65 + 2 × €35.

In total at least €135 for the labour, not including the heater and materials. The power line to the heater is an electrician’s job, and in October the outdoor shower’s supply is drained together with the rest of the house.

Often asked in Jūrmala

What is the price of water heater fitting in Jūrmala?

The labour price for fitting a water heater in Jūrmala starts from €65 (not including the water heater itself). The total price depends on the capacity of the water heater, where it is fitted and the pipework needed.

What capacity of water heater is recommended?

For one person a 50–80 l water heater is enough. For a family of 2–3 people, 80–120 l. For a larger family or a summer house in active use, 150–200 l. We give an exact recommendation after the inspection.

How long does a water heater last?

An annual service is recommended - replacing the magnesium anode and cleaning the tank, which extends its life and improves energy efficiency.

Can you repair a faulty water heater?

Yes, we repair faulty water heaters - thermostat replacement, heating element replacement, magnesium anode replacement, leak repair. Before the repair we assess whether it makes economic sense compared with buying a new water heater.

Can a water heater in a summer house be left switched on in winter?

Some models allow it: for example, the Lochinvar “Vacation” mode keeps the tank at about 10 °C and protects the water heater from freezing. If there is no such function, or the power in the house may go off, drain the water heater.

Do the sulphur springs affect water heaters in Ķemeri?

No, tap water does not come from the sulphur springs. Ķemeri’s centralised water supply uses the deeper Devonian Gauja aquifer, while hydrogen sulphide forms in the gypsum-bearing Salaspils aquifer under the bogs. In the water of the Ķemeri station, the sample of 22 May 2026 showed hardness of 2.33 mmol/l, the lowest of the four stations.

Why does hot water from a water heater smell of rotten eggs?

Lochinvar explains that the harmless bacteria present in tap water can multiply in a water heater and cause this smell. Sometimes replacing the anode with a special zinc anode helps; more on the page about water heater cleaning.

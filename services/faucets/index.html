Mixer tap repair and replacement in Jūrmala

A leaking mixer tap can seem a trifle, but every day it can waste up to 20 litres of water - and raise your bill. A faulty mechanism, worn inlet valves or simply an out-of-date model - the plumber repairs both older and newer mixer tap models.

If a replacement is needed, we can help you choose a suitable model or fit the mixer tap you have chosen.

  • Repairing leaking mixer taps

    Replacing worn inlet valves, sealing, repairing connections.

  • Replacing mixer taps

    Taking down the old mixer tap and fitting the new one in the kitchen or bathroom. From €25 per job.

  • Shower mixers

    Fitting and repair of thermostatic and ordinary shower mixers.

  • Bath mixer taps

    Fitting bath mixer taps - wall-mounted and freestanding models.

Jobs that cannot wait: a leak under the sink or a smell from the drain

If water leaks under a sink or bath, the fault is often in the trap or its connection to the waste pipe in the wall. For how a trap is replaced and what water seal a sink, bath or shower floor drain needs, read the page New trap under the sink.

If water runs from the tap body or the supply hose and cannot be stopped, close the shut-off valve under the sink or at the incoming supply and fill in the form. When water runs across the floor or to the neighbours, see the emergency plumber page.

In May, before the season at a guest house in Dzintari

After the Second World War many large sanatoria and rest homes were built in Dzintari. “Jūrmalas ūdens” (Jūrmala’s water utility) states that the number of residents and guests in Jūrmala changes considerably between summer and winter. Before the season, taps and showers that have stood all winter have to be made ready for guests.

For tourist accommodation ECDC recommends keeping shower heads and taps free of limescale and, in unoccupied rooms, running the taps and showers before guests arrive; more on the page water heater cleaning.

Limescale forms quickly in Jūrmala. At most stations “Jūrmalas ūdens” gives a total hardness of 3.21–4.25 mmol/l (in Ķemeri and Kauguri it is lower, see the question below), and according to a WHO document water above 1.8 mmol/l is very hard. A tap that started to drip in winter should be repaired or replaced before the season, not left until the first guests.

Worked example: opening the season at a guest house in Dzintari

At a guest house in Dzintari all the taps and water heaters are checked before the season. In one room a tap drips from its body, and the owner replaces it with a new one that the owner bought: tap replacement is from €25 per tap.

The water heater has stood all winter, so it is cleaned and the anode checked: a water heater clean-out with an anode check is from €75 per water heater. In total at least €100. If in a second room the tap can be repaired, the price of stopping a dripping tap depends on the tap model, the spare part and access.

The owner can run the taps of the unoccupied rooms, as ECDC recommends, and no plumber is needed for it. Labour price, materials not included.

Often asked in Jūrmala

How much does mixer tap replacement cost in Jūrmala?

Mixer tap replacement in Jūrmala starts from €25 per job (not including the cost of the mixer tap itself). If the customer supplies the mixer tap, the price is from €25. We give the exact price before the work starts.

Which mixer tap brands do you repair?

We repair and fit mixer taps of various brands, for example Grohe, Hansgrohe, Oras, Ekko, Ideal Standard, Kludi, Ravak.

Can you replace a mixer tap if I already have a new one?

Yes, if you already have a new mixer tap, we will fit it. Make sure it matches the connection size of your pipe system (usually 1/2 inch).

How long does a replacement take?

A standard mixer tap replacement takes 30–60 minutes. If extra pipe adjustment or new connections are needed, it may take somewhat longer.

Can a tap be left dripping in winter so the pipe does not freeze?

Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 (point 51.6) prohibit leaving internal taps before the meter open to prevent freezing without agreement with “Jūrmalas ūdens”. For an empty house the water supply is drained for winter.

How hard is the water in different parts of Jūrmala?

In one sample of 22 May 2026 the total hardness ranged from 2.33 mmol/l in Ķemeri to 4.0 mmol/l in Jaundubulti. For the water of the Kauguri station in 2026 (until 19 August) the average hardness was 2.68 mmol/l.

Does chlorine damage the seals of a new tap in Jūrmala?

“Jūrmalas ūdens” does not add chlorine or fluorides to drinking water. In Jūrmala taps are more often troubled by limescale, because the water is hard.

The sink is set into the worktop or on a cabinet and connected to the water and the waste. If the kitchen is used to cook not only for the family but also for guests, before any rebuilding you need to find out whether Jūrmala’s requirements for greasy waste water apply.

In Bulduri, when a guest house starts serving breakfast

In the part between the Lielupe, Bulduri and Dzintari south of the railway, the buildings are markedly mixed: new and historic detached houses, low-rise buildings and Soviet-era multi-storey blocks. If rooms are let in such a house and breakfast is cooked for guests, the kitchen sink becomes a business matter.

Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 (point 66) require properties that provide catering services or produce food to have separate sewerage for greasy waste water and grease traps. Whether your breakfasts already count as catering, ask “Jūrmalas ūdens” (Jūrmala’s water utility) before choosing the position of the sink.

The answer changes the order of the work. If a trap is needed, the sink’s waste pipe is planned to run to it, not straight to the riser, and room has to be left in the cabinet for servicing.

Jūrmala City Council binding regulations No. 38, point 66 (likumi.lv), checked 19 November 2026.

Hot water, a disposer and Daugavgrīva: assumptions and facts

Often assumed: anything can be poured down a kitchen drain as long as it is liquid.

In fact: Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 (sub-point 40.4) state that the temperature of waste water discharged into the drains must not exceed +40 °C and the pH must be 6.8–8.0. Water from boiling pasta is better left to cool than poured down the drain boiling.

Often assumed: a food waste disposer in a catering kitchen replaces a grease trap.

In fact: point 66 requires a grease trap regardless of what is under the sink. A disposer crushes the scraps, but the fat in the water stays the same.

Often assumed: everything that runs away from Jūrmala kitchens ends up in one place.

In fact: “Jūrmalas ūdens” writes that about 73% of the city’s waste water is treated in Sloka, while about 25% is pumped from the Lielupe system to Riga’s Daugavgrīva treatment plant. The sewerage system is 435.6 km long with 55 pumping stations.

Limescale on a kitchen tap: 3.5–4.0 mmol/l after the membrane replacement

Since 2023 “Jūrmalas ūdens” has been gradually replacing the nanofiltration membranes at all its stations, and the hardness of the treated water in 2024 was 3.5–4.0 mmol/l. By a WHO document water above 1.8 mmol/l is very hard, and additional softening is up to the consumers themselves.

A kitchen tap works harder than any other in the house, especially in a rented kitchen in summer, and limescale builds up first in the aerator at the end of the tap. Choose a tap whose aerator can be unscrewed and cleaned without tools.

If a water softener is planned for the kitchen, it goes before the tap and the dishwasher, and room for it in the cabinet is reserved when the sink is fitted.

Setting a sink into a worktop: the silicone joint and 24 hours

A built-in sink is joined to the worktop with sealant so that water cannot get under the edge. For its sanitary silicone Sikasil-117 the manufacturer Sika states curing of about 3 mm in 24 hours at 23 °C and 50% humidity, skin formation in about 15 minutes, and application at +5…+45 °C.

A thicker joint cures more slowly. So for the first day after setting the sink in, it is better not to load it with heavy pans and not to wash the joint. For a wooden worktop the edges of the cut-out are protected from moisture beforehand, because bare wood absorbs water.

Sika Sikasil-117 Sanitary product data sheet, checked 19 November 2026.

Fitting a food waste disposer in a rented kitchen

The Sloka wastewater treatment plant treats about 2.7 million m³ of waste water a year, and everything ground up in the kitchen ends up there too. A disposer needs a socket in the cabinet, and installing it is an electrician’s job.

In a rented kitchen where guests change every week, it is worth considering whether a disposer is needed at all: a waste bin under the sink and a strainer in the drain need less explaining.

Worked example: two sinks in the kitchen of a guest house in Bulduri

In the kitchen of a guest house in Bulduri two sinks are set in: one for vegetables, the other for dishes. Setting a sink into a kitchen worktop is from €50 per sink, so at least €100 for two, and the second needs an extra waste outlet from €35 per point. In total at least €135.

The grease trap connection is discussed once “Jūrmalas ūdens” has answered whether it is needed. Labour price, materials not included.

Often asked in Jūrmala

Will Jūrmala's water become softer after the membrane replacement?

The company writes that it does not plan to supply medium-hard or soft water. So a water softener in the kitchen is the owner’s own decision.

Is a grease trap needed if guests are served only coffee and rolls?

Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 (point 66) speak of properties that provide catering services or produce food. Where the line falls in your case, ask “Jūrmalas ūdens” before rebuilding the kitchen.

At what temperature can silicone be applied around a sink in an unheated summer house?

The manufacturer of Sika Sikasil-117 states application at +5…+45 °C. In a colder kitchen the joint is put off until the room has warmed up.

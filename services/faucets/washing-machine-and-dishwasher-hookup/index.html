Washing machine hookup - no knocking in the pipes and the right pressure

A machine’s water valve closes in an instant, so in the hookup what matters is not only the supply but also the pressure in the pipes and how they withstand that knock.

In a private house in Sloka connected to the town water supply after 2015

In 2010 only 52% of Sloka’s residents used the centralised water supply. In December 2015 expanded networks with 581 water supply connections were handed over in Kauguri and Sloka. Until then the other houses did not receive town water.

If the old well in the yard still works, the new washing machine has to be connected so that the two waters do not meet. Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 (point 22) require a building with two water sources to have at the joining point an air gap of at least 50 mm or a special non-return valve that provides it.

When switching to town water, the pressure in the pipes can change, and a machine that worked quietly can start knocking in the pipes. That is the moment to go through the connection again.

Jūrmala City Council binding regulations No. 38, point 22 (likumi.lv), checked 19 November 2026.

Clicks and pressure: assumptions and facts

Often assumed: a knock in the pipes when the machine takes in water is a normal sound.

In fact: in its sizing table the manufacturer Sioux Chief lists the solenoid valves of washing machines and dishwashers among the appliances that need water hammer arresting. A knock means the pressure wave is striking the pipes and joints.

Often assumed: the higher the pressure, the faster the machine fills and the better it washes.

In fact: the SYR Drufi+ filter pressure reducer is set at the factory to 4 bar and is adjustable within 1.5–6 bar. The manufacturer states that the inlet pressure must be at least 1 bar higher than the desired outlet pressure, so a reducer only lowers pressure and does not raise it.

SYR Drufi+ DFR operating instructions, checked 19 November 2026.

Water hammer arrester: 1.8 m from the machine and a 65 psi limit

Sioux Chief recommends that on a branch up to 20 ft (about 6 m) the arrester be placed no further than 6 ft (about 1.8 m) from the last appliance. A longer branch needs one arrester in every 20 ft section.

The size depends on the pressure. If the flow pressure exceeds 65 psi (about 4.5 bar), the manufacturer recommends choosing the next larger arrester. So before buying an arrester, measure the pressure at the machine or read the reducer setting.

If the incoming supply has an SYR 315 type reducer, the manufacturer states that at maximum flow the pressure behind it drops by 1.3 bar, but the pressure must be set at static pressure, when no water is running.

Sioux Chief Hydrarester sizing and placement guidelines; SYR 315 data sheet, checked 19 November 2026.

Washing machine hookup in a summer house that is not heated in winter

Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 (point 36) require the user to prepare the water supply networks, fittings and meter assembly in their part of the system for winter conditions. In an unheated summer house the washing machine is part of those fittings: water stays in its inlet valve, pump and hoses.

So the hookup in a summer house is planned with October in mind. The supply valve must be reachable so that it can be closed, and the machine is drained before winter following its instructions.

The Latvian Building Code LBN 222-15 (point 154) requires a water supply to be designed with a slope of at least 0.001 towards the outlet side, and in flat terrain not less than 0.0005. The aim is that the pipe can be drained completely. If a new branch is run for a machine, it is run with this fall, and it drains together with the rest of the water supply.

If the machine stands in a basement, Jūrmala’s requirements for sewer seals are explained on the page flooded basement.

Jūrmala City Council binding regulations No. 38, point 36; LBN 222-15, point 154 (likumi.lv), checked 19 November 2026.

Dishwasher hookup and hardness by Jūrmala station

In one sample of 22 May 2026 the total hardness ranged from 2.33 mmol/l in Ķemeri to 4.0 mmol/l in Jaundubulti, while the 2026 average for the Kauguri station is 2.68 mmol/l. “Jūrmalas ūdens” (Jūrmala’s water utility) writes that, according to a WHO document, water above 1.8 mmol/l is very hard.

So connecting a built-in dishwasher ends with the hardness setting, following the machine’s instructions. If it is left at the factory setting, the salt tank works as for soft water, and limescale builds up.

Connecting a fridge water supply in a house with its own well

LBN 222-15 (point 150) prohibits connecting a drinking water system to a system whose water does not meet drinking water requirements. An ice maker makes ice for drinks, so in a house with two systems it is connected to the town water side.

LBN 222-15, point 150 (likumi.lv), checked 19 November 2026.

Worked example: a washing machine and a reducer in Sloka

In a private house in Sloka the washing machine knocks in the pipes after connection to the town network. It is connected afresh with an arrester at the machine: washing machine hookup is from €30 per appliance.

Flushing the reducer filter and checking the pressure is hourly work from €25/h; if it takes two hours, at least €80 in total. The arrester and the reducer cartridge are materials. Labour price, materials not included.

Often asked in Jūrmala

How often should the filter at a pressure reducer be flushed?

The manufacturer of the SYR Drufi+ states that the filter is to be back-flushed when the flow drops, but at least once every six months, and the reducer setting checked once a year.

Can the inside of an SYR 315 reducer be replaced without changing the pressure?

Yes. The manufacturer states that the cartridge with all the working parts can be replaced without changing the set pressure, and the reducer has a built-in 0.25 mm strainer.

Is a water hammer arrester needed for a dishwasher too?

In the Sioux Chief sizing table, the appliances needing water hammer arresting include the solenoid valves of both washing machines and dishwashers. The arrester size is chosen by pressure, and above 65 psi (about 4.5 bar) the next larger one is taken.

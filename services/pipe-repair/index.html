Why pipes wear out faster in Jūrmala

Jūrmala’s climate - the nearness of the sea, damp and salty air - speeds up pipe corrosion and wear. Old Jūrmala villas often have metal pipes that are many years old and may start leaking at any moment.

  • Pressure problems

When a pipe is leaking: jobs that cannot wait

Water leak: a leak at a joint, a burst flexible hose or a yard service pipe leaking into the sand. First the water is stopped with the shut-off valve, then the place is looked for.

Frozen pipes: in winter the incoming supply or a section in an unheated room freezes, and after the thaw a split is found.

Pipe jobs you can plan in a flat and a house

Riser replacement in a flat: a cold, hot or soil section between floors in the multi-storey blocks of Dubulti, Kauguri and Buļļuciems.

Replacing pipes in a private house: an old water pipe in a timber villa or summer house, where fire safety regulations apply and, for some buildings, heritage requirements.

In Ķemeri, where the water main began in 1929, but sewerage for many houses only in 2015

Jūrmala’s centralised water supply began in Ķemeri in 1929, when two artesian wells were drilled and a water main and a water tower were built. As late as 2010, 77% of Ķemeri residents used the centralised water supply, but only 36% the centralised sewerage.

In November 2015 the phase III networks in Ķemeri were handed over, with 171 water and 429 sewer connections. Many houses joined the town sewerage only then, and until that time the waste water went to their own pit or tank.

Point 15 of Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 requires that after connecting, the user’s own structures that collected waste water be removed. The old pipe leading to them then becomes redundant: it is disconnected and sealed off, so that the house is not left with a dead end holding standing water.

Installing pipes and the water manifold

Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 (point 11) state that the user builds the service pipe and the outlet up to the connection point in accordance with the technical conditions of Jūrmalas ūdens (Jūrmala’s water utility). The meter is then installed by the company.

Inside a house the pipes begin after the meter. With a manifold every water point has its own pipe and its own valve, so one bathroom or kitchen can be shut off without closing the whole house.

Replacing the shut-off valve at the incoming supply

Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 (point 36) oblige the user to prepare, in their part of the service zone, the water pipe networks, fittings and meter point for winter conditions.

The shut-off valve at the incoming supply is exactly the fitting used to shut off and drain the meter point. A valve that has not been turned for years often does not close fully or starts to leak along the stem. So it is worth checking the valve in autumn and replacing it before the frosts begin.

Checked 19 November 2026: Jūrmala City Council binding regulations No. 38 of 25 July 2024, points 11, 15 and 36.

Pipes that rumble or bang when a tap closes

The manufacturer Sioux Chief advises fitting a water hammer arrester on a branch of up to about 6 m no further than about 1.8 m from the last appliance. On longer branches it advises one in every section of about 6 m. Among the appliances that need arresting, it names the solenoid valves of washing machines and dishwashers.

In its sizing table Sioux Chief also states that if the flow pressure exceeds 65 psi (about 4.5 bar), the next larger arrester should be chosen. So before choosing an arrester, the pressure is measured at the tap.

If the banging comes after the washing machine stops taking water, the fault is usually the sudden closing of the valve, not the pipe. Fitting an arrester or tightening a loose fixing cures it.

Pressure testing the pipes after the work

The Latvian Building Code LBN 222-15 (point 161) states that the test pressure of a water main before it is put into service is given in the construction design. The standard LVS EN 805:2022 may be used for the tests.

Inside a house, LBN 221-15 requires pipes and fittings to be designed for a working pressure of not less than 600 kPa, or 6 bar. The test pressure and duration are therefore taken from the design or the standard, not judged by eye.

Checked 19 November 2026: Cabinet Regulation No. 326 (LBN 222-15), point 161; Cabinet Regulation No. 332 (LBN 221-15).

Often asked in Jūrmala

What is the price of pipe repair in Jūrmala?

Pipe repair in Jūrmala starts from €35 for a simpler leak fix. Pipe replacement depends on the type of pipe, the length and the complexity. We give the exact price after an inspection.

How long does pipe replacement take?

A typical pipe repair takes 1–3 hours. A complete replacement of the pipe system in a flat may take 1–2 days. We confirm the time before starting the work, so that you can plan it conveniently.

What pipes do you use for replacement?

PEX, polypropylene and stainless steel pipes are used.

Can you repair a pipe under the floor or in a wall?

Yes, we work with hidden pipe systems.

How hard is the water in Jūrmala and what does it mean for pipes?

The total hardness is 3.21–4.25 mmol/l, and according to the WHO document water above 1.8 mmol/l is considered very hard. The company does not plan to supply softer water, so limescale builds up in hot water pipes and fittings.

What must be submitted to Jūrmalas ūdens after a new service pipe is built?

Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 (point 13) require an as-built survey plan and a CCTV survey for gravity networks of 200 mm diameter and above. They also require a statement that the user’s own well is not connected to the centralised system, and a statement that rainwater and drainage water are not discharged into the sewer.

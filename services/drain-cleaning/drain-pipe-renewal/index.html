In a villa in Dzintari that has no drawing of the outlet

Dzintari developed as a district of detached houses for well-off summer visitors, and the first plots were entered in the land register in 1871. In many such houses the drain outlet is older than any surviving drawing.

Points 32 and 33 of Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 require the user to keep their own diagram of the water supply and drains and as-built drawings showing pipe diameters, depth, fittings and meter points. If there are none, inventory drawings must be prepared.

Where new networks were built in a street in Dzintari, the sewer connection is usually an inspection chamber cover opposite the property. The new outlet runs from the house to this chamber. While the trench is open is the easiest moment to note the diameter, the length, the rodding eyes and the place of the non-return valve.

Two common assumptions about a new outlet

Often thought: a non-return valve and airtight rodding eyes are needed only in large houses.

In fact: point 53 of regulations No. 38 states that the user answers for the possible consequences if their system lacks shut-off fittings, the rodding eyes are not tightly closed or there are no non-return valves.

Often thought: in Babīte and Piņķi the same Jūrmala regulations apply at the outlet connection.

In fact: Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 apply to Jūrmala. In Babīte and Piņķi the water supply and sewerage are provided by SIA “Mārupes komunālie pakalpojumi”, and you ask about requirements there.

Checked 19 November 2026: Jūrmala City Council binding regulations No. 38 of 25 July 2024, points 32, 33 and 53.

EN 13564: which non-return valve suits grey water and which black water

Anti-flood valves have the standard EN 13564. Grey waste water comes from sinks, showers and the washing machine, black from the toilet.

The manufacturer ACO states that the Quatrix is a type 3 device for grey and black waste water, while the Junior (type 5) and the Triplex (type 2) suit grey waste water only. If there is a toilet behind the valve, you need a valve that is also intended for black waste water.

Where a basement has sinks, toilets, showers, floor drains or drainage, sub-point 34.4 of Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 requires automatic shut-offs or gate valves to be fitted in the sewer. So for a villa basement the valve type is chosen by what is connected there.

A valve needs access for cleaning, so it is placed where it can be opened, not under tiles or concrete without a hatch.

Checked 19 November 2026: Jūrmala City Council binding regulations No. 38 of 25 July 2024, sub-point 34.4.

A drain pipe is leaking: report a leak in a ditch too

“Jūrmalas ūdens” (Jūrmala’s water utility) asks you to report any sewage leak to its emergency service, even in a ditch. If the street network or a chamber before the property boundary is leaking, that is the company’s work.

If a pipe in the house or yard is leaking, do not use appliances that drain water into it, and tell the neighbour below. Then it is established whether one joint is leaking or the whole old line has to be replaced.

A calculation for the yard outlet of a villa in Dzintari

In a villa in Dzintari, 6 m of yard outlet is replaced from the house wall to the rodding eye before the street chamber, and the remaining old line is flushed. Replacing waste pipe, 6 m at €25/m, and flushing the drains with water pressure from €60: in total at least €210, labour price, materials not included.

A non-return valve to EN 13564 and a rodding eye are materials, and you go through their price on the call before the work. If the section lies under paving or a terrace, access also affects the price.

Often asked in Jūrmala

The chamber opposite the property is overflowing. Is that a plumber's job?

Usually not. The responsibility of “Jūrmalas ūdens” for the sewerage ends at the last chamber before the property boundary, so it is reported to the company’s emergency service. If the chamber is empty but the water will not drain from the house, the blockage is in your line.

Can only the part of the riser in my flat be replaced?

Yes, replacing a soil stack on one floor is a separate job from €120 per floor. Speak to the building manager about the riser first: it usually belongs to all the flat owners together, unless the building’s documents say otherwise.

What non-return valve suits if there is a toilet in the basement?

You need a valve that, under EN 13564, is also intended for black waste water, for example the ACO Quatrix (type 3). The manufacturer intends the ACO Junior and Triplex for grey waste water only.

Drain flushing - twice a year and before a CCTV survey

Drain flushing in Jūrmala is one way to meet point 34.5 of binding regulations No. 38: within your service zone, drains must be cleaned preventively at least twice a year.

Pressure flushing costs from €60, and for a riser from €80 per riser, labour price, materials not included.

Before selling a villa in Lielupe, when the outlet has no documents

In Lielupe, around Meža prospekts and Bulduru prospekts, there are pine-park development areas where only large detached houses on large plots are allowed. On a large plot the outlet from the house to the street network may be long, and cleaning it through a single inspection chamber is harder than a short line.

Point 57 of Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 states that if, during a reconstruction or a change of owner, there is no as-built documentation for the drain outlet, the owner must have a CCTV pipe survey made at their own expense. It must be submitted to “Jūrmalas ūdens” (Jūrmala’s water utility).

The pipe must be clean for the camera. If the walls are coated with deposits or a layer of grease, the recording shows only those, not the joints and the fall. So the line is flushed before the survey, and the timing is agreed with whoever will carry out the survey.

Wipes, cleaning products and twice a year: assumptions

Often thought: wet wipes labelled “flushable” dissolve in the pipe.

In fact: at the Sloka waste water treatment works, 14 tonnes of rubbish are screened out in a year from about 2.7 million m³ of waste water. An article in LV portāls about Jūrmala’s sewerage states that even “flushable” wipes belong in the rubbish bin. In a long yard line they usually catch on bends and at joints.

Often thought: before flushing, the line can be “thawed” with a strong acid cleaner.

In fact: regulations No. 38 (sub-point 45.4) prohibit discharging into the sewer acids and substances that may release gases dangerous to people, including hydrogen sulphide. In flushing, a water jet does the work, not chemicals.

Often thought: cleaning twice a year is required only of blocks of flats.

In fact: sub-point 34.5 speaks about the service user in their service zone, not only about blocks of flats. How it applies to a particular house, ask “Jūrmalas ūdens”, as a rule.

Checked 19 November 2026: Jūrmala City Council binding regulations No. 38 of 25 July 2024, points 15, 34.5, 40.4, 45.4, 53 and 57.

The rodding eye after flushing: point 53 and an airtight cover

A long yard line is flushed through a rodding eye or an inspection chamber. Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 (point 53) state that if the user’s system lacks shut-off fittings, the rodding eyes are not tightly closed or non-return valves are not fitted, the user answers for the possible consequences.

So after flushing, the cover of the rodding eye must be closed airtight. A damaged cover or its seal is worth replacing before the line is used again.

If the camera shows a crack, a sunken section or a displaced joint, flushing does not repair it. The next step is then drain pipe replacement in that section.

Flushing in wet sand instead of digging

In Melluži and Asari the town sewer is in places laid at a depth of 5–6 m by the pumping stations, and a high groundwater level hampered the construction. A house’s yard line lies shallower, but digging it up in wet sandy ground is also a big job.

So the yard line is flushed first, not dug. Hydrodynamic drain cleaning works with a water jet inside the pipe, and the ground above it stays untouched.

A calculation: three soil stacks in a block of flats in Dubulti, twice a year

In Dubulti, on the river side of Dubultu prospekts, there is a microdistrict of blocks of flats with buildings around shared yards. Suppose the building has three soil stacks and the building manager meets the requirement of sub-point 34.5 by flushing them twice a year.

Pressure flushing a riser is from €80 per riser, so 3 × €80 = at least €240 at a time and at least €480 a year. The risers are flushed separately from the yard line; labour price, materials not included.

Often asked in Jūrmala

May the flushing water be hotter than 40 °C?

In Jūrmala the temperature of waste water discharged into the sewer must not exceed +40 °C, and the pH must be 6.8–8.0 (sub-point 40.4 of regulations No. 38). The flushing water also ends up in the sewer, so the same limit applies to it.

Can the old holding tank stay in the line after connecting to the town sewerage?

No. Point 15 of regulations No. 38 requires that after connecting to the centralised sewerage, the user’s own decentralised sewerage structures used for collecting waste water be removed.

What non-return valve suits a house whose line is flushed through the basement?

Point 53 of regulations No. 38 lists the lack of non-return valves among the things whose consequences the user answers for. For anti-flood valves the standard is EN 13564, and the type must be chosen by whether toilet waste water also passes through the valve.

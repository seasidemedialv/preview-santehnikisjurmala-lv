Heating system repair and installation in Jūrmala - from a basement boiler to a summer house circuit

Heating system repair in Jūrmala and the Babīte area covers radiators, thermostatic heads, water underfloor heating, heated towel rails and the pipes between them, in a block of flats or in a summer house with its own boiler.

Changing one radiator costs from €70 per radiator, labour price, materials not included. We contact you within 5 minutes of your request.

Urgent jobs: a leak, or a room that stays cold

Radiator repair is needed when a radiator leaks or does not warm up. The fault is often in the thermostatic head: on an Oventrop head the snowflake position opens the valve only if the room cools below 6 °C. The price depends on the fault, the type of system and access.

Underfloor heating repair is urgent when the manifold leaks or one loop no longer warms. For its own system, Myson describes the loop test as 3 bar for 30 minutes, and it shows whether the pipe under the screed is sound before the floor is opened.

Planned jobs: replacement, flushing, a new floor

Radiator replacement costs from €70 per radiator. In a timber summer house, fire safety matters too: for work with a flame or sparks, the area within a 5 m radius must be cleared of combustible materials or those materials must be protected.

Heating system flushing suits a circuit where the same water or antifreeze has circulated for years. The price depends on the number of radiators, the type of boiler and the amount of dirt.

Laying underfloor heating loops is priced per m², and the area, the manifold and the floor construction set the price. From pouring the screed to full heating takes almost a month.

Changing a heated towel rail in the bathroom costs from €80 per towel rail. For a combined towel rail, Terma requires that the electric element’s output does not exceed 60% of the towel rail’s output.

29 July 2024: flooded streets and yards in Kauguri and Sloka

In the storm of 29 July 2024, 112 had received about 350 calls from Jūrmala and other places around Riga by midday. Streets and yards were flooded, especially in Kauguri and Sloka.

BALTA states that floods damage basements and heating equipment most, and advises placing water pumps and heating equipment at least 10–20 cm above the floor in flood-prone places. The protected summer-house districts from Dubulti to the Lielupe and from Vaivari to Jaundubulti are also at risk of flooding.

The State Fire and Rescue Service (VUGD) lists the basement among the places with a higher risk of electric shock, because of conductive floors and metal pipes. If there was water in the room with the heating pump, do not switch the equipment on until an electrician has checked the connection. After that, a plumber can move the circulation pump, the manifold or the expansion vessel above the floor.

Heating in a historic villa: Article 21 and the Cultural Space Centre

Article 21 of the law “On Protection of Cultural Monuments” says that cultural monuments of national and regional importance may be repaired only with the written permission of the National Heritage Board. Monuments of local importance need the municipality’s permission.

Jūrmala’s territorial plan data (around 2010) listed 558 architectural monuments and 11 urban planning monument areas, and most of the protected buildings are timber dwellings. In a local-importance area, repair and rebuilding of a historic building that may alter it may be carried out only with the written permission of the Jūrmala Cultural Space and Environmental Design Centre.

The same conditions ask for sudden swings in humidity and temperature to be avoided in historic interiors. That is why new heating in such a house is brought up gradually, not straight to full output. Ask the centre before the work whether your job needs a permit.

Topping up the circuit from the water supply: LBN 222-15 and hard water

If the pressure in the heating system drops, the circuit is topped up from the water supply. Point 150 of LBN 222-15 prohibits joining a drinking-water system to a system whose water does not meet drinking-water requirements. A heating circuit with antifreeze is exactly that: the manufacturer of Sentinel X500 prohibits it in drinking-water systems. So the filling connection is closed after topping up.

Every top-up brings new water into the circuit. “Jūrmalas ūdens” (Jūrmala’s water utility) gives total hardness of 3.21–4.25 mmol/l and calcium of 85–98 mg/l, and according to the WHO document, water above 1.8 mmol/l is very hard. If the circuit has to be topped up every week, water is going somewhere, and it is worth finding the spot rather than carrying on adding more.

Worked example: a Vaivari summer house circuit in May

The Vaivari summer-house blocks fall within urban planning monuments. Suppose that in May the heating circuit in such a house is refilled and bled after the winter, and two old radiators must be replaced with new ones.

Changing one radiator is from €70 per radiator, so at least €140 for both. The price of bleeding depends on the fault, the type of system and access, and you go through it on the phone before any work starts. Labour price, materials not included.

Often asked in Jūrmala

How high in a basement should a heating pump be raised if the room can flood?

BALTA advises placing water pumps and heating equipment at least 10–20 cm above the floor in flood-prone places. In Jūrmala, several protected summer-house districts are also at risk of flooding.

What antifreeze concentration does the circuit of an unheated summer house need?

It depends on the product. For example, the manufacturer of Sentinel X500 requires a dose of at least 20%: at 20% the freezing point is about -6 °C, at 50% about -29 °C. This product must not be used in drinking-water systems.

Is water hardness the same across Jūrmala?

No. For water from the Kauguri water treatment station in 2026 (until 19 August), the average hardness was 2.68 mmol/l. That is also above 1.8 mmol/l, which “Jūrmalas ūdens”, following the WHO document, calls very hard water.

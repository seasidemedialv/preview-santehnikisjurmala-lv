In a Dzintari villa after patching the screed: heat without jumps

Dzintari developed as a prestigious detached-house district for wealthy summer residents, and after 1920 mainly standard-design family houses with classical forms and national romantic details were built here. “Dzintaru - Bulduru vasarnīcu kvartāli” (the Dzintari - Bulduri summer-house blocks) is a separate urban planning monument (No. 6084).

Jūrmala’s conditions for historic interiors ask for sudden swings in humidity and temperature to be avoided. If in such a house the leak in underfloor heating is chiselled out and the screed is poured again, the floor is not switched straight on to full output.

For its own system, Myson requires the screed to be left to cure for at least 21 days and heating to start from 30 °C, raising it by 3 °C each day. A patched piece of screed is protected like freshly poured screed, and the room temperature changes gradually.

Pump position and a covered sensor: assumptions and facts

Often assumed: the new mixing unit is better placed right on the floor, closer to the pipes.

In fact: BALTA states that floods damage basements and heating equipment most, and advises placing water pumps and heating equipment at least 10–20 cm above the floor in flood-prone places. If the unit is being changed, it is worth raising it.

Often assumed: if underfloor heating does not warm, the fault is surely in the pipe under the screed.

In fact: about its own thermostatic heads, Oventrop states that the thermostat or its remote sensor must not be covered by curtains or furniture. A covered sensor feels warmer air and closes the loop earlier than it should.

Pressure test loop by loop: 3 bar for 30 minutes

The Myson Floortec instructions describe the test of an underfloor heating loop as 3 bar for 30 minutes. In repair, the same principle is used to tell a pipe leak from a fault in the manifold or a valve.

The loop is shut off at the manifold and tested separately from the others. If the pressure holds for 30 minutes, the pipe under the screed is sound, and the fault is looked for in the manifold valve, the actuator or the balancing. If the pressure falls, the leak is traced before the floor is opened.

The test pressure and time for each system are set by its manufacturer. If you have the underfloor heating documents, show them to the plumber.

Underfloor heating not warming: thermostat, manifold or mixing unit

On an Oventrop head, position 0 closes the valve, the snowflake position opens it only if the room cools below 6 °C, and the scale 1–5 corresponds to about 12, 16, 20, 24 and 28 °C. A head left on the snowflake heats only against frost in winter, and the floor seems faulty although it is not.

Replacing the underfloor heating mixing unit is a bigger job, and it is assessed on the phone by the type of unit. In a basement or plant room that can flood, the new unit with its pump is placed higher than the floor.

Underfloor heating manifold repair and balancing in Sloka: a calculation

Sloka is mostly dense detached-house development. Suppose that in such a house one loop stays cold, but the 3 bar test holds for 30 minutes. The fault is in the manifold: a valve actuator must be replaced and the flow balanced.

If that takes three hours, hourly work from €25/h gives at least €75 for the job; the actuator comes on top. If the test shows a leak under the screed, the price of tracing a hidden leak depends on the leak’s location, access and the test method. Labour price, materials not included.

Often asked in Jūrmala

May the underfloor heating pump be switched on straight after a flood?

No. VUGD regards the basement as a place with a higher risk of electric shock, because of conductive floors and metal pipes. A flooded pump and its electrical connection are checked by an electrician before switching on.

Does a basement plant room with a floor drain need a non-return valve?

Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 (point 34.4) require automatic shut-offs or gate valves to be fitted in the drains if there are sanitary appliances in the basement, including floor drains, or drainage.

Can the thermostat on a manifold be locked so tenants do not change it?

If it is an Oventrop thermostatic head, the manufacturer states that the setting can be limited or locked with limiter pins. For other models, see their instructions.

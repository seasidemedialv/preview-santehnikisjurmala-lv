Drainage and basement pumps in Jūrmala each work within their manufacturer’s limits.

A drainage pump in a chamber pumps clean groundwater up to +40 °C, while a lift unit behind a basement toilet raises waste water through a rising main with a loop above street level. We repair both, connect basement appliances, and on the phone you go through the job and the price by pump model.

Pedrollo TOP and Saniflo SANICUBIC: what the manufacturer states

Equipment Manufacturer’s limit or requirement
Pedrollo TOP drainage pump For clean water without abrasive particles, liquid up to +40 °C, solid particles up to 10 mm
Pedrollo TOP 1-3 Pumps water out down to 14 mm above the floor; float switch included
Saniflo SANICUBIC rising main A loop above the maximum backflow level, at street level unless local requirements say otherwise
Saniflo SANICUBIC venting Outlet at roof level, open in both directions, without air admittance valves
Saniflo servicing A visual check once a month, an inspection by a qualified specialist once a year

If you do not know your pump’s model, photograph the label on the housing and enter the name in the form. From it the plumber sees which limits apply to your pump.

If water is already standing in the basement, start with the page Flooded basement; if the pump in the chamber has stopped, see Drainage pump repair, and if it is a unit behind a toilet, Sewage lift pump repair.

In Sloka by the Lielupe, where groundwater is 0.5–2.0 m deep

Sloka lies on the left bank of the Lielupe, about 2 km from the Gulf of Riga. Jūrmala’s territorial plan says that in river and lake floodplains and in the dips between dunes a high groundwater level is typical, 0.5–2.0 m.

A basement whose floor is below this level stands in groundwater, and the drainage pump in the chamber switches on every time the level rises. That is a job for clean water, the kind Pedrollo TOP is meant for. If the same basement has a toilet or a shower, their waste water is a different job and a different unit.

When the Kauguri and Sloka networks were opened, a representative of “Jūrmalas ūdens” said that cesspits are not the safest solution for the city, especially by the river, where groundwater is high. So basement drains lead to the central sewerage, and, if they are lower than the street, through a lift unit.

In December 2015, the extended networks in Kauguri and Sloka were handed over, with 581 water supply connections and 531 sewer branches. For houses on these streets, basement drains can lead to the street network, not to a pit in the yard.

Planned jobs: a basement sink, a laundry corner or a new rising main

Planned jobs are replacing a drainage pump with a new rising main, connecting appliances to an existing lift unit and fitting shut-offs in the basement drains. They are done in dry weather, when work in the chamber and the basement is not rushed.

Saniflo states that the connected appliances must not be used during a power cut. If a sink or washing machine is connected to the unit in the basement, that means two more appliances that cannot be used without power, and that is worth knowing before the work.

Saniflo requires a visual check of the unit once a month, so someone must be at it every month. In a summer house where nobody lives in winter, the unit is therefore drained in autumn or prepared with antifreeze, as the manufacturer requires; see the page draining a summer house’s water system for winter.

Calculation: a sink and a washing machine in a Sloka basement

In the basement of a Sloka detached house, a sink and a washing machine are connected to the existing lift unit. A basin fixed to the wall and connected is from €50, hooking up the washing machine from €30, in total at least €80, labour price, materials not included.

For these jobs you do not pay for an inspection separately: an on-site inspection from €40 is the price when the plumber comes and looks and no other work is done. The plumber assesses on site whether the unit has enough inlets for both appliances.

Points 53 and 59: a basement with drains under Jūrmala's regulations

Point 53 of Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 says that the user is responsible for possible consequences if their system lacks shut-off fittings, the rodding eyes are not tightly closed or non-return valves are not fitted. A basement sink or floor drain without a shut-off is exactly such a case.

Point 59 of the regulations prohibits storing products and valuables in basements where sanitary appliances and drainage systems are fitted. After a laundry corner or a shower is installed, the basement is no longer a storeroom.

In an old house, a venting outlet at roof level means a pipe through the storeys, and it is worth looking at this route before the unit is put in the basement.

Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38, points 53 and 59 (likumi.lv, checked on 20 November 2026). In a block of flats, what counts as common property is usually set by the building’s documents.

Often asked in Jūrmala

Can a basement toilet in Melluži manage without a lift unit?

It depends on how deep the street sewer lies opposite the house. In Melluži and Asari it is laid in places 5–6 m deep by the pumping stations, elsewhere shallower; the connection point and depth are shown by the technical conditions and the house’s as-built drawings.

How long must a pump's cable be if the pump works outdoors?

The Pedrollo data sheet states that the standard EN 60335-2-41 requires a 10 m supply cable for outdoor use. This lets the cable joint be kept outside the chamber.

Do Jūrmala's regulations apply to a basement in Babīte and Piņķi?

No. Babīte and Piņķi are in Mārupe Municipality, where water and sewerage are provided by “Mārupes komunālie pakalpojumi”, and Mārupe Municipality binding regulations apply there. Ask that company about the requirements for basement drains.

Sewage pump repair in Jūrmala often ends not at the pump itself, but at the rising main and the venting.

Saniflo requires the rising main to be taken up in a loop above street level and the unit to be vented to the roof without an air admittance valve. The price is set by the pump type, the fault and cleaning, and the plumber names it after the inspection.

In summer in Asari by the Skautu iela pumping station, when the house has guests

In phase IV in Asari, 6.5 km of main sewer pipeline and a sewage pumping station on Skautu iela were built. “Jūrmalas ūdens” states that the system’s load changes with the number of residents and guests in summer and in winter.

In a house the same thing happens on a smaller scale: in summer, when there are guests, the basement toilet and shower work several times an hour, and the lifting unit switches on more often than in winter. That is when the weak spots appear that could not be heard in winter.

Saniflo requires the rising main to be taken up in a loop above the maximum backflow level, which, unless local requirements say otherwise, is street level. On 29 July 2024 the streets in Asari were like rivers, and on such a day a rising main without a loop lets waste water flow back.

Venting and servicing: two assumptions

Often assumed: an air valve next to the toilet is enough to vent the unit.

In fact: the Saniflo SANICUBIC instructions require venting with an outlet at roof level, open in both directions; air admittance valves (mechanical valves) are not permitted. If the unit smells or empties slowly, first check whether the venting has been replaced by such a valve.

Often assumed: the unit is looked at when it stops.

In fact: Saniflo advises a visual check of the lifting unit once a month and an inspection by a qualified specialist once a year. For the monthly check it is enough to see whether there is damp around the unit and whether it switches off after discharging.

Shut-off valves on the inlets and the rising main

Saniflo requires shut-off valves to be fitted on the lifting unit’s inlets and rising main so that servicing can be done safely, and the room must be ventilated and protected from frost. Without valves the unit cannot be opened without draining the whole rising main back into the tank.

The order of work is this: the valves are closed, the unit is disconnected from the power, and only then is the cover opened. If there are no valves, they are usually fitted during the repair, because the next servicing without them will be just as dirty.

Saniflo SANICUBIC instructions; Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38, points 34.4, 40.4 and 53. Checked on 20 November 2026.

A sewage pump without power: why the connected appliances are not used

Saniflo states that the connected appliances must not be used during a power cut. The unit then does not pump out the waste water, and every toilet flush or shower raises the level in the tank until it can overflow onto the basement floor.

So when the power goes, the basement toilet, shower and sink are not used until the power is back and the unit empties the tank by itself. If afterwards the unit hums but the tank does not empty, or it switches on for no reason, describe it in the form; the plumber looks for the reason on site.

In a house with several appliances in the basement, it is worth knowing which of them go through the unit. Appliances above street level that drain by gravity are not affected by a power cut.

Calculation: a basement toilet in an Asari house with a new rising main

In an Asari house the rising main of a basement toilet has no loop. It is relaid with a loop above street level: 4 m of waste pipe at €25/m is at least €100. After the inspection of the unit, the toilet is put back (from €45), in total at least €145, labour price, materials not included.

The plumber assesses the repair of the unit itself by the pump type, the fault and whether the tank must be cleaned before the work. You go through the price of both jobs on the phone before the work starts. If the whole unit has to be replaced, the plumber names the price of replacing the sewage pump after the inspection.

Often asked in Jūrmala

What do Jūrmala's regulations require for a basement toilet with a lifting unit?

Point 34.4 of binding regulations No. 38 requires automatic shut-offs or gate valves to be fitted in the drains if the basement has sinks, toilets, showers, floor drains or drainage. Under point 53, the user is responsible for the consequences if non-return valves are missing.

How hot may the water be that a unit pumps into the street network?

The temperature of waste water discharged into Jūrmala’s drains must not exceed +40 °C, and the pH must be 6.8–8.0 (binding regulations No. 38, point 40.4). For the unit’s own temperature limit, see the instructions of the particular model.

How high must the rising main loop be raised?

Above the maximum backflow level. Saniflo takes this to be street level, unless local requirements say otherwise.

In an Asari fishing-village house, where a summer house has become a home

Asari is one of Jūrmala’s old fishing villages, with irregular plots and modest buildings that combine a family dwelling with a summer house. In neighbouring Melluži live a considerable share of Jūrmala’s permanent residents.

When such a house becomes a year-round home, the bathroom has to work in January too, and in a narrow room a shower is often put where the bath was.

In an old summer house the floor is often timber, and under it there may be an unheated crawl space. In summer that did not matter, because the water was drained for winter. Now the shower and its drain will work over a cold crawl space all winter.

Winter and hot water: what people take for granted about a shower

Often assumed: if the pipes under the floor of a summer house have not frozen so far, they will not freeze when the house is lived in during winter either.

In fact: so far they were protected by the water being drained for winter. Point 36 of Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 obliges the user to prepare their part of the water supply networks and fittings for winter operation, and in a home this means pipes that stay full over winter.

Often assumed: a shower in itself uses less hot water than a bath.

In fact: Hamworthy states that push-button showers, which switch off after 20–30 seconds, use less hot water, because ordinary showers are often left running. The saving depends on how the shower is used.

A drain under a timber floor: LBN 221-15 point 255 and a cold crawl space

Point 255 of LBN 221-15 requires sewerage pipes to be laid so that they do not freeze. If the shower drain runs under a timber floor through an unheated crawl space, this requirement applies to it directly.

The City of Toronto advises insulating exposed pipes in basements, crawl spaces, garages and attics with foam sleeves. In a crawl space the new drain needs insulation or another route that stays in the warm part of the house.

The same applies to the water supply to the new shower tap. If it comes from the basement through the crawl space, in a summer house it was safe only because it was empty in winter.

A shower in a timber floor in place of a bath: between the joists or on a step

If the shower is left where the bath was, the drain is usually already close to the house’s outlet. If it is moved to another corner, the pipe gets longer, and its fall is calculated from the pipe diameter (more on the new supply and waste points page).

In a timber floor that means a choice: either the pipe runs between the joists and there is room for the fall, or the tray is raised on a step. If a pipe between the joists runs over an unheated crawl space, point 255 of LBN 221-15 on not freezing applies to this section as well.

Worked total for an Asari house: shower in the opposite corner with 3 m of new drain

In an Asari house the bath is taken out, and the shower with a tray goes into the opposite corner by the outer wall. In place of the old drain, 3 m of new pipe has to go under the floor, running through the crawl space.

Replacing a waste pipe is from €25/m, so for 3 m of pipe at least €75. The price of the shower area where the old bath stood depends on taking out the old bath, the type of shower and moving the drain, and is given after an inspection. The tray, trap, pipes and insulation sleeves are extra.

Often asked in Jūrmala

Does rebuilding a summer house bathroom in Asari need permission?

Asari falls within the urban planning monuments of national and local importance for summer house districts. In such an area, the repair and rebuilding of a historic building that may alter the building may be carried out only with the written permission of the Jūrmala Cultural Space and Environmental Design Centre. An indoor shower in place of a bath does not usually alter a building; if in doubt, ask the centre.

May a timber floor joist be notched for the shower drain?

That is a question of the house’s structure, not of plumbing. Notching a joist is decided together with a carpenter or a structural engineer, and if it cannot be done, the pipe is taken between the joists or the tray is raised on a step.

Can a shower be fitted without a tray, with a floor drain in a timber floor?

It can, if a floor drain with the necessary fall and waterproofing can be laid into the floor. In a timber floor in an old house this is a bigger job than a tray, because the floor level has to change, and the waterproofing is planned with the finishing tradesperson.

Shower cubicle repair - seals, trap and shower head

Shower cubicle repair in Jūrmala is usually replacing seals, door rollers, the tray trap or the shower head, not replacing the whole cubicle.

In spring in a Vaivari summer house, when the cubicle is opened after winter

The part of Vaivari from Vēju iela to Kaugurciems is characterised by wide areas of forest, large plots and only a few large complexes. The Vaivari summer house quarters belong to the summer house district urban planning monument.

In a house that has stood empty over winter, the cubicle is opened in spring after several months. After a long absence the Health Inspectorate advises running both the cold and the hot water: open the tap slightly at first, so that no aerosol forms, and within ten minutes increase the flow to full.

It is at this moment that you often see the door seals have dried out, there is no water in the trap and the shower head is full of limescale.

If the cubicle is used only in summer, the water in the trap evaporates over winter, and a sewer smell appears in the bathroom. This is fixed by simply pouring water into the trap, and it is not a repair.

The head, running the water and the leak: what is not true about a cubicle

Often assumed: the shower head is only a matter of comfort and does not need cleaning.

In fact: in its 15-point plan for tourist accommodation, ECDC includes keeping shower heads and taps clean of limescale. A head full of limescale is a place where water stands.

Often assumed: if nobody uses the cubicle, nothing happens to it.

In fact: ECDC advises running all the taps and showers for a few minutes in unoccupied rooms at least once a week and before each guest arrives. The same applies to a summer house or a second bathroom.

Seals, limescale and 20–50 °C: where water stands in a cubicle

ECDC explains that legionella bacteria multiply in standing water at 20–50 °C, including in pipes with low flow, limescale and rubber seals. In a shower cubicle such places are the shower hose, the head and the seals of the thermostatic cartridge.

So in a repair the seals and the hose are replaced, not just cleaned, if they are cracked or black on the inside. Door seals and rollers are replaced to the same profile, which can be read off the old part or from the cubicle manufacturer’s marking.

A thermostatic cartridge in the cubicle panel is replaced with one of the same manufacturer and size. If the cartridge is no longer available, the whole mixer unit is replaced.

If the cubicle leaks from under the tray, the fault is often in the trap connection or in the joint at the tray edge, and the cubicle does not have to be replaced.

Water gets from the cubicle to the floor below: the room valve or the meter assembly

In an emergency, “Jūrmalas ūdens” advises a private house owner to shut off the water to the particular room or at the meter assembly for the whole building. In a flat that is the valve on the bathroom or on the flat’s incoming supply, by the meters.

If water from the cubicle is already reaching the neighbour below, close the valve, do not use the shower and tell the neighbour below. If there is electricity in the cubicle, for example lighting or a radio, switch off its fuse too.

If the valve cannot be closed, “Jūrmalas ūdens” advises calling the company’s emergency service. Once the water has been stopped, fill in the form, and we contact you within 5 minutes of your request. In the form also write where you can see the water.

In a Vaivari summer house after winter, the door seals and the shower hose have to be replaced, and the tray trap checked. Often asked in Jūrmala

The shower cubicle leaks: what should I write in the form?

Note when the water appears: only during the shower, only after the shower, or also when nobody is using it. Write this timing and the place where you see the water in the form, because it helps prepare the spare parts.

At what temperature do legionella bacteria multiply in a shower?

ECDC states 20–50 °C in standing water, so in a shower the places where water stands matter: the hose, the head and the seals.

Can the cubicle door rollers be replaced separately?

Often they can, if the roller diameter and fixing match. It is worth measuring or photographing the old roller next to a ruler, because there are many cubicle models.

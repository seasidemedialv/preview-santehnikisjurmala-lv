Shower set fitting - aerosol and limescale in the nozzles

Shower set fitting in Jūrmala concerns the place where the risk of legionellosis at home is highest: the Centre for Disease Prevention and Control (SPKC) states that people are infected by breathing in water aerosol in the shower.

In Jūrmala’s hard water the rain head nozzles clog with limescale, so the head and the filter are planned for upkeep already at installation. The price of fitting a shower set depends on whether it is surface-mounted or built in, on the supply pipes and on materials.

In a Soviet-era Bulduri block, where the heat unit gives at least 55 °C

In the part between the Lielupe, Bulduri and Dzintari south of the railway, the development is distinctly mixed: new and historic detached houses, low-rise buildings and Soviet-era multi-storey blocks.

In a block of flats the hot water comes from the building’s heat exchanger. Cabinet Regulation No. 906 has required the building manager since 9 March 2018 to ensure constantly that the water at the outlet of the heat exchanger is not cooler than +55 °C.

SPKC data show that Latvia registers on average 33 cases of legionellosis a year. People are infected by breathing in water aerosol, and the highest risk at home is in the shower.

The shower head and hose are exactly the place where water is sprayed into tiny droplets. So in a shower system what matters is not only the temperature, but also how clean the nozzles are and how easy they are to reach.

Temperature, limescale and push buttons: three assumptions about the shower

Often assumed: to avoid scalding in the shower, the building manager should lower the temperature at the heat unit.

In fact: Cabinet Regulation No. 906 requires the building manager to keep the hot water no lower than +55 °C, and the Health Inspectorate states that a constantly high temperature is the technically simplest way to limit legionella. The temperature in the shower is regulated by the mixer tap, not by the building’s heat unit.

Often assumed: limescale does not bother a stainless steel rain head.

In fact: Jūrmala’s drinking water is drawn from 20 artesian boreholes with a high concentration of calcium and magnesium. ECDC’s 15-point plan for tourist accommodation includes keeping shower heads and taps clean of limescale.

Often assumed: a push-button shower is only for gyms.

In fact: Hamworthy states that showers with a push button that switches off after 20–30 seconds use less hot water than ordinary ones, which are often left running. In a guest house with shared showers it is worth considering.

Filter-reducer before the showers: backflushing at least every 6 months

If the house or flat has a filter-reducer, its strainer is the first place where hard water and dirt hold back the flow on the way to the shower. For example, the SYR Drufi+ filter must be backflushed when the flow drops, but at least every six months.

For the same manufacturer, the reducer’s setting must be checked once a year. The SYR 315 reducer has a built-in 0.25 mm strainer, and the whole working-parts cartridge can be replaced without changing the set pressure.

In a guest house these two intervals are easy to tie to the season: the filter is flushed before summer and in autumn, and the reducer’s setting is checked once a year before the first guests. Upkeep of the filter and reducer can be discussed during the call together with the shower installation.

Rain shower installation: a head you can reach for cleaning

The total hardness of Jūrmala’s water is 3.21–4.25 mmol/l, and “Jūrmalas ūdens” does not plan to supply moderately hard or soft water. Limescale will form on the rain head nozzles on any model.

Among the places where legionella bacteria multiply, ECDC lists limescale and rubber seals. A rain head has both, so cleaning the nozzles is not only a matter of looks. The head is placed so that it can be taken off or reached by hand, and the cleaning product is chosen according to the head manufacturer’s instructions.

Whether the system will be surface-mounted or built into the wall is a separate choice. New outlets higher in the wall are described on the new supply and waste points page.

Thermostatic mixer installation and the 49 °C limit

In its water heater instructions, Lochinvar states that above 120 °F (49 °C) the risk of scalding rises, and advises thermostatic mixing valves at the draw-off points. In a house with 55 °C hot water that means a thermostatic mixer at each shower.

A thermostatic mixer can also go in front of push-button showers, which Hamworthy writes switch themselves off after 20–30 seconds. Then the temperature is set at the mixer, and the button only opens the water.

Worked total for a Dubulti guest house: three push-button showers before the season

In a Dubulti guest house with three shared showers, push-button shower sets with a thermostatic mixer are fitted before summer. On the same visit the water heater is cleaned, because ECDC advises tourist accommodation to clean, drain and disinfect water heaters once a year.

A water heater clean-out with an anode check is from €75 per water heater, so the work is at least €75 plus the fitting of three shower sets. The price of fitting them depends on whether the set is surface-mounted or built in, on the supply pipes and on materials, and is given after an inspection. The sets and spare parts are extra.

While the rooms are still empty, ECDC advises letting every tap and shower run for a few minutes at least weekly, and again before each guest arrives.

Often asked in Jūrmala

How do I clean a rain head when the water hardness is 3.21–4.25 mmol/l?

Limescale will form on the nozzles in Jūrmala’s water, because additional softening is up to the consumers themselves. ECDC advises keeping shower heads clean of limescale, and the product is chosen according to the head manufacturer’s instructions.

How long does a push-button shower run after one press?

Hamworthy writes about showers that switch themselves off after 20–30 seconds. They use less hot water than ordinary showers, which are often left running.

How fine is the strainer in the pressure reducer before the shower?

That depends on the model. For example, the SYR 315 reducer has a built-in 0.25 mm strainer, and its working-parts cartridge can be replaced without changing the set pressure.

Wall-hung toilet frame repair - limescale, button and frame height

Wall-hung toilet frame repair in Jūrmala often starts with limescale: the total hardness of the water is 3.21–4.25 mmol/l, and it settles in the fill valve and its strainer behind the wall.

Sometimes one of the two buttons does not work or the electric frame no longer moves. The price of repairing a wall-hung toilet depends on access behind the wall, the mechanism type and spare parts.

In the new low-rise blocks of Lielupe, when only the large button works

Until the property “boom” years, Lielupe was mainly detached houses, but now there are low-rise blocks of flats among them too. Around Meža prospekts and Bulduru prospekts, the plan allows only large detached houses on large plots.

Newer flats and houses often have wall-hung toilets on a concealed frame. In a dual-flush cistern each button flushes its own amount of water, for example 3 and 6 litres for WISA XS cisterns.

If only the large button works, the fault is often in the small button’s lever or its seat on the flush valve, not in the whole cistern. During the call, say which button does not work and since when.

Limescale and drawings: two assumptions about the hidden cistern

Often assumed: the cistern behind the wall is protected from limescale, because it has no contact with air.

In fact: the total hardness of Jūrmala’s water is 3.21–4.25 mmol/l, and according to the WHO document, water above 1.8 mmol/l is already very hard. “Jūrmalas ūdens” does not plan to supply moderately hard or soft water, so limescale in the fill valve is to be expected behind the tiles too.

Often assumed: after tiling, nobody knows any more where the cistern’s supply runs behind the wall.

In fact: points 32 and 33 of Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 require the user to keep their own water supply and sewerage diagrams and as-built drawings with the pipe diameters and fittings. If there are none, inventory drawings must be drawn up. In a new building, ask the building manager for them.

WISA XS WC Variotronic: 380–580 mm and 400 kg

For the XS WC Variotronic frame, WISA states a toilet rim height that is electrically adjustable from 380 to 580 mm, and a static load of up to 400 kg. Such a frame is both a plumbing and an electrical device.

If the frame itself is damaged, it is a new installation, not a repair, and fitting a concealed frame with a wall-hung toilet is from €85 per set. More often the fault is smaller: if the toilet no longer changes height but flushing works, first find out whether the frame is receiving power.

The electrical part is checked by an electrician. The plumbing part is the cistern with dual 3/6 l flushing, its valves and the supply.

Frame repair: the fill strainer in water of 3.21–4.25 mmol/l

Hardness is not the same across the city: in one sample of 22 May 2026 it was 4.0 mmol/l in Jaundubulti, but lower in Ķemeri, 2.33 mmol/l, which is below the 3.21–4.25 mmol/l stated by “Jūrmalas ūdens”.

The fill valve on the supply often has a fine strainer. In such water it slowly clogs, and the cistern takes longer and longer to refill after flushing, until the small button no longer gives a full flush.

One observation helps during the call: how long the cistern takes to fill after the large button, and whether this time has become longer over recent months.

Worked total for a Lielupe flat with two toilets

In a flat in a Lielupe low-rise block, the concealed cistern in the guest toilet has a dead small button, while in the bathroom the cistern of a floor-standing toilet leaks after flushing. Repairing the flush mechanism is from €35 per job, and the on-site inspection is already included in this price.

In total that is at least €35 plus the wall-hung toilet repair, whose price depends on the mechanism type, spare parts and access behind the wall. The lever, valve and other spare parts are extra.

Often asked in Jūrmala

How many litres does a dual-flush concealed cistern flush?

It depends on the model. For example, WISA states dual flushing of 3 and 6 litres for XS cisterns. If the small button flushes too little, its lever and the fill are checked first.

What should I do if the concealed cistern leaks through to the flat below?

Shut off the water to the flat at the incoming valve and tell the neighbour below. In the form, describe where you can see dampness, for example by the skirting board or on the neighbour’s ceiling, and since when.

How high can the toilet rim be raised in an electrically adjustable frame?

It depends on the model. For the WISA XS WC Variotronic the rim height is stated as 380 to 580 mm, so within 200 mm, and a static load of up to 400 kg. If the height no longer changes, the power to the frame is checked first.

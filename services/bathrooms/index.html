Bathroom plumbing installation in Jūrmala

More and more homeowners in Jūrmala are renovating their old bathrooms, replacing old Soviet-era fittings and building stylish, practical rooms. The work differs between old villas with complicated pipework and new builds.

We take on the whole plumbing part: pipework, fitting of the appliances and the supply and waste connections. The customer buys the fittings.

  • Fitting shower cubicles

    All kinds of shower cubicles: corner, recessed, open. Waterproofing, waste connection, water supply.

  • Fitting baths

    Steel, cast-iron and acrylic baths. Freestanding and built-in. Installation with a hydromassage system.

  • Fitting toilets

    Floor-standing and wall-hung toilets. Concealed flushing systems. Rimless technology.

  • Basin installation

    Inset, pedestal and wall-mounted basins. Traps, supply pipes, mixer tap fitting.

  • Reworking pipes

    Replacing old metal pipes with PEX or PP. New branch lines and manifold installation.

  • Underfloor heating installation

    Electric underfloor heating for the bathroom, for comfort and dry feet all year round.

When it leaks or will not flush: urgent bathroom jobs

Toilet and cistern repair: the cistern leaks or the toilet leaks at the floor. Repairing the flush mechanism starts from €35, with the on-site inspection already included.

Wall-hung toilet frame repair: the cistern behind the wall fills slowly or one of the buttons does not work. In Jūrmala’s hard water the fault may be limescale in the fill valve.

Shower cubicle repair: the tray, the seals or the trap leaks. A damaged part can be replaced without changing the whole cubicle.

Bath edge resealing: water gets under the bath through the joint. In a timber house it is worth doing before the floor goes soft.

In Priedaine and Bražciems, when the house is finally connected to the networks

In the city’s 2012 plan, the centralised water supply and sewerage networks in Priedaine were still only planned. On 25 September 2020 “Jūrmalas ūdens” (Jūrmala’s water utility) signed a contract to extend the networks in Bražciems and Bulduri, which also covers Priedaine.

The user builds the service pipe and the outlet up to the connection point in line with the technical conditions of “Jūrmalas ūdens” (binding regulations No. 38, point 11). After connecting, the user’s own decentralised sewerage structures used for collecting wastewater must be decommissioned (point 15).

If the house’s waste used to run to its own holding tank or septic tank, after connection it runs to the street network. The bathroom outlets then stay the same, and only the route the waste takes out of the house changes.

Worked total for a Dubulti block of flats: bath, toilet and basin

In Dubulti, on the river side of Dubultu prospekts, there is a separate micro-district of blocks of flats with buildings around shared courtyards. In a flat in such a building, the bath, the toilet and the basin are changed in the bathroom at the same time, while the shared riser stays.

Taking out the old bath and connecting the new one is from €70, swapping or fitting a toilet from €45, a basin fixed to the wall and connected from €50, at least €165 per job in total. Fittings and materials are extra.

If opening the wall shows that the riser also has to be replaced, this is agreed with the building manager, because the riser is shared. A water riser replaced on one floor is from €120 per floor.

Often asked in Jūrmala

How much does bathroom plumbing installation cost in Jūrmala?

The price of full bathroom plumbing installation starts from €450 (excluding the price of fittings and materials). The exact price depends on the number of appliances, the amount of pipe rework and the level of difficulty.

Can you fit a shower cubicle that I have bought myself?

Yes, we install shower cubicles, baths, toilets and basins from any manufacturer that the customer has bought independently. You can also ask us to suggest suitable models in different price ranges.

Do you also tile bathrooms?

Our specialisation is plumbing: pipework, fitting of appliances and supply connections.

What minimum waste pipe diameters does a bathroom need?

LBN 221-15 annex 3: toilet 100 mm; bath, shower and washing machine 40 mm; hand basin 32 mm; floor drain 50 or 100 mm.

Does a bathroom rebuild also require checking the house's sewer outlet?

Point 57 of Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 states that during reconstruction or when the owner changes, if there is no as-built documentation for the sewer outlet, the owner must carry out a CCTV pipe survey at their own expense. It must be submitted to “Jūrmalas ūdens”.

Why do bathroom taps and showers get limescale in Jūrmala?

Jūrmala’s water is taken from artesian boreholes with a high calcium and magnesium content, and the hardness of the treated water in 2024 was 3.5–4.0 mmol/l. That is very hard water.

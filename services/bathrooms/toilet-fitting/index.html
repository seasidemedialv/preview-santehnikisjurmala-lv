In Kauguri, where a third of Jūrmala's residents live

At the start of 2024, 16,821 people lived in Kauguri, or 32.3% of Jūrmala’s residents. Back in 2010, 97% of the people there used the centralised water supply, 20,895 of 21,503.

So swapping a toilet in Kauguri usually means work on the town water pipe and on the shared soil stack, which also carries the waste from the floors above. While the old toilet is off, the riser branch is closed with a plug, so that smell and water do not come into the flat.

The second Kauguri question is the flat’s shut-off valve. If there is none, the water must be turned off at the riser, which usually happens with the building manager’s knowledge. Swapping a toilet is a good moment to fit your own valve at the incoming supply.

Outlet, frame and gurgling: where assumptions go wrong

Often assumed: the new toilet will fit any old outlet, as long as an adapter can be found.

In fact: LBN 221-15 annex 3 sets a minimum diameter of 100 mm for the toilet’s waste connection. The 110 mm you see on plastic pipes is the outside size. An adapter to a narrower pipe does not cancel this requirement.

Often assumed: if the toilet gurgles after the swap or the water disappears from the trap, the fault is in the new toilet.

In fact: gurgling usually means the riser is short of air. LBN 221-15 requires the sewerage to be vented through a riser that goes out above the roof, and this riser must not be joined to the room ventilation or a flue.

Venting above the roof: 0.5 m, and a vacuum valve up to 5 storeys

Point 249 of LBN 221-15 states that the end of the sewerage venting riser above a pitched roof must be at least 0.5 m high. Above a flat roof that is not used, 0.3 m is enough.

Point 280: in a house of up to five storeys, a riser that is not taken out above the roof may be closed with a vacuum valve, if another riser in the same building is vented. The valve is fitted higher than the rim of the highest appliance.

In Babīte and Piņķi private houses this matters when a new toilet appears on the first floor up or in an extension. If the new branch has no venting, during the call we go through whether a valve above the toilet rim is enough or whether a pipe has to be run up to the roof.

Building code LBN 221-15 (Cabinet Regulation No. 332 of 30 June 2015): venting in points 249, 251 and 280, diameters in annex 3; viewed on likumi.lv on 19 November 2026.

Wall-hung toilet fitting and the drawing that stays after the tiles

Fitting a wall-hung toilet starts with the frame, which then disappears behind the wall finish. Fitting a concealed frame with a wall-hung toilet costs from €85 per set, labour price, materials not included.

Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 (points 32 and 33) require the user to keep their own water supply and sewerage diagrams and as-built drawings with the pipe diameters, fittings and meter assemblies. If there are none, inventory drawings must be drawn up.

Behind the frame stay the water supply, its shut-off valve and the 100 mm outlet. Photograph them with a tape measure before the wall is closed. That way you will later know where you may drill for a shelf, and the photo will also serve this requirement of the regulations.

Urinal installation in a cafe or guest house

A urinal is usually placed next to the toilet and the basin on the same waste branch. Point 253 of LBN 221-15 requires a rodding eye, that is, a cleaning opening, where a waste pipe serving three or more appliances begins.

On a horizontal run of pipe up to 100 mm, the distance between rodding eyes may be up to 12 m, and between end caps up to 8 m (table 3). A urinal needs its own water and waste outlet, and a new outlet costs from €35 per point.

Worked total for a Kauguri flat: toilet and basin in one go

When a floor-standing toilet and the basin next to it are replaced in a Kauguri block of flats, the labour total is at least €95: swapping or fitting a toilet from €45 and a basin fixed to the wall and connected from €50. The toilet, flexible pipe, seals and fixings are extra.

If the old outlet has to be replaced, replacing a waste pipe is from €25/m. If you want to move the toilet to another wall, that is already new supply and waste points work.

Often asked in Jūrmala

What fall must the pipe from the toilet to the riser have?

LBN 221-15 states that 75–100 mm pipes are laid without calculation at 20 ‰, or a fall of 2 cm per metre. The slope must not change between connections, and the largest permitted slope is 150 ‰.

What if the flat has no shut-off valve of its own?

Point 53 of Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 states that the user is responsible for the consequences if the user’s system lacks shut-off fittings. So when a toilet is swapped, it is worth fitting the flat’s own valve.

Can sewerage venting be connected to a ventilation duct?

No. Point 251 of LBN 221-15 forbids joining the venting riser to the room ventilation or a flue. It must go out above the roof or end with a vacuum valve, if the building meets the conditions of point 280.

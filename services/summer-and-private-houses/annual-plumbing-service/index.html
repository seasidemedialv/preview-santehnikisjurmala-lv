Plumbing service for a private house - an annual check of the house plumbing

Plumbing service for a private house in Kauguri, Lielupe or Piņķi is a visit, usually once a year, in which the plumber checks the parts that wear slowly and out of sight: the pressure-reducing valve, shut-off valves, traps, flush mechanisms and the water heater.

In Jūrmala there are two reasons for it: very hard water and regulations that require drains to be cleaned as a precaution. The price is set by the size of the property, the number of parts to check and the defects found.

In the first year after connecting, in Kauguri's detached-house streets

In 2018 the fourth phase of the water works designed networks in Kauguri’s detached-house streets, for example Dambja, Jurģu, Kauņas, Pērļu, Rūpniecības, Staburaga, Tīruma, Uzvaras and Vētras iela. In houses that connect there to the new water main, the whole house network works for the first time on town water and its pressure.

For the water from the Kauguri water treatment station in 2026 (until 19 August) the average hardness was 2.68 mmol/l. According to the WHO document cited by “Jūrmalas ūdens”, water above 1.8 mmol/l counts as very hard. Hard water leaves limescale on tap aerators, shower heads and the water heater’s heating element.

This is a good moment for a first service: the new service pipe and the pressure-reducing valve have run for a year, and you can see how the water acts on the house’s appliances.

What is often assumed about servicing and how it really is

Often assumed: the pressure-reducing valve is set once and for all. In fact: the SYR Drufi+ instructions require the valve’s setting to be checked once a year. It is set at the factory to 4 bar and adjustable within 1.5–6 bar.

Often assumed: a drain is cleaned when it blocks. In fact: point 34.5 of Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 requires the user to clean the sewer networks in their service zone as a precaution at least twice a year.

Often assumed: a pumping unit in the basement is looked at only when it starts to signal. In fact: Saniflo advises checking it by eye once a month and having a qualified specialist look at it once a year; appliances connected to it must not be used during a power cut.

The pressure-reducing valve: a 0.25 mm strainer and a 1.3 bar drop

The SYR 315 pressure-reducing valve (EN 1567) has a built-in 0.25 mm strainer, and the cartridge with all the working parts can be replaced without changing the set pressure. The strainer catches sand and sediment, so at a service it is flushed.

The manufacturer states that at maximum flow the pressure after the valve drops by 1.3 bar, and that the pressure must be set at static pressure, when no tap is running. If it is set while someone is using the shower, the reading is misleading.

For the SYR Drufi+, the inlet pressure must be at least 1 bar higher than the outlet pressure you want, and the strainer is back-flushed when the flow drops, but at least once every six months.

The SYR 315 data sheet and the SYR Drufi+ DFR operating instructions; Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38, point 34.5. Checked 19 November 2026.

The annual house plumbing check: what is looked at

The total hardness of “Jūrmalas ūdens” water is 3.21–4.25 mmol/l, and the company does not plan to supply medium-hard or soft water. So in the annual check the main thing is the parts where limescale builds up: the water heater’s safety valve and heating element, the mixer tap cartridges, the toilet’s flush mechanism.

Next come the shut-off valves. A valve that has not been touched for years may not close in an emergency. At a service each shut-off valve is turned off and back on, and if it leaks or does not close, it is marked for replacement.

Last come the drains: traps, floor drains and rodding eyes, and also the yard sewer, which the regulations require to be cleaned twice a year.

Example: a detached house in the Lielupe pine park

In Lielupe, around Meža and Bulduru prospekts, there is pine-park housing where only large detached houses on large plots are allowed. At a service in such a house the plumber finds a worn toilet flush mechanism (from €35) and a dripping mixer tap, replaced with your own tap (from €25).

For the yard sewer, which the regulations require to be cleaned twice a year, the drain line is cleaned on the same visit (from €50). Together at least €110 for the repairs, plus the service by the number of parts checked. The prices are labour prices, materials not included.

Often asked in Jūrmala

Where can I see how hard the water is in my part of Jūrmala?

It differs by station. In one sample on 22 May 2026 the total hardness ranged from 2.33 mmol/l in Ķemeri to 4.0 mmol/l in Jaundubulti. “Jūrmalas ūdens” publishes its measurements on its website.

What pressure must there be before the pressure-reducing valve?

The SYR Drufi+ instructions require the inlet pressure to be at least 1 bar higher than the outlet pressure you want. If the town pressure is 4 bar, you cannot set more than 3 bar after the valve.

Does a service also clean the yard sewer?

If needed. Point 34.5 of regulations No. 38 requires the sewer in your zone to be cleaned as a precaution at least twice a year; cleaning the yard drain line is from €50 and can be added to the same visit.

From there the plumber builds the yard section to the house and joins it to the indoor system, and “Jūrmalas ūdens” accepts the connection in six steps. The price of the yard work is set by length, depth, restoring the surface and permits.

When a capped pipe appears at the fence in Dzintari

Where networks were built along the street in Dzintari and Majori, the water service pipe up to the property boundary is often visible as a capped pipe above ground, and the sewer connection as an inspection chamber cover opposite the property. In the fourth phase the networks are brought right up to the plot boundary, so these two spots decide where the yard trench will start.

“Jūrmalas ūdens” prepares the technical conditions in 10 working days free of charge, and they state where and how to connect. With them in hand you can plan the length and depth of the trench.

The technical conditions are valid for two years, so before digging it is worth checking that this period has not yet expired.

The meter and the duty to connect: two assumptions

Often assumed: the water meter at the incoming supply is installed by the plumber who builds the service pipe.

In fact: point 11 of regulations No. 38 states that the user builds the service pipe and outlet up to the connection point, and the meter is then installed by “Jūrmalas ūdens”. The plumber prepares the place for the meter assembly with access.

Often assumed: once a network has been built in the street, the house must connect to it at once.

In fact: for the fourth-phase streets “Jūrmalas ūdens” states that connecting is not compulsory. But if the house does connect to the centralised sewer, point 15 of regulations No. 38 requires its own structures used to store waste water to be removed.

Six steps and the documents under point 13 of regulations No. 38

“Jūrmalas ūdens” describes the connection in six steps: a request for technical conditions to the company’s technical department, a building design, construction with a qualified building specialist, meter installation, an opinion on readiness and a contract.

After construction the owner submits an as-built survey plan and, for gravity networks of 200 mm diameter and up, a CCTV survey as well. Two written confirmations go with it: about the owner’s own well or driven well and about rain and drainage water not being discharged into the sewer.

Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38, points 11, 13 and 15; the “Jūrmalas ūdens” connection procedure. Checked 19 November 2026.

The register of decentralised systems after connecting

In Jūrmala, septic tanks, holding tanks and treatment plants are registered by SIA “Jūrmalas ūdens”, which keeps the register of decentralised systems (binding regulations No. 37, points 4.2.1, 28 and 29). If anything in a system changes, point 30.8 of the regulations requires it to be reported within a month.

When a septic tank or holding tank is removed after connecting, the register entry changes too. For how to do it formally, ask “Jūrmalas ūdens”, since it keeps this register itself.

Jūrmala binding regulations No. 37 (25 July 2024), in force since 6 September 2024, likumi.lv; checked 20 November 2026. They do not apply to villages in Mārupe and Tukums Municipalities.

Worked example: a Dzintari house where the septic tank must be removed after connecting

In a Dzintari house on a fourth-phase street, once it is connected to the centralised network the old septic tank must be removed (regulations No. 38, point 15), and a place with access must be prepared for the meter assembly (regulations No. 38, point 11). The removal of the septic tank is reported to “Jūrmalas ūdens” within a month so that the register entry changes (regulations No. 37, point 30.8).

The metres of yard pipe are set after an inspection, since depth, restoring the surface and permits change them. Inside the house two new outlets are needed, for water and for waste: an extra outlet is from €35 per point, so 2 × €35 is at least €70, labour price, materials not included.

Often asked in Jūrmala

At what pressure is the new service pipe tested before handover?

The building code LBN 222-15 (point 161) states that the test pressure for the water pipe is given in the building design, and the standard LVS EN 805:2022 can be used for the tests. So look for the exact figure in your connection’s design.

When does a “Jūrmalas ūdens” specialist need to be arranged to inspect the connection in an open trench?

Before backfilling, while the pipes in the trench are still visible. For the fourth-phase streets “Jūrmalas ūdens” states that the yard connection is shown to the company’s specialist in the open trench, so it is worth arranging the time before digging starts.

In Babīte, Piņķi or Lapmežciems, is the connection agreed with “Jūrmalas ūdens”?

No. In Babīte and Piņķi water and sewerage are provided by SIA “Mārupes komunālie pakalpojumi”, and in Lapmežciems, Bigauņciems and Ragaciems, which are in Tukums Municipality, by SIA “Tukuma ūdens”. Ask the relevant company about the connection requirements.

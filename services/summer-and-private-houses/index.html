Urgent: frost is already in the pipe or it has burst

Frozen pipes are the only job in this section that does not wait. If there is no water at the tap and behind the wall or under the floor there is an unheated room, the plumber looks for the frozen stretch, thaws it without an open flame and checks that the pipe has not burst.

If the pipe has already burst and water is running, first shut the water off for the room, or at the meter assembly for the whole house, as “Jūrmalas ūdens” advises, and fill in the form. The page on the emergency plumber describes the burst and the clearing of the water.

Frost also hits the meter. “Jūrmalas ūdens” warns that if an inspection finds a frozen, damaged meter in the chamber that was not properly protected, the cost of replacement and related work is paid by the service user. So it is better to thaw a frozen incoming supply from the house side and then look at the meter chamber too.

Plannable: before the season, building work or a sale

Plumbing for a new private house is new-build or rebuild work: water supply, indoor drains and connecting the fittings. The price is from €55 per point. In Spuņciems, for example, the detailed plan for new houses currently allows local water supply and a sealed holding tank until the networks are built. So the supply and outlet in the house are placed where they can later run to the street network.

Connection to the mains water and sewer is the yard section from the house to the connection point at the boundary. In Jūrmala the owner builds it to the technical conditions of “Jūrmalas ūdens”, which the company prepares in 10 working days free of charge. After construction the owner submits an as-built survey and confirmations, including that rain and drainage water are not discharged into the sewer.

Draining down a summer house for winter is draining in autumn and refilling in spring: pipes, water heater, traps and, if needed, the heating loop. In Jūrmala, leaving indoor taps open before the meter so the pipe does not freeze is prohibited without agreement from “Jūrmalas ūdens”, so the water is drained, not left to drip.

Plumbing service for a private house is a visit, usually once a year, that checks the pressure-reducing valve, shut-off valves, flush mechanisms and waste pipes. Jūrmala’s regulations require drains in your own zone to be cleaned as a precaution at least twice a year, and very hard water slowly deposits in taps and the water heater.

A plumbing check before buying or selling a house shows what needs repair before you sign the contract. When ownership changes and there are no as-built documents for the sewer outlet, the regulations require a CCTV drain survey of the pipes.

Autumn in the old fishing villages: Asari, Dubulti, Kauguri

The Jūrmala territorial plan describes two kinds of residential areas. In the old fishing villages (Dubulti, Vecbulduri, Asari, Kauguri) the plots are irregular, and one house is usually both a family home and a summer house. In the parts planned for summer houses (Lielupe, Bulduri, Dzintari, Jaundubulti) the plots are large and regular.

If only part of such a house is lived in over winter, the pipes to the empty wing or the upper floor sit without flow in an unheated space. “Jūrmalas ūdens” itself notes that the number of residents and guests in Jūrmala changes greatly between summer and winter, and the whole load on the sewer system depends on it.

Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 (point 36) make the user responsible for preparing their own water pipes, fittings and meter assembly for winter. So in autumn the work in such a house is split into two zones: the lived-in part stays in service, while for the unheated part the branch is shut off and the water drained. In the Babīte area and the coastal villages as far as Ragaciems, Jūrmala’s regulations do not apply: Mārupe or Tukums Municipality binding regulations are in force there. For the technical requirements of a connection, ask the local water service provider.

Example: the upper floor of a house in Asari before winter

In Asari there is a house with a lived-in ground floor and a summer floor upstairs. In autumn the water is drained from the upper floor; the plumber estimates this job by the number of water points and the access. On the same visit a dripping mixer tap in the kitchen is replaced (from €25, your own tap) and a leak at a branch valve is put right (from €35).

For the two repairs together that is at least €60, plus the draining work by scope. If another leak turns up in spring when the water is refilled, fixing it is from €35 per leak point. The prices are labour prices, materials not included. If the house is outside Jūrmala and the places around it on this page, whether the trip costs extra, you find out on the phone, before any work starts.

Often asked in Jūrmala

Where does the “Jūrmalas ūdens” network end and the house plumbing begin?

According to the company’s explanation, its responsibility for the water supply ends at the last valve before the property boundary, and for the sewer at the last inspection chamber before the boundary. The networks on the plot and in the building belong to the owner, unless the building’s documents say otherwise.

Does “Jūrmalas ūdens” supply water in Babīte and Piņķi?

No. Babīte and Piņķi are in Mārupe Municipality, where water supply and sewerage are provided by SIA “Mārupes komunālie pakalpojumi”, to which the former “Babītes siltums” was added on 6 September 2024. Lapmežciems, Bigauņciems and Ragaciems are in Tukums Municipality, where the tariffs of SIA “Tukuma ūdens” are set by the regulator’s (SPRK) decision of 29 October 2026.

Can every part of Jūrmala connect to the town sewer?

“Jūrmalas ūdens” states that after the fourth phase of the water works, centralised sewerage is available to 99 % of residents. The rest use decentralised systems, for example a holding tank or a septic tank.

A summer house for winter without a dripping tap - draining down and refilling the water system

Winterising a summer house’s water system in Jūrmala cannot rely on a dripping tap: point 51.6 of binding regulations No. 38 prohibits leaving indoor taps open before the meter without agreement from “Jūrmalas ūdens”.

In October in Vaivari, when a summer house is locked up until May

In Vaivari, plots began to be allotted to summer residents at the end of the 19th century, and the Vaivari summer-house blocks are part of the urban planning monument of national importance that covers the summer-house districts of Vaivari, Asari, Melluži, Pumpuri and Jaundubulti. In a summer house the pipes tend to run through a veranda or under a floor with no basement, where there is no heat in winter.

Point 36 of Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 puts the preparation of the water pipes, fittings and meter assembly for winter on the user. The meter assembly is often in a chamber in the yard, not in the house.

“Jūrmalas ūdens” asks you to check before winter that the meter chamber lid is intact and closes tightly, and where needed to insulate the chamber further with moisture-resistant materials, keeping access to the meter. This can be done at the same time as draining the water.

The water heater and the basement toilet: two assumptions

Often assumed: in an empty house the water heater is either left on as usual or drained.

In fact: the Lochinvar electric water heater’s instructions include a “Vacation” mode that holds around 10 °C and is meant for a long absence: it saves energy and protects the water heater from freezing in cold weather. It suits a house where the power stays on in winter; whether your water heater has a similar mode is shown in its instructions.

Often assumed: a toilet with a macerator pump can be left as it is.

In fact: the Saniflo SANICUBIC instructions require that in an unheated building the toilet, pipes and pump are either prepared for winter with antifreeze intended for such systems or drained completely.

A fall of 0.001: why the pipe drains completely

The building code LBN 222-15 (point 154) requires water pipes to be designed with a slope of at least 0.001 towards the drain-off end, and on level terrain not less than 0.0005. That is 1 mm per metre, and it is exactly why a correctly laid pipe can be drained completely.

The house’s as-built drawings show where the pipes have their low points and drain valves. Points 32 and 33 of Jūrmala’s regulations require the user to keep water and sewer diagrams with pipe diameters, depths, fittings and meter assemblies, and where there are none, survey drawings must be drawn up.

The yard service pipe from the meter chamber to the house does not drain by fall, so depth protects it from frost. The building code LBN 003-19 notes that sandy soils freeze deeper than clay ones.

LBN 222-15, point 154; LBN 003-19; Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38, points 32, 33, 36 and 51.6 (likumi.lv, checked 20 November 2026).

Refilling the water system in spring: fill the water heater before the power

The Lochinvar water heater’s instructions require the tank to be filled completely, and the air let out, before the power is connected; more on the page about a flat flooded from above.

Only after that is the power switched on for the water heater, and until then the switch in the fuse board stays off. If the water heater is from another manufacturer, see its instructions for the order and timing.

Worked example: a Vaivari summer house with a basement toilet and a heating loop

In a Vaivari summer house the basement has a toilet with a pumping unit and a heating loop with radiators. In autumn the water supply is drained and the unit emptied; the price of this work depends on the number of water points and access.

The previous winter the loop was not drained and one radiator has burst: changing it is from €70. In the basement a toilet has burst too: fitting a new toilet is from €45. Together at least €115, labour price, materials not included, plus the winterising work by scope.

Often asked in Jūrmala

What Sentinel X500 dose does a heating loop in an empty house need?

The manufacturer of Sentinel X500 requires a dose of at least 20%, so the dose is chosen by how cold the unheated house can get. The freezing temperatures for each dose are on the heating page.

Does a heated towel rail protect an empty house from frost?

Only if it has such a function. For example, the Terma MOA 43D electric heating element switches on its own frost-protection mode if the temperature at the sensor falls below 6 °C, even when the heater is switched off. One towel rail does not protect a whole house.

Do Jūrmala's winterising rules apply in Ragaciems and Lapmežciems?

No. Lapmežciems, Bigauņciems and Ragaciems are in Lapmežciems parish in Tukums Municipality, where water services are provided by SIA “Tukuma ūdens” and Tukums Municipality binding regulations apply. Ask this company about the requirements for the meter assembly.

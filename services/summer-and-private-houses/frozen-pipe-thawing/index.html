Frozen pipes in Jūrmala - when frost finds an empty room

Frozen pipes in Jūrmala are most often where the water does not move for weeks: in an empty summer house, under a veranda floor or under an unoccupied flat.

In March in Bulduri, when a summer house is opened after winter

In Bulduri, plots began to be rented to summer residents in 1833, and in 1875 there were around 400 summer houses here. The territorial plan names Bulduri among the parts created specifically for summer houses, with large, regular plots.

In a house where nobody lives in winter, the water in the pipes does not move. Point 36 of Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 puts the preparation of the water pipes, fittings and meter assembly for winter on the user, and if that has not been done, frost finds every full stretch.

In March, when the owner arrives and opens the main tap, it is worth letting the water in for the first time only when someone stands at the meter assembly and can shut it again at once. The thermostat and the dripping tap: two assumptions

Often assumed: if the radiator thermostats in an empty house are on 0, the house is protected from frost.

In fact: on an Oventrop thermostatic head, position 0 closes the valve, and the radiator does not heat even when the room gets cold. The snowflake position opens the valve if the room cools below 6 °C, so in a house with the heating on, the heads are left on that setting.

Often assumed: a dripping tap will keep the pipe from freezing, and it is everyone’s own business.

In fact: in Jūrmala it is prohibited, without agreement from “Jūrmalas ūdens”, to leave indoor taps open before the meter to prevent a pipe freezing (regulations No. 38, point 51.6). It is safer to insulate the stretch or reroute it.

Foam sleeves in a garage, an attic and a crawl space

For example, the City of Toronto advises insulating exposed pipes in basements, crawl spaces, garages and attics with foam sleeves. In Jūrmala’s summer houses the same places are the veranda, the summer kitchen and a floor with no basement.

The sleeve is chosen by the pipe’s outer diameter so that it fits tightly, and the seam is taped. Insulation slows the cooling, but in a pipe where water stands for weeks in an unheated space it does not stop freezing, so in an empty house such a branch is better shut off and drained.

A frozen water pipe under an empty flat's floor

In January 2024 a block of flats in Jūrmala had no cold water for 10 days, because part of the frozen water pipe ran under the floor of an unheated, unoccupied flat. In this way one empty flat can leave without water everyone who draws from the same pipe.

To reach the pipe you need access to the empty flat. This is usually arranged by the building manager with the flat’s owner, unless the building’s documents say otherwise. The plumber works in a room that the manager or owner opens.

The price of thawing such a stretch is set by how long the pipe runs under the floor and whether the floor has to be opened. If the burst is under the floor, replacing the pipe is charged by the metre, and during the call you go through both jobs. For buildings with a manager, see also plumbing for building managers.

Worked example: a Bulduri summer house with a burst stretch under the veranda

In a Bulduri summer house a 6 m stretch of water pipe under the veranda floor has burst, and a tap has split in the frozen summer kitchen. Replacing the pipe is from €10/m, so 6 m is at least €60, and replacing the tap with your new tap is from €25.

The total is at least €85, labour price, materials not included, and the plumber estimates the thawing by the length of the frozen stretch and access. If you can see ice at the meter assembly, say so during the call right away.

Often asked in Jūrmala

Does a water barrel brought in a crisis help in frost?

Only for a short time. In a crisis “Jūrmalas ūdens” can bring a water barrel, but in frost the water freezes in the barrel too. So it matters more to thaw the pipe and replace the burst stretch.

Can an insulated pipe in a summer house stay full all winter?

In an unheated space that is a risk. Insulation only slows the cooling, so in an empty house the branch is shut off and drained, as described on the page about winterising a summer house’s water system.

Does Jūrmala's point 51.6 apply in Babīte and Lapmežciems?

No, Jūrmala’s regulations do not apply there. In Babīte and Piņķi the binding regulations of Mārupe Municipality are in force, and in Lapmežciems, Bigauņciems and Ragaciems those of Tukums Municipality; ask the water service provider there about the rules on taps before the meter.

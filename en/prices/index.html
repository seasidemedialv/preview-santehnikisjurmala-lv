Inspection from €40 and the labour price: when you pay for which

The “from” price of every job already includes the plumber’s trip to Jūrmala and the other places listed on the page where we work. On-site inspection from €40 per visit is the price when the plumber comes, inspects and no other work is done. This €40 is not added to the labour price.

Small jobs at an hourly rate from €25/h is a plumber by the hour for jobs that are not given a separate row in the table; they are listed on the page plumber by the hour. An urgent visit while water is leaking has no figure: the price depends on whether it is night or a weekend, on the distance and on the urgency.

If the job is outside these places, whether the trip costs extra, you find out on the phone, before any work starts.

May and October: two rows without a figure

In Jūrmala many houses and flats stand empty in winter, so two rows of the table are made for the season: draining a summer house for winter and refilling in spring and seasonal plumbing service. For both, the price depends on the number of water points, on whether the house has a pump or a water heater, and on access.

The autumn work is set by the city’s rules. Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 (point 36) require the user to prepare the water pipe, fittings and meter assembly on their side for winter. “Jūrmalas ūdens” states that if a meter that froze in its chamber was not protected from frost, the user pays for the replacement and the related work.

In spring, after a long absence, the Health Inspectorate advises running off both the cold and the hot water: open the tap only slightly at first. If something then leaks or will not drain, those are jobs with a row of their own.

Worked example: a rented flat in Jaundubulti in May. Before the first tenants, a cracked toilet must be replaced (swapping or fitting a toilet at least €45), a new washing machine connected (at least €30), and a kitchen tap that the owner bought replaced (at least €25). In total at least €100, labour price, materials not included.

€27.25 is the fee of “Jūrmalas ūdens”, not ours

“Jūrmalas ūdens” itself publishes its fee for connecting a property to the water and sewer networks: €27.25 including VAT. This sum is not in our price table, and it is paid to the company.

Before a connection the company prepares the technical conditions free of charge (within 10 working days); they are valid for two years. The service pipe and the outlet pipe up to the connection point are built by the user (binding regulations No. 38, point 11). So our table has a row for it, yard connection to the town network: the price depends on length, depth, reinstating the surface and permits.

Around Babīte the rules are different: Mārupe Municipality co-finances building a private house connection if the main network is in the street by the property and the connection was built after 1 July 2022. More on the page connection to the mains.

Hard water of 3.21–4.25 mmol/l and limescale work

“Jūrmalas ūdens” states a total hardness of 3.21–4.25 mmol/l; according to a WHO document, above 1.8 mmol/l water is already very hard, and the company does not plan to supply softer water. In one sample of 22 May 2026 the hardness in Jaundubulti was 4.0 mmol/l, and in Ķemeri 2.33 mmol/l, so lower than this range.

In the price table this shows in two rows: water heater clean-out with an anode check from €75 per water heater, and hourly work when tap aerators and shower heads need cleaning.

Worked example: a detached house in Dzintari in October. A water heater clean-out with an anode check is at least €75. Cleaning aerators and shower heads at three points, if it takes 2 hours, is 2 h × €25/h, at least €50. In total at least €125, labour price, materials not included (anode, seals).

Often asked in Jūrmala

Is the €40 inspection paid on top of the labour price?

No. On-site inspection from €40 per visit is the price only if the plumber comes, inspects and no other work is done. If the work is done, you pay the labour price, which already includes the trip.

How is riser replacement priced in a multi-storey building?

Per floor. Replacing a water riser on one floor is from €120 per floor, and replacing a soil stack on one floor is also from €120 per floor. Pressure flushing a riser is a separate row: from €80 per riser.

How is pipe replacement priced by the metre?

Replacing water pipe is from €10/m, replacing waste pipe from €25/m. If the whole water supply in a flat or house must be replaced, it has its own row: from €500 per property.

Radiator not heating: what to check at the thermostat and the valve

Radiator not heating? First look at the thermostatic head: in position 0 it closes the valve completely, and at a low position in a warm room the valve rightly stays shut.

If the head is open, but the radiator is cold at the top and warm at the bottom, there is usually air in it. If every radiator in the house is cold, the problem is in the system or the heat supply, not in one radiator.

Why a radiator stays cold: the valve, air and circulation

A thermostatic head is not an on switch, but a regulator. On an Oventrop head, position 0 closes the valve, the scale 1–5 corresponds to about 12, 16, 20, 24 and 28 °C, and the snowflake opens the valve only if the room cools below 6 °C. If the room is already warmer than the setting, the valve closes, and the radiator rightly turns cool.

The head measures the air around itself. Oventrop states that the thermostat or its remote sensor must not be covered by curtains or furniture: warm air collects behind a thick curtain, the head thinks the room is warm and closes the valve, although the room is cool.

Air collects at the top of a radiator because it is lighter than water. Then the radiator is warm at the bottom and cold at the top, and you often hear bubbling in it. If the whole radiator is cold but the head is open, the valve pin may be stuck, or no flow reaches the radiator.

What is safe to check yourself

Turn the head up to 5 and wait. If after a while the radiator starts to warm, the fault was in the setting or in the sensor’s position. Check that the head is not covered by a curtain, a sofa or a decorative screen.

If the head cannot be turned all the way, it may have limiter pins. Oventrop states that the setting can be limited or locked with these pins. It is not a fault.

Check by hand whether the radiator is warm at the bottom and cold at the top, or cold all over, and whether it is the same in other rooms. This information shows the plumber or the building manager at once where to look.

In a summer house left over winter with minimal heating, the snowflake position is meant for exactly that: it keeps the room above 6 °C. If in an empty house the radiator is cold in this position, that is normal while the room is warmer than 6 °C.

If the bathroom towel rail with an electric element is not heating

A combined heated towel rail that is connected to the heating and also has an electric element has its own rules. Terma requires that the element’s output does not exceed 60% of the radiator’s output at 75/65/20 °C and that the pressure does not exceed 10 bar.

An air cushion must remain in the radiator, and, while the element runs, one valve must remain open. If both valves are shut, the element heats trapped water, so in summer, when the heating is off, check the valves, not only the element’s switch. Do not open the electrical part yourself.

What the manufacturer's instructions set

If the head is behind a curtain and cannot be moved, the solution is a head with a remote sensor. The scale figures 1–5 correspond to the temperature approximately, not exactly.

The heat carrier’s pressure, its temperature and the bleeding of the system in a block of flats are set by the building’s heating system, not by the flat’s radiator. So if all the radiators are cold, first ask the building manager whether the building is connected to the heating and whether there is work on the system.

Oventrop thermostatic head instructions (positions 0, snowflake, 1–5; sensor placement; limiter pins). Checked on 19 November 2026.

When it is the building manager's job and when a plumber's

In a block of flats, for example in Kauguri or Dubulti, the common heating system, the risers and their bleeding are usually the building manager’s responsibility, unless the building’s documents say otherwise. A radiator in a flat can be changed or repaired by a plumber, but work on a riser is agreed with the building manager.

In a private house with its own boiler, the whole circuit is yours. A plumber bleeds the radiators, frees a stuck valve pin, changes the thermostatic head or the valve and, if needed, flushes the circuit. The price of radiator repair or bleeding is set by the fault, the type of system and access; changing one radiator is from €70, labour price, materials not included.

Often asked in Jūrmala

What does the snowflake on a thermostatic head mean?

The frost-protection position. In this position an Oventrop head opens the valve only if the room cools below 6 °C, so in an ordinary room the radiator stays cold.

Why can the thermostatic head not be turned up to 5?

The head may have limiter pins. The manufacturer states that with them the setting can be limited or locked within a certain range.

What temperature does position 3 correspond to?

About 20 °C for an Oventrop head. Position 1 is about 12 °C, 2 about 16 °C, 4 about 24 °C and 5 about 28 °C.

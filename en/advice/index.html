Why the questions are different in Jūrmala homes

Jūrmala tap water is very hard: the company “Jūrmalas ūdens” states a total hardness of 3.21–4.25 mmol/l, so limescale builds up in water heaters and aerators faster than in soft water. In the storm of 29 July 2024 streets and yards flooded in several districts of the city, including Kauguri and Sloka. For owners of historic buildings, Jūrmala’s conditions require historic interiors to be protected from sudden swings in humidity and temperature, and a leak in a timber villa is exactly such a swing.

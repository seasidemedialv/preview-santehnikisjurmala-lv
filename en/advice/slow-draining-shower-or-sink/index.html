Why water does not drain away - water seal, hard water and wipes

Water most often does not drain because hair and a layer of soap, formed by the calcium in Jūrmala’s hard water, have built up in the water seal of the floor drain or trap.

The second cause is what should not be in the pipe: at Sloka’s treatment works, 14 tonnes of rubbish are screened out in a year.

Why it happens: a 30–50 mm water seal and 85–98 mg/l of calcium

Every floor drain and trap has a water seal: a bend in which water stands permanently and holds back the smell of the drains. For example, the ACO ShowerDrain S+ DN 50 floor drain comes in versions with a 50 mm and a 30 mm water seal (EN 1253-1). The flow slows at this point, and this is exactly where hair stops first.

“Jūrmalas ūdens” (Jūrmala’s water utility) gives a total hardness of 3.21–4.25 mmol/l and calcium in the treated water of 85–98 mg/l. Calcium forms an insoluble layer with soap, which sticks to hair and to the walls of the drain, so in a shower the outlet narrows slowly but steadily.

Further along the pipe, the problem is wipes and hygiene products. “Jūrmalas ūdens” reminds people that wet wipes, even those labelled “flushable”, belong in the rubbish bin; at the Sloka works about 2.7 million m³ of waste water are treated in a year and 14 tonnes of rubbish are screened out of it. On the way, wipes pass through the town network: Jūrmala’s sewerage is 435.6 km long, with 55 pumping stations.

A summer house after winter: a dried-out seal and standing water

In a house where nobody has run water for months, the seal of a floor drain and trap may dry out. Then a smell of drains comes from the outlet although there is no blockage, and it goes away when the seal has water in it again.

After a long absence the Health Inspectorate advises running both the cold and the hot water: open each tap a little at first and increase the flow to full within ten minutes. This running also fills all the water seals. If after that water stands in one outlet, look at the floor drain; if it stands in all of them, the common outlet pipe.

What is safe to check yourself without tools

Pour a bucket of water into a floor drain that has not been used for a long time. If the smell of the drains then goes away, the water seal had dried out and there is no blockage.

Take off the grate of the shower floor drain and remove the hair by hand or with a hook. Many floor drains have a removable insert under the grate, and it can be rinsed under the tap.

Do not use cleaners containing acid. Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 (sub-point 45.4) prohibit discharging into the sewer acids and substances that may release gases dangerous to people, including hydrogen sulphide.

Do not pour boiling water down a sink to “melt” a blockage. Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 (sub-point 40.4) state that the temperature of waste water discharged into the sewer must not exceed +40 °C.

If water rises in several places or in a basement floor drain, stop using water: cleaning a trap will not clear the common pipe.

Twice a year: what sub-point 34.5 of No. 38 requires

Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 (sub-point 34.5) require the user, within their service zone, to clean the drains preventively at least twice a year, not only when the water no longer drains away. In a private house this covers the drains of the house and yard up to the last inspection chamber.

Guest houses and cafés have one more point: properties that provide catering must have separate drains for greasy waste water and grease traps (point 66).

Checked 19 November 2026: points 34.5, 40.4, 45.4 and 66 of Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 (likumi.lv) and the reminder from “Jūrmalas ūdens” about wet wipes (lvportals.lv).

When water stands in the street chamber: the Jūrmalas ūdens emergency service

The responsibility of “Jūrmalas ūdens” for the sewerage ends at the last chamber before the property boundary. If waste water flows from a street chamber or into a ditch, the company asks you to report any such leak to its emergency service.

Up to the last chamber, the drains of the house and yard are the plumber’s job: clearing a blocked indoor drain from €40, flushing with water pressure from €60, labour prices, materials not included; more under drain cleaning.

Often asked in Jūrmala

May wet wipes labelled “flushable” be put down the toilet?

No. “Jūrmalas ūdens” reminds people that these wipes too belong in the rubbish bin; at the Sloka treatment works 14 tonnes of rubbish that has reached the sewer are screened out in a year.

May a pipe cleaner containing acid be used?

No. In Jūrmala it is prohibited to discharge into the sewer acids and substances that may release gases dangerous to people (No. 38, sub-point 45.4). A blockage is cleared mechanically or flushed out with water pressure.

How often must the drains of a private house be cleaned under Jūrmala's rules?

At least twice a year in your service zone, as sub-point 34.5 of No. 38 requires. It is preventive cleaning, not only when the water no longer drains away.

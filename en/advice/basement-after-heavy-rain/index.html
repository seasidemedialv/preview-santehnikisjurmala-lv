Basement after heavy rain: what to do

Is the basement full of water after heavy rain? First switch off the basement power at the consumer unit, if that can be done from a dry place, and do not step into the water while appliances are switched on there.

Then find out where the water comes from: up the floor drain from the sewer, through the walls from the ground or through the doors and windows from the street. That decides whether you need a plumber, the municipality or “Jūrmalas ūdens” (Jūrmala’s water utility).

Why water comes up the floor drain: an overloaded sewer and backflow

In the storm of 29 July 2024, by midday 112 had received about 350 calls from Jūrmala and other places around Riga. Streets and yards flooded, especially in Kauguri, Jaunķemeri, Vaivari, Sloka, Asari, Melluži, Majori and Dzintari, and in Asari, Pumpuri and Melluži the streets were like rivers.

In most of Jūrmala, rainwater has its own sewer, separate from the domestic sewerage. Discharging rain, snowmelt and drainage water into the domestic sewerage is prohibited (No. 38, sub-points 45.8 and 45.9). If someone connects them anyway, in a downpour the domestic network may overflow, and then the water looks for the lowest outlet: a basement floor drain, a shower or a toilet.

Jūrmala’s regulations (sub-point 34.4) also require that a basement with sinks, toilets, showers, floor drains or drainage has automatic shut-offs or gate valves in the sewer.

Checks that are safe to do yourself

The State Fire and Rescue Service (VUGD) lists the basement among the places with an increased risk of electric shock, because of the conductive floors and metal pipes. In a basement, garage or outbuilding only special 12 V lights may be used. While the floor is wet, do not touch the pump, the water heater or extension leads.

When the water is gone, look at whether the floor drain or the drain pipe has a gate valve or a non-return valve and whether it is closed. See whether the rodding eye covers are in place and tight. Mark how high the water reached and photograph it before you start cleaning: it helps to see whether the water came from the floor drain or from outside.

The company BALTA advises not keeping valuables in basement areas that flood, and placing water pumps and heating equipment at least 10–20 cm above the floor. Jūrmala’s regulations (point 59) prohibit storing food and valuables altogether in basements with sanitary fittings and drainage.

What Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 require of a basement

The service user must protect their basements from flooding by water and waste water by keeping the networks airtight in their zone of responsibility (point 61). If the system lacks shut-off fittings, the rodding eyes are not tightly closed or non-return valves are not fitted, the user answers for the possible consequences (point 53).

Non-return valves have their own standard, EN 13564. For example, the ACO Quatrix is a type 3 device for grey and black waste water, while the Junior (type 5) and the Triplex (type 2) suit grey waste water only. A basement toilet needs a valve that is also intended for black waste water.

If a basement toilet or shower works through a lifting unit, the manufacturer has its own requirement. The Saniflo SANICUBIC instructions require the rising main to be built with a backflow loop above the maximum backflow level, which, unless local requirements say otherwise, is the street level.

Checked 19 November 2026 on likumi.lv: points 34.4, 45.8, 45.9, 53, 59 and 61 of Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38.

Who does what: the municipality, Jūrmalas ūdens and the plumber

The rainwater sewer is not the domestic sewerage operator’s concern: the Engineering Structures Division of the municipality’s Development Department is responsible for it, and since January 2023 rainwater and drainage systems have been managed by Jūrmala city municipality. If rain gullies in the street have flooded, that is a matter for the municipality.

If waste water flows from a street chamber or into a ditch, “Jūrmalas ūdens” asks you to report it to its emergency service. Everything in the house and yard up to the last chamber is the owner’s section.

The plumber works there: clears a blocked outlet, repairs a drainage or sewage pump and fits a non-return valve or gate valve. The price of pump repair is set by the pump type, the fault and access to the chamber; clearing a blocked indoor drain is from €40, labour price, materials not included.

Often asked in Jūrmala

What non-return valve suits a basement toilet?

One that, under EN 13564, is also intended for black waste water. For example, the ACO Quatrix is a type 3 device for grey and black waste water, while the ACO Junior and Triplex suit grey waste water only.

May food and belongings be stored in a basement with a shower?

Point 59 of Jūrmala’s regulations prohibits it: in basements where sanitary fittings and drainage systems are installed, food and valuables must not be stored.

Who in Jūrmala is responsible for the rainwater sewer in the street?

The municipality. “Jūrmalas ūdens” states that the Engineering Structures Division of the municipality’s Development Department is responsible for the rainwater sewer, and since January 2023 the municipality has managed these systems.

Below, a short note on each strip; the pages for all the places are at the top of the list.

Jūrmala from east to west

The first place from the Riga side along the A10 road is Bražciems, on the right bank of the Lielupe by the road bridge. Next come Priedaine, about 17 km from Riga, and Buļļuciems, where the Lielupe flows into the gulf.

The Lielupe, Bulduri, Dzintari, Majori and Dubulti are part of the monument of national importance “Dubultu-Majoru-Dzintaru-Bulduru-Lielupes vasarnīcu rajons” (No. 6083). In Dubulti the Lielupe comes within only about 300 m of the sea.

Jaundubulti, Pumpuri, Melluži, Asari and Vaivari are a summer-house strip where, in Melluži and Asari, the sewer is in places laid 5–6 m deep. In the west are Kauguri, with the largest water treatment station (3,222 m³ a day), Sloka, Jaunķemeri and Ķemeri, 44 km from Riga, with the smallest (191 m³ a day).

The Mārupe Municipality side: Piņķi, Babīte and Sala parish

In 2024 Piņķi was the fourth largest village in Latvia. Babīte lies between the A5 and A10 roads. Spuņciems, the centre of Sala parish, is on a spit of land between the Lielupe and Lake Babīte; Sīpolciems and Vārkaļi are on the northern shore of the lake, with Vārkaļi only about 2 m above sea level.

Water and sewerage in Babīte, Piņķi and Spuņciems are provided by “Mārupes komunālie pakalpojumi”.

The coast and Smārde in Tukums Municipality

Bigauņciems, Lapmežciems and Ragaciems are in Lapmežciems parish between the Gulf of Riga and Lake Kaņieris, by the P128 road. Lapmežciems and Bigauņciems have in effect merged; Ragaciems is 3 km further on. From 1 December 2026 the tariffs of “Tukuma ūdens” apply to Lapmežciems parish.

Smārde is 12 km from Tukums; south of the railway, four multi-storey blocks built in the Soviet period stand alongside the private houses. If your place is not on the list, choose “Other” in the form.

One water, four stations

In Jūrmala the water is drawn from 20 artesian boreholes and treated at four stations: in Dzintari, Jaundubulti, Kauguri and Ķemeri. In Ķemeri the centralised water supply uses the deep Devonian Gauja aquifer, not sulphur spring water. Around Babīte and on the coast there are other water providers, so the rules and the connection procedure there differ from the city’s.

  2. Priedaine is on the right bank of the Lielupe, about 8 km from the centre of Jūrmala and 17 km from Riga. consulted 19 November 2026
  3. Buļļuciems is the eastern part of Jūrmala where the Lielupe flows into the Gulf of Riga, where a fishing village developed. consulted 19 November 2026
  4. The Lielupe is part of the urban planning monument of national importance “Dubultu - Majoru - Dzintaru - Bulduru - Lielupes vasarnīcu rajons” (No. 6083). consulted 19 November 2026
  5. In Dubulti the Lielupe bends north and comes within only about 300 m of the sea; it is Jūrmala's narrowest point between the river and the sea. consulted 19 November 2026
  6. In Melluži and Asari the sewer is in places laid 5–6 m deep by the pumping stations, and construction was hampered by a high groundwater level. consulted 19 November 2026
  7. The largest water treatment station is in Kauguri (3,222 m³ a day), the smallest in Ķemeri (191 m³ a day) consulted 19 November 2026
  8. Jūrmala's drinking water is drawn from 20 artesian boreholes and treated at four stations (Dzintari, Jaundubulti, Kauguri, Ķemeri); the sources have a high concentration of iron, sulphates, chlorides, calcium and magnesium. consulted 19 November 2026
  9. Ķemeri's centralised water supply uses the deepest Devonian Gauja aquifer (borehole DB-1933 on Partizānu iela near the city boundary), and the water is treated for iron and manganese before it enters the network (2016 data). consulted 19 November 2026
  10. Ķemeri is the western part of Jūrmala 44 km from Riga; the territory was long linked with the Duchy of Courland, while the rest of Jūrmala was in the Governorate of Livonia. consulted 19 November 2026
  11. By population, Piņķi was the fourth largest village in Latvia in 2024; in 2025 about 4,000 people lived there. consulted 19 November 2026
  12. The village lies between the A5 and A10 roads, about 4.7 km from the parish centre Piņķi and 13 km from the centre of Riga. consulted 19 November 2026
  13. Spuņciems is the centre of Sala parish in Mārupe Municipality, on a spit of land that separates the Lielupe from Lake Babīte, by the A10 road. consulted 19 November 2026
  14. Vārkaļi is only about 2 m above sea level - lower than the other villages of the parish. consulted 19 November 2026
  15. Water supply and sewerage in Babīte are provided by “Mārupes komunālie pakalpojumi”; the former service provider “Babītes siltums” was merged with it on 6 September 2024. consulted 19 November 2026
  16. “Babītes siltums”, which served Piņķi, was registered in Piņķi; in September 2024 it was merged with “Mārupes komunālie pakalpojumi”, which now provides water and sewerage services. consulted 19 November 2026
  17. Water and sewerage are now provided by “Mārupes komunālie pakalpojumi” with a single municipal tariff from 1 July 2026. consulted 19 November 2026
  18. In the territorial plan Bražciems is called the “gateway to Jūrmala”; it has detached-house development, low-rise development by the railway and unauthorised development in the forest, and building height is limited to keep the scale of the historic detached houses. consulted 19 November 2026
  19. Lapmežciems is the centre of Lapmežciems parish in Tukums Municipality between the Gulf of Riga and Lake Kaņieris, by the P128 road. consulted 19 November 2026
  20. To the north, along the Starpiņupīte, it borders Ragaciems, to the south, along the Siliņupe, Bigauņciems, with which it has in effect merged; 48 km to Riga, 28 km to Tukums. consulted 19 November 2026
  21. The village is 3 km from Lapmežciems and 52 km from Riga; it has in effect merged with Lapmežciems. consulted 19 November 2026
  22. Under the regulator's decision of 29 October 2026, the tariffs of “Tukuma ūdens” from 1 December 2026 apply also to Lapmežciems parish. consulted 19 November 2026
  23. The parish has two railway stations - Smārde and Milzkalne; 12 km to Tukums. consulted 19 November 2026

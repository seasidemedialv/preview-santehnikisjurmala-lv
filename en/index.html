An emergency at home: what to do first

A water leak or a blocked drain? Act quickly!

  • Shut off the water supply

    Find the main water valve (usually in the basement or by the water meter) and close it to stop the flow of water.

  • Switch off the electricity

    If water has reached electrical appliances or sockets, switch off the power at the fuse box.

  • Get the water off the floor

    Use towels, rags or a bucket to collect the water and stop the damage spreading to the floor and walls.

17 city districts and 10 places around: which homes where

Santehniķis Jūrmalā works in a city that grew up historically as 26 districts, from Bražciems and Priedaine in the east to Ķemeri and Jaunķemeri in the west; the place pages cover 17 city districts and 10 places around the city. After phase IV of the water utility’s works, centralised sewerage in Jūrmala is available to 99% of residents, but the homes themselves vary widely: multi-storey blocks and old timber summer houses, and the plumbing work in them differs.

Block-of-flats areas in Kauguri, Sloka and Dubulti

The territorial plan describes Kauguri and Sloka as a mass of multi-storey housing from the 1960s and 1970s next to uniform streets of detached houses. In Dubulti, the multi-storey blocks were built in the 1970s between the railway and Strēlnieku prospekts. In such buildings the work usually involves shared risers and the flat’s drains: riser replacement, drain cleaning. Place pages: Kauguri, Sloka, Dubulti.

Summer-house quarters from Lielupe to Dubulti

The Dubulti-Majori-Dzintari-Bulduri-Lielupe summer-house district is an urban planning monument of national importance (No. 6083), and it also contains monuments of local importance, for example “Dzintaru-Bulduru vasarnīcu kvartāli” (No. 6084). In the territory of the locally important monuments, repair of a historic building that may alter it may be carried out only with the written permission of the Jūrmala Cultural Space and Environmental Design Centre.

Where the networks were built in the street during phase IV, in Dzintari the water service pipe up to the property boundary is often visible as a capped pipe above ground. The sewer connection shows as an inspection chamber cover opposite the property. From there to the house the user themselves builds the service pipe: connection to the mains, plumbing in an old timber villa. Places: Bulduri, Dzintari, Majori.

Fishing villages and summer houses from Pumpuri to Ķemeri

Asari is one of the old fishing villages with irregular plots, where a house often combines a family home and a summer house. In Melluži and Asari the sewer is in places laid 5–6 m deep, and construction was hampered by high groundwater. In the storm of 29 July 2024 the streets in Asari, Pumpuri and Melluži were like rivers.

After such a downpour, the first things to check are the basement pump and the non-return valves: drainage and basement pumps. In Ķemeri the town water supply uses the deep Devonian Gauja aquifer, not sulphur spring water. Places: Asari, Melluži, Ķemeri.

Private houses around Babīte

Piņķi, Babīte, Spuņciems and the other villages of Babīte and Sala parishes are in Mārupe Municipality. In Babīte, Piņķi and Spuņciems, water and sewerage are provided by “Mārupes komunālie pakalpojumi”, which was merged with “Babītes siltums” on 6 September 2024. If the main network is in the street by the house, the municipality may co-finance building the connection. For a new house: plumbing a new house. Places: Piņķi, Babīte.

The coast to Ragaciems, and Smārde

Bigauņciems, Lapmežciems and Ragaciems are in Lapmežciems parish in Tukums Municipality, and from 1 December 2026 the tariffs of “Tukuma ūdens” apply there. In Smārde, south of the railway, four multi-storey blocks built in the Soviet period stand alongside the private houses. For summer houses on the coast, in autumn draining a summer house for winter is useful. Places: Lapmežciems, Ragaciems, Smārde; all 27: where we work.

In May, when summer houses and rented flats open up

In summer Jūrmala is home to more than its residents: already at the start of 2009 the city had 42 accommodation venues with 3,869 beds. In summer houses and rented flats that stood empty over winter, the water has sat in pipes and water heaters for months.

After a long absence the Health Inspectorate advises running off both the cold and the hot water: open the tap only slightly at first to avoid forming an aerosol, and within ten minutes raise the flow gradually to full. For guest houses ECDC advises keeping the hot water in the system at 50–60 °C and the cold below 20 °C: guest houses and lets.

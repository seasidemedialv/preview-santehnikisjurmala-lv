If the fault is outside your home

The responsibility of “Jūrmalas ūdens” (Jūrmala’s water utility) ends at the last gate valve before the property boundary for the water pipe, and at the last inspection chamber for the sewer. In an emergency the company advises a private house owner to shut off the water to the particular room, or at the meter assembly for the whole building. If that is not possible, contact the company’s emergency service. Any sewage leak must also be reported, even in a ditch.

In Babīte, Piņķi and Spuņciems the water is supplied by “Mārupes komunālie pakalpojumi”, in Lapmežciems parish by “Tukuma ūdens”. Faults in the house and yard are put right by a plumber: emergency plumber.

A summer house where nobody lives at the moment

If the owner is not on site, three things are useful in the form’s text:

  • who opens the gate or hands over the key, and on which days;
  • whether the water is shut off at the meter assembly and whether the meter is in a chamber in the yard;
  • when someone will live in the house again, so the work can be arranged around that date.

Before winter “Jūrmalas ūdens” advises checking the cover of the meter chamber and, if necessary, insulating the chamber while keeping access to the meter. If a meter in a chamber has frozen and was not protected from frost, the user pays for its replacement, so also write whether the chamber is accessible and whether the cover is intact. Often asked in Jūrmala

Which water company serves Babīte and Lapmežciems?

In Babīte, Piņķi and Spuņciems it is “Mārupes komunālie pakalpojumi”, in Lapmežciems parish (Lapmežciems, Bigauņciems, Ragaciems) “Tukuma ūdens”. The Jūrmala city networks are run by “Jūrmalas ūdens”.

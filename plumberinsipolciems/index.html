The tank in the yard and the toilet in the house: two jobs in Sīpolciems

Replacing drain pipes. If the house is not connected to centralised sewerage, waste water runs along a yard pipe to a septic tank or holding tank. If the pipe has settled or its joints have come apart, waste water stands in the pipe or leaks into the ground. Replacing waste pipe costs from €25/m. For example, 12 m of pipe from the house to the tank at the per-metre price is at least €300 of labour, materials not included. If the pipe is whole but blocked, cleaning is enough: “Cleaning the yard drain line” costs from €50. It is worth fitting a rodding eye by the house wall so that the next blockage can be cleared without digging up the pipe.

Toilet repair. In Mārupe Municipality a septic tank must be emptied at least twice a year, but once a year if water consumption is under 40 m³ a year. A running cistern sends water into the drains continuously, and in a septic tank it ends up together with everything else. Repairing the flush mechanism costs from €35, and the on-site inspection is already included in that price.

Sīpolciems lies outside Jūrmala, 25 km from the centre of Riga. In the prices of both these jobs, getting to Sīpolciems is already included.

Where the waste water goes in Sīpolciems

In the regulator’s 2020 tariff decision, centralised sewerage in Sala parish is named only in Spuņciems, and Sīpolciems is not included in it. The municipal water utility is “Mārupes komunālie pakalpojumi”; whether a particular address is on its network you can ask the company. The earlier provider, “Babītes siltums”, was merged into it on 6 September 2024, and from 1 July 2026 there is a single water and sewerage tariff in the municipality.

If a main network ever reaches the street by the property, Mārupe Municipality binding regulations No. 16/2025 provide co-funding for a private house’s connection if it was built after 1 July 2022. Until then the septic tank or holding tank remains part of the house’s system.

In 2021, 43 people lived in Sīpolciems in an area of 0.64 km², and it is one of the smallest villages in the parish. In a village this small, each house’s water and waste system has to be looked at separately: one has a septic tank, another a holding tank. In the form, write what system your house has and how old it is, if you know.

From the A10 to the northern shore of the lake

The village begins at the A10 and lies about 5.5 km from the parish centre, Spuņciems, and 23 km from the municipal centre, Mārupe. Questions about the register of decentralised sewerage and its rules are handled by the Mārupe Municipality, not by Jūrmala institutions.

The village is only about 4 m above sea level by a shallow lake whose average depth is 0.9 m. For a tank or an inspection chamber at ground level, a tight-fitting lid matters so that rain and surface water do not flow into the system. For the Lake Babīte polder area, LVĢMC has prepared flood maps for sea surges; you can check your own plot on them.

Lake Babīte has been a nature reserve since 1957. Its strict regime zone covers 292 ha, and in the rest of the lake there is a seasonal restriction from 1 January to 30 June. The restriction applies on the lake, but plumbing work in a house or yard is requested through the form as in any other village.

3 m³ per person a month, if the house has no water meter

Mārupe Municipality binding regulations No. 13/2023 set that, without a water meter, the calculation assumes 3 m³ per registered person a month. For a family of three registered people that is 9 m³ a month, or 108 m³ a year. That is well above the 40 m³ limit below which a septic tank only needs emptying once a year.

We do not empty septic tanks. The plumber’s work here is in the house and yard: so that excess water from running fittings does not reach the system, and so that the yard pipe carries waste water all the way to the tank.

Mārupe Municipality binding regulations No. 13/2023 on recording and supervising decentralised sewerage systems. Checked 19 November 2026.

Often asked in Jūrmala

Does Sīpolciems have centralised sewerage?

In the regulator’s 2020 tariff decision, centralised sewerage in Sala parish is named only in Spuņciems, and Sīpolciems is not included in it. For your own address ask “Mārupes komunālie pakalpojumi”. If the house has a septic tank or holding tank, it is a decentralised system, which must be entered in the Mārupe Municipality register.

We have no water meter. Can the septic tank be emptied only once a year?

Under the Mārupe Municipality rules, once a year is enough if consumption is under 40 m³ a year. Without a meter the calculation assumes 3 m³ per registered person a month, so one person is 36 m³ a year (under 40), while two are already 72 m³ (over 40). Ask the municipality for the exact answer for your house.

Can a small job also be requested for Sīpolciems, for example replacing a single tap?

Yes. Tap replacement (your own tap) costs from €25, and the on-site inspection is already included in that price. The trip to Sīpolciems is also within that same price.

Plumber in Bulduri - basements, villas and mixed building

A plumber in Bulduri works in the part of Jūrmala between the Lielupe and Dzintari, where the oldest part between the railway and the river is called Vecbulduri.

South of the railway, one block can hold historic and new detached houses, low-rise buildings and Soviet-era blocks of flats. So before the work it is worth giving not only the address but also the type of building and whether it has a basement.

Along Vienības prospekts from Bulduri station to Vecbulduri

Bulduri railway station was established in 1877 with the opening of the Riga–Tukums railway. Bulduri had been a bathing place for Riga people since the 1830s, in 1833 plots began to be let to summer visitors, and in 1875 there were about 400 summer houses here. Jūrmala’s conditions require owners of historic buildings to inspect the building regularly, especially the roof, the rainwater drainage elements and the foundations. For a plumber this means that water from the roof must go away from the foundations, not into the basement.

Vienības prospekts is the only street in this part with a railway level crossing, so it has become the main street of the district. Along it and to the south, building is distinctly mixed. In a Soviet-era block, work usually concerns the riser and the flat’s branches, while in the villa next door the whole network from the boundary to the tap belongs to the owner.

Vecbulduri is one of the old fishing villages with irregular plots, and “Vecbulduru zvejniekciems un vasarnīcu rajons” is an urban planning monument (No. 6093). In the Lielupe flood risk plan, the Bulduri manor granary and this same Vecbulduri district are named among the cultural objects at risk of flooding. For a house on the river side, the basement and its sewer outlet are the first place to look.

Rainwater in Bulduri goes its own way

Bulduri is one of the places with the largest closed rainwater sewer systems in Jūrmala, together with Kauguri, Dubulti and Majori. Rainwater and land drainage systems have been managed by the Jūrmala municipality since January 2023.

Domestic sewerage is a different network. Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 forbid discharging rain, snowmelt and plot drainage water into it, and when connecting to the networks the owner confirms this in writing (points 13.4, 45.8 and 45.9). In practice this means a basement drainage pump’s rising main must not simply be led into the nearest sewer rodding eye.

A basement pump and a check before buying a villa

Water in a basement. “Repairing a drainage or basement pump” has no fixed price: it depends on pump type, fault and access to the sump. “On-site inspection” (from €40 per visit) applies if the plumber comes, looks and does no other work; if the pump is repaired, the trip is part of the repair price. A basement with a sink, toilet, shower, floor drains or drainage needs automatic shut-offs or gate valves on the sewer (Jūrmala regulations, point 34.4). Pedrollo states that its smallest TOP models (TOP 1–3) pump water down to 14 mm above the floor. BALTA advises keeping basement pumps and heating equipment at least 10–20 cm above the floor against floods; the control unit and pump socket are better raised too (an electrician does that).

Check before buying. “Plumbing check before the purchase” has no fixed price: the price depends on the size of the house, the number of systems and the depth of the check. Jūrmala’s regulations (points 32 and 33) require the user to keep diagrams of the water supply and sewerage with pipe diameters, depth and fittings. When buying a 19th-century summer house or a Soviet-era flat in Bulduri, the first question to the seller is whether such diagrams exist. In Bulduri the trip is included in the check price.

Phase IV pipes in Bulduri streets from 2020

The phase IV contract “Buļļuciems–Bulduri”, with work starting in May 2020, provided for 4.9 km of water main, 6.2 km of sewer and two pumping stations on streets where there was no network. The phase IV design list also included sections of Bulduru prospekts near lines 13–14, 21–22 and 23–25. Whether the network on your street has already been built, Jūrmalas ūdens can say exactly.

Where the street has a network, the service pipe and outlet up to the connection point are built by the user according to the technical conditions, and the meter is then installed by Jūrmalas ūdens (regulations No. 38, point 11). Connecting is not compulsory, but if a house moves to the town sewer, its own decentralised structures for storing waste water must be eliminated afterwards (regulations No. 38, point 15).

Often asked in Jūrmala

Does a basement with a shower in Bulduri need a flood-protection valve?

Jūrmala’s regulations (point 34.4) require automatic shut-offs or gate valves if the basement has sanitary appliances or drainage. For anti-flood valves the standard is EN 13564: the ACO Quatrix is a type 3 device for grey and black waste water, while the Junior (type 5) and Triplex (type 2) suit grey water only.

Do you need a CCTV drain survey before buying a villa in Bulduri?

If a sewer outlet has no as-built documentation, Jūrmala’s regulations (point 57) provide that during reconstruction or when ownership changes, the owner carries out a CCTV survey of the pipeline at their own expense and submits it to Jūrmalas ūdens. How this applies to your purchase, ask Jūrmalas ūdens.

What can be stored in a Bulduri basement that has drainage and a toilet?

In Jūrmala, in basements where sanitary appliances and drainage systems are installed, storing food and material valuables is forbidden (regulations No. 38, point 59). So such a basement is a technical room, not a storeroom.

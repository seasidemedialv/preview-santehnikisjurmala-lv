Plumber in Asari - one house for winter and for summer

A plumber in Asari works between Melluži and Vaivari, 28 km from Riga; on the Lielupe side, the small Viņķi bog, crossed by only a few roads, separates Asari from Valteri.

Asari is an old fishing village with irregular plots and modest buildings that combine a family home with a summer house, so one water system works in two modes in a year.

Full rooms in summer, fewer people in winter

Jūrmalas ūdens notes that the number of residents and guests in Jūrmala changes significantly between summer and winter, and the amount of waste water and the load on the system depend on it. In an Asari house the same happens on a smaller scale: if relatives or guests also live in the house in summer, the water heater and the drains work at full load, while in winter some taps stand untouched.

Standing water is the other side. The trade magazine Water Technology notes that any water heater that has not been used for a long time can start to smell, because odour-causing bacteria multiply in a standing tank. After a long absence, the Health Inspectorate advises running both cold and hot water: open a tap only slightly at first so that no aerosol forms, and within ten minutes gradually increase the flow to full.

A house locked up for winter, and drains after a full summer

Draining a summer house for winter. Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 (point 36) oblige the user to prepare the water supply networks, fittings and meter assembly in their own service part for winter conditions. For a house that is not heated in winter, the water is drained from the pipes and the water heater, and in spring the system is started again. If a house stays heated, some water heaters have an absence mode; for example, Lochinvar’s “Vacation” mode keeps about 10 °C. Draining a summer house for winter and refilling in spring: the price depends on the number of water points, the pump or water heater, and access.

Drain flushing. Jūrmalas ūdens notes that the load on the waste water system also depends on the number of guests, so after a summer with a full house it is time to flush the drains. Flushing the drains with water pressure is from €60 per job, and cleaning the yard drain line from €50 per job, labour price, materials not included. If the house line is flushed and the yard section cleaned in one visit, the work is at least €110. The trip to Asari is already included in both prices.

From the pumping station on Skautu iela to Asari station

On Skautu iela stands a sewage pumping station, painted as an urban art work about the seasons in Jūrmala (by Karīna Volbeta). It was built under the phase IV contract “Asari”, together with 6.5 km of sewer and 5.6 km of water trunk main. The trunk main is new, but the house branch and the yard section can be much older, and a blockage is usually looked for exactly there.

On Zemgales iela, in the former Asari primary school, the Nature Conservation Museum opened in 1983. In Melluži and Asari, phase III built 30 km of water supply and 34 km of sewer network in less than a year, in narrow streets, and the trenches ran between existing utilities. Even on a plot in an irregular fishing village there may be cables and old pipes underground, so before digging find out where they run.

From Asari station until the First World War, strawberries were taken to Riga and St Petersburg. In the storm of 29 July 2024, streets and yards flooded, in Asari too, and streets there were like rivers. In a basement where sanitary appliances or drainage are installed, Jūrmala’s regulations (point 59) forbid storing food and material valuables, and that is a good reason to keep nothing there that water would harm.

Cleaning twice a year under point 34.5

Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 (point 34.5) require the user to clean the sewer networks in their own service zone preventively at least twice a year. For an Asari house with a changing load, a convenient time is before summer and after it, when the system has been through its heaviest load. Jūrmalas ūdens' responsibility ends at the last inspection chamber before the property boundary; unless the building’s documents say otherwise, everything beyond it is yours to look after.

Often asked in Jūrmala

What non-return valve should go in the basement of an Asari house with a shower and toilet?

In a basement with sanitary appliances, Jūrmala’s regulations (point 34.4) require an automatic shut-off or gate valve on the sewer. For ACO anti-flood valves the standard is EN 13564: the Quatrix is a type 3 device for grey and black waste water, while the Junior (type 5) and Triplex (type 2) suit grey water only. If the basement has a toilet, you need a device for black waste water.

In winter in Asari, can a tap before the meter be left open so the pipe does not freeze?

No, without agreement with Jūrmalas ūdens that is forbidden (regulations No. 38, point 51.6). A water pipe can be drained: LBN 222-15 (point 154) requires it to be designed with a fall of at least 0.001 towards the outlet, and on level terrain not less than 0.0005, so a correctly laid pipe can be drained completely.

What must not go down the sewer in Asari?

Wet wipes, even those labelled “flushable”, must go in the rubbish; the Sloka treatment plant separates out 14 tonnes of waste a year. Jūrmala’s regulations forbid discharging acids and substances that can release hazardous gases (point 45.4), and the temperature of waste water must not exceed +40 °C (point 40.4).

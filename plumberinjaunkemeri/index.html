Plumber in Jaunķemeri - a resort in Ķemeri National Park

A plumber in Jaunķemeri works at the western end of Jūrmala on the Gulf of Riga, between Kaugurciems and Bigauņciems; Lake Sloka and the Vecslocene separate this part from Ķemeri.

The whole area up to the beach is a resort territory in Ķemeri National Park, and in 2010 there was no centralised sewerage here. So before any work in Jaunķemeri, first find out where the house sends its waste water.

Along Kolkas iela to the Kaugurrags dolomite in the sea

Kolkas iela runs through Jaunķemeri, part of the P128 Sloka–Talsi road. Detached housing grew up historically around Jaunķemeru ceļš and the Talsi highway, and the territorial plan calls the whole part the “quiet resort”. In 2018, for phase IV, water main and sewer networks were designed on Jaunķemeru iela and Kolkas iela, on the stretch from Jaunķemeru ceļš to Kolkas iela 6. Whether they have already been handed over on your stretch, check with Jūrmalas ūdens (Jūrmala’s water utility) before any yard work.

After 1967 two large balneological sanatoriums were built here: “Jaunķemeri” (1967, now a resort rehabilitation centre) and “Jantarnij bereg” (1972). In the rehabilitation centre, 200-litre baths are filled with warm hydrogen sulphide mineral water, and in 2014 LSM wrote that it is the only place in Latvia where hydrogen sulphide is used for treatment. That is the centre’s medicinal water; in the 2010 data, home taps received water from the Kauguri water supply system.

Near Jaunķemeri a layer of Kaugurrags dolomite reaches out into the sea, and around 2009 the Jaunķemeri beach was one of three Jūrmala beaches with a Blue Flag award. Only the foundations remain of the interwar seaside pavilion, while the “Health Spring” has survived. At the start of 2024, 58 people lived in Jaunķemeri, the fewest of any Jūrmala part. This affects the networks too: in phase IV the networks are brought to the boundary of properties where at least one resident is registered.

Drainage pump and a new outlet in a Jaunķemeri house

Drainage pump repair. For the line “Repairing a drainage or basement pump”, the price is set by the type of pump, the fault and access to the chamber. In the storm of 29 July 2024, Jaunķemeri was among the Jūrmala parts with many reports of damage, and LSM also named Jaunķemeri among the places with flooded streets and yards. The pump’s rising main must not be connected to the domestic sewer: binding regulations No. 38 (points 45.8 and 45.9) forbid sending drainage water there.

New supply and waste points. “Extra water or waste outlet” costs from €35 per point, and “One plumbing point in a new house” from €55 per point; both are labour prices, materials not included. After connecting to the centralised sewer, the regulations (point 15) require your own waste water storage structures to be closed down. So an outlet that used to go to your own tank has to be re-routed to the new yard service pipe.

The pumping station that links Ķemeri with Sloka

In 2010 Jaunķemeri was in the Kauguri water supply system: 51 of 67 residents used centralised water, but there was no centralised sewerage service. In phase I of the water utility programme, a new sewage pumping station was built in Jaunķemeri, which connected the Ķemeri waste water system to the Sloka system. It is the city’s link station; connections for the Jaunķemeri houses themselves depend on the phase IV networks.

In phase IV the network is brought to the plot boundary, and the owner builds the yard section, alone or with a builder. After the work the owner submits to Jūrmalas ūdens an as-built survey plan and a statement that their own well or driven well is not connected to the centralised system. A further statement says that rain and drainage water is not sent into the sewer (binding regulations No. 38, point 13). Connecting is not compulsory.

Jaunķemeri came about after a tram line from Ķemeri to the seaside opened in 1912: Sloka parish set out 150 plots, and by 1914 about 20 summer houses had been built. In 1928 part of it was included in the town of Ķemeri, and in 1959, together with Ķemeri, in Jūrmala. Houses this old may have no water and sewer diagram. Regulations No. 38 (points 32 and 33) require diagrams with pipe diameters, depth, fittings and meter assemblies to be kept, however, and, where there are none, inventory drawings to be drawn up.

When ownership changes or during a rebuild, if the sewer outlet has no as-built documentation, the owner must have a CCTV survey of the pipes done at their own cost and submit it to Jūrmalas ūdens (point 57). Before buying a house in Jaunķemeri it is therefore worth finding out where the outlet runs and whether a diagram is available; a plumbing check before the purchase helps with this.

Often asked in Jūrmala

Do the hydrogen sulphide baths in Jaunķemeri mean such water also comes from the house tap?

They are different waters. The “Jaunķemeri” rehabilitation centre uses hydrogen sulphide mineral water for its baths, while in the 2010 data homes received water from the Kauguri water supply system. If you notice the smell only in hot water, the trade magazine Water Technology explains that the cause is usually in the water heater, where bacteria use the hydrogen produced by the anode. More: water or water heater smells of hydrogen sulphide.

Nobody is registered at our house in Jaunķemeri. Is the phase IV network brought to the boundary?

In phase IV, Jūrmalas ūdens brings the networks to the plot boundary for properties where at least one resident is registered. For a property with no registered resident and for service pipe options, ask Jūrmalas ūdens. Once the service pipe is at the boundary, the yard section can be built.

Does digging the yard in Jaunķemeri need national park approval?

Jaunķemeri lies in Ķemeri National Park. For the neighbouring Ķemeri part, the plan provides that building designs and the choice of building sites are agreed with the park administration. Whether your yard service pipe counts as such a building design, ask the municipality before work; a house’s internal plumbing is usually not tied to it.

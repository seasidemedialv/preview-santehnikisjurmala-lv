Taps and water heaters in Lielupe's hard water

The total hardness of Jūrmalas ūdens water is 3.21–4.25 mmol/l. According to a WHO document, water above 1.8 mmol/l counts as very hard, and the company does not plan to supply moderately hard or soft water. Calcium in the treated water is 85–98 mg/l. In Lielupe this shows in two places: the tap aerator and the water heater tank. Limescale builds up not only in the aerator screen but also in the tap’s ceramic cartridge, which after a time starts to drip or turns stiffly.

Mixer tap replacement and repair. “Tap replacement (your own tap)” costs from €25 per tap. “Stopping a dripping tap” has no fixed price: the price depends on the tap model, the spare part and access. In a low-rise block a tap can be changed after closing the flat’s shut-off valve; in a villa with several bathrooms you first have to find which valve closes which branch.

Water heater installation. “Wall-mounting a water heater” costs from €65 per water heater, labour price, materials not included. In the instructions for its electric water heater, the manufacturer Lochinvar requires the tank to be filled completely and the air let out before the power is connected. If a villa stands empty for longer, the same manufacturer has a “Vacation” mode that keeps about 10 °C and protects the tank from freezing. A water heater and two taps together come to at least €115, and the trip to Lielupe is included in that sum.

From Lielupe station to Raga Dune

The Lielupe railway station building was built in 1913. The place used to be called Buļļu gals, and at the end of the 19th century the summer-house colonies built in the farthest forest plots of the Bulduri manor were called “America” and “Australia”. With an older house it is worth checking before the work whether the water supply diagram has survived. Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 (points 32 and 33) require the user to keep diagrams of their networks with pipe diameters, depth and meter assemblies, and, if there are none, to draw up inventory drawings. With the diagram in hand the plumber sees where the branches run, and fewer walls need opening.

The “Lielupe” tennis centre is at Oskara Kalpaka prospekts 16. Around Meža prospekts and Bulduru prospekts, pine-park building areas are set, where only large detached houses on large plots are allowed. Jūrmalas ūdens' responsibility for the water supply ends at the last gate valve before the property boundary, and for the sewer at the last inspection chamber, so the whole long stretch up to the villa belongs to the owner.

To the north is the nature park “Raga kāpa” with wooded dunes and old pine stands. In phase IV, new water and sewer networks in Lielupe and Stirnurags were put into operation, as well as a sewage pumping station on Zvejas iela. For a house that connects to these networks, the service pipe and outlet up to the connection point are built by the user according to the technical conditions of Jūrmalas ūdens (regulations No. 38, point 11), and the meter is installed afterwards by the company.

Monument of national importance No. 6083 and the Lielupe villas

Lielupe belongs to the urban planning monument of national importance “Dubultu–Majoru–Dzintaru–Bulduru–Lielupes vasarnīcu rajons” (No. 6083). Article 21 of the law “On Protection of Cultural Monuments” provides that cultural monuments of national importance may be repaired only with the written permission of the National Heritage Board. Whether this applies to your building and to the particular job, ask the National Heritage Board before the work, especially if a new outlet has to be taken through the facade.

Law “On Protection of Cultural Monuments”, Article 21 (likumi.lv, id 72551), checked on 19 November 2026.

Where Lielupe's waste water goes

Since 1998, waste water from Dubulti–Lielupe has been pumped through an 11 km line to the Daugavgrīva treatment plant in Riga. The Lielupe system collects about 25% of the city’s waste water, while about 73% reaches the Sloka plant. Lielupe also has the site of a reserve waste water treatment plant. In the first phase of water management, the 2001–2004 project, the water supply on the Lielupe–Dzintari section was reconstructed.

What is poured down the sink in a house travels on through pumps and a rising main. Jūrmala’s regulations (point 40.4) set that waste water discharged into the sewer must not exceed +40 °C, and its pH must be 6.8–8.0. So when draining a water heater, the hot water is first left to cool, rather than letting the whole tank out into the drain at once.

Often asked in Jūrmala

Will the water in Lielupe become softer?

Since 2023 Jūrmalas ūdens has been gradually changing its nanofiltration membranes to reduce hardness, and in 2024 it was 3.5–4.0 mmol/l. The company does not plan to supply moderately hard or soft water, so limescale in taps and water heaters in Lielupe remains an everyday matter.

Does the pumping station on Zvejas iela mean a house needs its own pump?

No, not automatically. The pumping station on Zvejas iela is part of the Jūrmalas ūdens network. Your own pumping unit is usually considered if some appliance, for example in a basement, is below the street sewer; in that case Saniflo, for example, requires a loop above street level in the rising main to stop backflow.

Does a phase I water supply in Lielupe mean the pipes in the house are new too?

No. In the 2001–2004 project the street water supply on the Lielupe–Dzintari section was reconstructed. The pipes beyond the last gate valve before the property boundary belong to the owner, and their age is that of the house itself or of its last renovation.

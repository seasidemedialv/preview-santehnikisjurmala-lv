The detached-house villages Lielpriede and Silarāji belong to Piņķi, so the work here often concerns private house plumbing: installation from scratch and the service pipe to the street network.

The Piņķi water main: from “Babītes siltums” to the municipal company

“Babītes siltums”, which served Piņķi, was registered in Piņķi itself. In September 2024 it was merged into “Mārupes komunālie pakalpojumi”, and this company now provides water and sewerage services. Connection documents from before 2024 may have been issued under the old name, and they are still useful when looking for where your service pipe runs.

In 2020 Piņķi village was included in the regulator-approved centralised sewerage tariff, together with Babīte village, Priežciems and Spuņciems. So the village has a network to connect to. For a particular house the question is different: whether the main pipe is already in your own street, and how many metres it is from there to the house wall.

By population, Piņķi was the fourth largest village in Latvia in 2024, and the village covers 6.75 km². In a village this large the houses are of different ages and the jobs differ: in one an old service pipe has to be replaced, in another all the plumbing has to be built from nothing.

From the A5 and A10 junction to the detached-house villages

Piņķi is 12 km from the municipal centre, Mārupe. Water and sewerage here are provided by “Mārupes komunālie pakalpojumi”, and in the municipality where this company provides the service there is one tariff. Rules on connection and decentralised sewerage in Piņķi are found in the Mārupe Municipality binding regulations.

The village’s public centre is around Babīte secondary school, Babīte music school, Babīte sports complex, the Latvian International School and the ice rink. These buildings have their own managers. For a private house owner the nearer question is a different one: where their own system ends and the network begins.

Lielpriede and Silarāji are detached-house villages that belong to Piņķi. In a detached house everything from the yard inspection chamber to the last tap is one owner’s system: water pipes, drains, water heater and often a heating loop too. So it is worth keeping a pipe diagram here, or at least photographs from the time when the walls were still open.

A holding tank in Piņķi until the house is connected to the network

A house that is not yet connected to centralised sewerage must follow Mārupe Municipality binding regulations No. 13/2023. A septic tank must be emptied at least twice a year, while the emptying frequency of a holding tank is calculated from water consumption and the tank volume.

For plumbing this means: every running toilet or dripping tap raises consumption and with it the number of emptyings. We do not empty tanks, but repairing the flush mechanism costs from €35, and the on-site inspection is already included in that price.

A septic tank and a holding tank follow different regimes: for a septic tank the frequency is fixed, for a holding tank it is calculated. If you do not know which of them is in your yard, the plumber can tell you at the inspection whether the tank is sealed or has an overflow to a filtration field.

Mārupe Municipality binding regulations No. 13/2023 on recording and supervising decentralised sewerage systems. Checked 19 November 2026.

A new build or a service pipe: two jobs for Piņķi detached houses

Plumbing a new house. Lielpriede and Silarāji are detached-house villages, and in a new house the plumbing is built point by point: each sink, shower, toilet or washing machine is one water and waste point. One plumbing point in a new house costs from €55, and an Extra water or waste outlet from €35. For a house with 8 points, at the per-point price that is at least €440 of labour, materials not included.

Connection to the networks. If the main water pipe or sewer is in the street by the house, Mārupe Municipality may co-fund building the connection, including the technical documentation. The price of a yard connection to the town network depends on length, depth, restoring the surface and permits. “On-site inspection” (from €40) is the price if the plumber comes, inspects and does no other work.

Both jobs share one technical requirement from the building code LBN 222-15. A water pipe is designed with a fall of at least 0.001 towards the drain-off end, and on level ground not less than 0.0005, so that the pipe can be emptied completely (point 154). The test pressure before commissioning is stated in the building design, and the standard LVS EN 805:2022 can be used for the tests (point 161). That is why a new pipe is pressure-tested before it is backfilled or closed into a wall.

Piņķi lies outside Jūrmala, and Jūrmala is to the west of the village. Even so, getting to Piņķi is included in the labour price, just as in Jūrmala.

Often asked in Jūrmala

Our house is in Silarāji. Should we choose Piņķi in the form?

Yes. The detached-house villages Lielpriede and Silarāji belong to Piņķi. Choose Piņķi and write the village name in the description.

Can a holding tank in Piņķi be emptied less often if few people live in the house?

Mārupe Municipality binding regulations No. 13/2023 link the emptying frequency of a holding tank to water consumption and tank volume, so lower consumption usually means less frequent emptying. Ask the municipality for the exact frequency for your house.

Does the municipality's co-funding for a connection also cover the design?

Mārupe Municipality binding regulations No. 16/2025 provide that co-funding may also include technical documentation, within the overall maximum. Ask the municipality for the amount and the procedure; the yard service pipe work can be requested through the form.

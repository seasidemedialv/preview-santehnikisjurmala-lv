Plumber in Sala parish - from Spuņciems to Gātciems and Kaģi

A plumber in Sala parish (Salas pagasts) works in the western part of Mārupe Municipality on both banks of the Lielupe, between Babīte parish, Jūrmala, and Slampe and Valgunde parishes.

In the 2020 tariff decision, centralised water supply and sewerage in the parish is named only in Spuņciems, so in the other villages you need to find out before work whether the house is connected to the network or has its own system.

From Spuņciems along the Lake Babīte polders

The parish centre is Spuņciems, and the other larger villages are Gātciems, Sīpolciems, Straupciems, Pavasari and Kaģi. The parish covers 76.3 km² in total, of which 20.3 km² is water. The water supply and drainage arrangement can differ from village to village, so in the form write the village name and whether the house has a network connection, a septic tank or a holding tank. The area includes Ķemeri National Park and Lake Babīte, both Natura 2000 sites. If you plan to dig a new yard route near their boundaries, ask the municipality about the conditions first.

Lake Babīte is a lagoon-type lake of 25.6 km² with an average depth of only 0.9 m and a silt layer up to 5 m. On its eastern and southern shore the Babīte, Dzilnupe, Trenči and Gātupe polders have been built. In a polder, pumping stations keep the water level, while a house’s basement or drainage pump is the owner’s own equipment.

In 2026 ZMNĪ completed the reconstruction of the Jāņupīte polder pumping station in Sala parish: it has new automatically controlled pumps, water level monitoring and backup generators. A house pump usually has no backup power, and in a power cut it stops. Saniflo states for its lifting units that connected appliances must not be used during a power cut.

A pump and a yard pipe: work in Sala parish houses

Drainage pump repair. The price of repairing a drainage or basement pump depends on the pump type, the fault and access to the chamber. “On-site inspection” (from €40) is the price if the plumber comes, inspects and does not repair the pump; if it is repaired, getting there is included in the repair price. The pump model matters: for example, Pedrollo TOP drainage pumps are intended for clean water without abrasive particles, and solid particles may be up to 10 mm. If a pump draws sand or silt in the chamber, it wears out sooner.

Cleaning the yard drain line. The Mārupe Municipality rules on decentralised sewerage apply to all villages of the municipality: a septic tank must be emptied at least twice a year, and the system must be registered with the municipality. The pipe from the house to the septic tank is the same yard drain. If it is blocked, the water does not drain away even when the septic tank is empty. Cleaning the yard drain line costs from €50; we do not pump out septic tanks.

A drainage pump and a sewage pump are different equipment: the first is meant for clean water, the second for waste water. After a power cut it is worth checking the float and the valves of a basement lifting unit. If the unit then switches on and does not stop, or hums without flow, sewage pump repair helps.

Sala parish lies outside Jūrmala, and its villages are scattered on both banks of the Lielupe. Even so, getting to any of them is included in the labour price.

Tariffs and networks in Sala parish since 2020

In Sala parish, water and sewerage tariffs had not been reviewed since 2020 until a single Mārupe Municipality tariff was introduced. From 1 July 2026, “Mārupes komunālie pakalpojumi” applies one tariff everywhere it provides the service.

The parish’s history explains why it is one municipality: in 1990 Gāte village was reorganised into Sala parish, in 2009 it was included in Babīte Municipality, and in 2021 Babīte Municipality was joined to Mārupe Municipality. That is why Mārupe Municipality binding regulations now apply to Sala parish homes.

If the main network reaches the street by the house, Mārupe Municipality binding regulations No. 16/2025 provide co-funding for building a private house’s connection, if the connection was built after 1 July 2022. After connecting, the yard sewer no longer goes to a septic tank but to the street network.

Mārupe Municipality binding regulations No. 13/2023 (decentralised sewerage) and No. 16/2025 (co-funding for a connection). Checked 19 November 2026.

Often asked in Jūrmala

The house in Gātciems has a septic tank. Does it have to be registered with the municipality?

Mārupe Municipality binding regulations No. 13/2023 apply to all villages of the municipality: a decentralised system must be entered in the municipality’s register, and a septic tank must be emptied at least twice a year. Ask the municipality how to register.

Does the Jāņupīte polder pumping station also serve my house's drainage?

No, it is a different system. The polder pumping station was rebuilt by ZMNĪ, and it keeps the polder’s water level. A house’s drainage or basement pump is the owner’s equipment, and its repair can be requested through the form.

Our house is on the other bank of the Lielupe, but it is still in Sala parish. Can we request work?

Yes. Sala parish lies on both banks of the Lielupe. In the form choose Sala parish and write the village name in the description.

Plumber in Bigauņciems - summer houses and homes by the Siliņupe

Bigauņciems is a village of Lapmežciems parish in Tukums Municipality by the P128 road, between the Gulf of Riga and the Vecslocenes, and along the Siliņupe it has in effect merged with Lapmežciems.

Since the 19th century it has also been a place for summer houses, and in a house lived in only in summer, the water stands in the water heater and pipes for months.

The Bigauņciems year, from the Kupskalni beach to a locked-up summer house

Bigauņciems became a summer house place in 1824, when stagecoach service from Riga opened. The Lapmežciems-Bigauņciems or Kupskalni beach is a bathing place laid out by the municipality. When there are guests in the house in summer, it is not only the litres of the water heater that decide, but also how the hot water is used. Rinnai explains that a 50 l tank can cover even 75 l of daily use if it is used in three draws of 25 l. If the tank runs out in the evening, the answer is not always a larger water heater; sometimes a gap between showers is enough.

If the house is locked up in autumn, the water stays standing in the water heater. The trade magazine Water Technology notes that any water heater left unused for a long time can start to smell, because odour bacteria multiply in a standing tank. In spring, after a long absence, the Health Inspectorate recommends running both cold and hot water: open the tap a little at first so that no aerosol forms, and within ten minutes raise the flow to full.

But Bigauņciems is not only summer houses. The population grew from 263 in 1970 to 485 in 2011, and in 2025, 442 people lived here. A house lived in all year does not need winterising, but the state of the water heater and the heating before winter is the same question. For such a house a seasonal plumbing service is suitable; Seasonal plumbing service: the price depends on the size of the property, the number of points to check and the defects found.

Along Vecais Ķemeru ceļš to the mouth of the Siliņupe

In 1840 a road across the bog to Ķemeri was built, now called Vecais Ķemeru ceļš. Jaunķemeri is right here, but Bigauņciems is in Tukums Municipality, and Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 do not apply here. A house with its own septic tank follows the Tukums Municipality procedure: a septic tank must be emptied at least once a year.

Bigauņciems grew up on the bank of the Siliņupe as a fishing village. Because of its landscape the Latvian feature films “Zvejnieka dēls” and “Nāves ēnā” were shot here, and the Tukums Municipality coastal plan sets a historic development area in Bigauņciems. If the house is an old wooden building, soldering pipes with a flame in it is fire-hazardous work: the Fire Safety Regulations forbid heating hollow structures without checking their contents (point 314.1.5).

At the mouth of the Siliņupe in the sea begins the Kupskalni nature trail. Across the river to the north-west is Lapmežciems, and both villages are in one parish. For houses connected to the network the tariffs are therefore the same: the Public Utilities Commission decision of 29 October 2026 on “Tukuma ūdens” applies to the whole Lapmežciems parish.

Winterising and water heater cleaning in a Bigauņciems summer house

Summer house winterising. The building code LBN 003-19 states that sandy soils freeze deeper than clayey ones, and their standard depth is found by multiplying the value for clayey soil by 1.2. A pipe that lies shallow in the yard or runs under an unheated floor must be drained for winter. Draining a summer house for winter and refilling in spring: the price depends on the number of water points, on whether the house has a pump or water heater, and on access.

Water heater cleaning. The manufacturer Lochinvar explains that the harmless bacteria usually present in tap water can multiply in a water heater and cause a rotten-egg smell, and sometimes replacing the standard anode with a special zinc anode helps. A water heater that has stood all winter is worth cleaning in spring, and its anode inspected. Water heater clean-out with an anode check: from €75 per water heater, labour price, materials not included.

Lochinvar also warns that the anode must not be removed from the water heater altogether. To reduce the smell the tank can be heated to 60 °C, but above 49 °C the scalding risk rises, so the manufacturer recommends thermostatic mixing valves at the draw-off points. In a summer house where children also shower, this valve is worth its place.

Bigauņciems is 46 km from Riga and outside the Jūrmala boundary. Even so, getting to Bigauņciems is already included in the water heater cleaning price.

Often asked in Jūrmala

We only come to Bigauņciems in summer. What should be done with the water heater from October to May?

There are two ways. The water heater can be drained together with the whole system, or left switched on in absence mode, if the model has one. For example, the “Vacation” mode of a Lochinvar water heater keeps about 10 °C and protects the tank from freezing. Whether your water heater has a similar mode is written in its instructions.

The house is right by the Siliņupe. What should I write in the form: Bigauņciems or Lapmežciems?

The Siliņupe is the boundary between the two villages, but they have in effect merged and both are in Lapmežciems parish. Choose the village written in your address. What helps the plumber more is knowing whether the house has a network connection or its own tank.

Six people live in the house in summer. Is a larger water heater better?

Not always. Hamworthy recommends sizing a hot water system by the greatest simultaneous load and the recovery time: the larger the tank, the longer it takes to heat up again, but a tank that is too small will not meet demand. In a Bigauņciems summer house this means counting how many showers happen one after another in the evening after the beach.

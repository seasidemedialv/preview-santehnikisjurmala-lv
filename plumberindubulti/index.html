Plumber in Dubulti - risers in the blocks and timber verandas

A plumber in Dubulti works where the Lielupe comes within only about 300 m of the sea; it is the narrowest place in Jūrmala between the river and the sea.

On this narrow strip, 1970s blocks and single-storey timber summer houses stand side by side, so within one street you can need both a riser section in a block of flats and careful work in a timber wall.

From the Lutheran church by the station to Liedaga iela

Opposite Dubulti station stands the Art Nouveau Lutheran church (1907–1909, architect W. Bockslaff). Dubulti also has the second-lift pumping station with reservoirs, built in phase I of the water management project. It maintains the pressure in the town network, so if a weak flow is only in one flat or house, the cause is usually to be found inside: a blocked strainer at the meter, an unadjusted pressure-reducing valve or a tap aerator full of limescale.

At Strēlnieku prospekts 26 stands the Orthodox Church of St Prince Vladimir, built in 1896, and between the railway and Strēlnieku prospekts, blocks of multi-storey residential buildings were put up in the 1970s. In such houses the water and sewer risers usually run through all the floors in one shaft. Replacing a riser on one floor therefore also affects the neighbours above and below your flat, and shutting off water in the riser is usually agreed with the building manager.

On the river side of Dubultu prospekts is a separate micro-district of blocks of flats, where the buildings are grouped around shared yards. In such yards the outlets from several entrances can flow together into shared chambers, and then a blockage in one section can stop the drains in several stairwells. At Liedaga iela 7, 18 and 20, in turn, stand the typical Dubulti single-storey timber summer houses with verandas and wood carving. In such a house a pipe can run under the floor or in a wall cavity, and the fire safety regulations (point 314.1.5) forbid soldering and heating hollow structures without making sure what is inside.

Along an 11 km line to Daugavgrīva: where Dubulti's drains go

Dubulti is in the Lielupe sewer system. Since 1998 the waste water of Dubulti and Lielupe has been pumped through an 11 km line to the Daugavgrīva treatment plant in Riga. On the way the waste water passes through pumping stations, so a problem in a street line or a station is a job for the Jūrmalas ūdens emergency service: the company asks you to report any sewer leak, even in a ditch. If the blockage is only in your own house line, that is a plumber’s job.

The phase IV contract “Dubulti-Pumpuri”, signed on 23 March 2020, was the first of the 2020 phase IV contracts to be put into operation. In it, 10.12 km of water main, 13.9 km of sewer and 6 pumping stations were built in Dubulti, Jaundubulti and Pumpuri. The networks are built up to the property boundary, but the section in the yard is the owner’s job, and Jūrmalas ūdens issues technical conditions free of charge within the project area. The yard part can be built as a connection to the town networks.

Risers in the blocks and drains in shared yards

Riser replacement. In houses between the railway and Strēlnieku prospekts, a riser can be replaced floor by floor, not in the whole house at once. Replacing a water riser on one floor is from €120 per floor, and replacing a soil stack on one floor is also from €120 per floor. If both the water riser and the soil stack are replaced on one floor, the work is at least €240, labour price, materials not included. The trip to Dubulti is part of these labour prices.

Drain cleaning. In a micro-district around shared yards, a blockage may be not in the flat but in the yard section between the building and the chamber. Clearing a blocked indoor drain is from €40 per job, and cleaning the yard drain line from €50 per job. Jūrmalas ūdens' responsibility for the sewer ends at the last inspection chamber before the property boundary, so the yard networks and everything in the house usually belong to the owners and are repaired at their expense, unless the building’s documents say otherwise.

Roof water is not for domestic sewerage

Dubulti is one of the places with the largest closed rainwater sewer systems in Jūrmala, and it is separate from the domestic drains. Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 forbid discharging rain, snowmelt and plot drainage water into the centralised sewer (points 45.8 and 45.9), and when connecting to the networks the owner confirms this in writing (point 13.4). If a timber summer house’s downpipe or yard floor drain is connected to the domestic sewer, the connection is rebuilt so that rainwater no longer gets into it.

Checked on 19 November 2026: Jūrmala City Council binding regulations No. 38 of 25 July 2024, points 13.4, 45.8 and 45.9.

Often asked in Jūrmala

In a 1970s house in Dubulti, can a riser be replaced only on your own flat's floor?

Yes. The section that runs through your flat is replaced and joined to the existing riser above and below it. During the work the water in the riser is shut off, so the time is usually agreed with the building manager and the neighbours. Replacing a water riser on one floor is from €120 per floor, labour price, materials not included.

Why does soldering in a timber summer house on the Liedaga iela side need extra care?

The fire safety regulations count work with an open flame or sparks as fire-hazardous: the work area within a 5 m radius must be cleared of combustible materials or the materials protected. A temporary work site must be supervised for at least four hours after the work ends (point 306). In a timber house with verandas, joints without a flame, such as press-fit or screwed ones, are therefore often chosen.

Who looks after the street rainwater chambers in Dubulti?

Rainwater sewerage in Jūrmala is overseen by the Engineering Structures Division of the municipality’s Development Department, not by the domestic sewerage operator. Since January 2023 the municipality has managed rainwater and land drainage systems. If a street rainwater chamber is blocked, report it to the municipality; if a house drain line is blocked, request a plumber through the form.

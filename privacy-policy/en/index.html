Privacy policy

This page explains how this website processes the personal data you send with the request form, and what rights you have.

Page updated on 20 November 2026.

Who processes the data

The website santehnikisjurmala.lv is run by the brand “Santehniķis Jūrmalā” (“we”). We send the company’s legal name and details on request: write it in the “Additional information” field of the request form on the contact page.

What data you enter in the form

  • name;
  • phone number;
  • address and district (a part of Jūrmala or a place around Jūrmala);
  • type of problem and urgency;
  • additional information that you write yourself.

The time of the request and the name of the website are sent with it. The website has no user accounts and no payments. Please do not write data in the form that the job does not need, for example health data.

The website’s hosting service Cloudflare processes technical data, for example the IP address, browser information and the time of the request, for security and operation.

What the data is for

We use the data only to get in touch with you about your request: to clarify the job and its price, to agree on a time for the visit and to carry out the work. The basis is your request before a contract is made and the performance of the contract (Article 6(1)(b) of the General Data Protection Regulation).

We do not sell the data, publish it or use it for advertising.

Where the data goes

  • The form sends the data to the website’s own form address, which runs on the Cloudflare network. There we keep a backup copy of the request, so that it is not lost if sending does not succeed at once.
  • From there the request goes to our request-handling tool Activepieces, which passes it on so that we can contact you.
  • We contact you by phone or WhatsApp on the number you gave; after your request you may also receive an automatic WhatsApp message confirming that it was received.
  • The plumber who contacts you and does the work receives the request data, only as much as the work needs.
  • We pass data to public authorities only when the law requires it.

Some service providers may also process data outside the European Economic Area. In that case the transfer relies on the data protection safeguards set out in Chapter V of the Regulation, for example the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep the data

The backup copy at the form address deletes itself after 90 days. We keep the rest of the request data as long as it is needed to handle the request, and then delete it, unless the law requires it to be kept, for example for accounting documents.

Your rights

  • to access your data and receive a copy of it;
  • to correct inaccurate data;
  • to ask for the data to be deleted;
  • to ask to restrict the processing or to object to it;
  • to receive your data in a portable form.

Send your request with the form on the contact page, writing what you want in the “Additional information” field. If you believe your data has been processed incorrectly, you can lodge a complaint with the Data State Inspectorate (Datu valsts inspekcija).

Cookies, statistics and fonts

This website does not use cookies or visitor statistics tools. Fonts are loaded from the website itself, not from Google Fonts or another external server.

Changes

If the data processing changes, we update this page and its date.

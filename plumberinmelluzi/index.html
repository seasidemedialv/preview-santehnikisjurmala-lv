Plumber in Melluži - new branches and winter heating

A plumber in Melluži works between Pumpuri and Asari, where the built-up area stretches in a wedge from the station almost to the bank of the Lielupe.

A considerable part of Jūrmala’s permanent residents live in Melluži, so sewerage and heating here work all year, not only in summer.

From Mellužu prospekts in a wedge towards the Lielupe

On both sides of Mellužu prospekts and Asaru prospekts, the plan also allows shops and services on the street frontage. If a café or kitchen is set up in such a space, Jūrmala’s regulations (point 66) require separate sewerage for greasy waste water and a grease trap. That is planned already when choosing where the sinks and the dishwasher will stand.

The Melluži sewage pumping station and rising main were among the first large Jūrmala sewer facilities, built in the late 1960s and early 1970s. Later, in Melluži and Asari, the sewer was in places laid 5–6 m deep near the pumping stations, and building work was hindered by a high groundwater level. For a house outlet this means groundwater may appear in a yard trench already while digging, and the pipe bedding must then be made more carefully.

On the Lielupe side, Melluži borders Valteri. On the Melluži-Valteri section, the second phase built 15.5 km of water main with 328 building connections and 13.8 km of sewer with 369 building connections. If your house is one of them, the connection in the street has been built, but the section from the boundary to the house, and all that is inside the house, are the owner’s responsibility.

After connecting, the old holding tank has to be eliminated

On 30 April 2015 the phase III contract for Melluži and Asari was signed: more than 50 km of networks, 950 water branches and 1,122 sewer branches. Water supply in this district had already been partly provided before that; so the contract has more sewer branches. Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 (point 15) provide that after connecting to centralised sewerage the owner must eliminate their own decentralised sewerage structures used for storing waste water. Emptying the tank is a sewage tanker operator’s job; a plumber’s job is to redirect the house outlets to the new branch.

Checked on 19 November 2026: Jūrmala City Council binding regulations No. 38 of 25 July 2024, points 15 and 66.

A former summer-house district lived in all year

Melluži and Asari were a quiet place to rent summer houses for teachers and cultural workers; Emīls Dārziņš, Rūdolfs Blaumanis and Jānis Rozentāls lived here. Nowadays a considerable part of Jūrmala’s permanent residents live in Melluži. A house once built for summer can be heated all winter, and its pipes then work in conditions they were not originally meant for.

In winter the weak spot is a pipe in an unheated room. In January 2024 one Jūrmala block of flats had no cold water for 10 days, because part of the frozen water pipe ran under the floor of an unheated, uninhabited flat. In summer the test is a downpour: in the storm of 29 July 2024 streets in Melluži were like rivers, and then in a basement with a shower or toilet a non-return valve helps. After such a downpour, first check whether the drains in the house run freely and whether the water in the yard inspection chamber has risen to the level of the outlet. If it has, do not use the washing machine or the shower until the level drops.

An outlet to the branch and radiators in a winter house

Replacing drain pipes. When a house outlet is redirected from the old tank to the phase III branch, the old section may have to be replaced completely. Replacing waste pipe is from €25/m, labour price, materials not included, and a 15 m section from the house to the branch is at least €375. The trip to Melluži is already included in this price.

Radiator replacement. In a house built for summer and now lived in all year, the heating circuit may have been extended in parts, and the radiators are of different ages. Changing one radiator is from €70 per radiator, and changing four radiators is at least €280 of work. If a radiator simply stays cold, start with radiator repair: Radiator repair or bleeding, the price depends on the fault, the type of system and access.

Often asked in Jūrmala

How deep does the town sewer lie in Melluži?

Near the pumping stations in Melluži and Asari the sewer has been laid in places 5–6 m deep, and building work was hindered by a high groundwater level. At your plot boundary the depth of the branch may be different; ask Jūrmalas ūdens for exact information before digging.

Which thermostat setting should be left in a room that no one lives in during winter?

On an Oventrop thermostatic head, position 0 shuts the valve, while the snowflake position opens it if the room cools below 6 °C; the scale 1–5 corresponds to roughly 12, 16, 20, 24 and 28 °C. In an unoccupied room with water pipes, the snowflake protects against frost, while 0 does not. On the heads of other manufacturers the scale may differ.

In the Melluži summer-house districts, does changing a radiator need permission?

Melluži belongs to the monument of national importance “Vaivaru-Asaru-Mellužu-Pumpuru-Jaundubultu vasarnīcu rajons” and the monument of local importance “Vaivaru-Asaru-Mellužu vasarnīcu kvartāli”. In the area of a monument of local importance, repair and reconstruction that can change a building may be carried out only with the written permission of the Jūrmala Cultural Space and Environmental Design Centre. Whether changing a radiator in its existing place needs permission, ask the centre before the work, especially in a historic interior or a building that is itself a monument.

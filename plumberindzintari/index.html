Plumber in Dzintari - from the street connection to the water heater

A plumber in Dzintari works between Bulduri and Majori, where quarters of villas stretch from the Lielupe to the sea and where the Jūrmalas ūdens water treatment station is also found.

The 2020 contract brought water supply and sewerage to streets that had none, so one of the jobs here is the yard connection. The other comes from the water itself: it is hard, and in a water heater it stays behind as limescale.

Dzintari streets after the contract of 30 March 2020

The phase IV contract “Dzintari and Majori” was signed on 30 March 2020. It provided for 11.6 km of water main, 12.1 km of sewer and one pumping station on streets where there was no network. The networks were built up to the property boundary, and the yard networks are the owner’s responsibility.

Where networks were built on a Dzintari street, the water connection up to the property boundary is often visible as a pipe with a plug above ground, and the sewer connection as an inspection chamber cover opposite the property. These two points are the start of the yard job: from the plug to the house’s incoming supply, and from the house outlet to the cover. A plug by the fence is not yet a connection: connecting is not compulsory. The built yard section is shown to a Jūrmalas ūdens specialist in the open trench before backfilling.

The connection at the plug and water heater cleaning

Connecting to the networks. “Yard connection to the town network” has no fixed price: the price depends on the length, the depth, restoring the surface and permits. Jūrmalas ūdens describes a connection in six steps: a request for technical conditions, the building design, construction, meter installation, an opinion on readiness and a contract. The company prepares the technical conditions in 10 working days free of charge, and they are valid for two years.

Water heater cleaning. “Water heater clean-out with an anode check” costs from €75 per water heater, labour price, materials not included. The Dzintari station has iron removal and nanofiltration to take out sulphates, but the hardness in the treated water in 2024 was 3.5–4.0 mmol/l, and the calcium stays at the bottom of the tank. Jūrmalas ūdens does not add chlorine to drinking water, and total iron in the treated water is 0.02–0.07 mg/l. When a water heater is cleaned, you see how much sediment has built up and what condition the anode is in. In a villa with two water heaters it is at least €150, and the trip to Dzintari is already in that sum.

From the water tower to the concert hall on Turaidas iela

Dzintari has the Jūrmalas ūdens water treatment station and a water tower, put into operation in the late 1960s and early 1970s. In 2024 the Dzintari artesian well had a major borehole renovation. The water that comes from a tap in Dzintari houses starts in a deep borehole, and before the network iron and part of the sulphates are removed from it.

Dzintari grew up on the land of the Bulduri manor as a district of prestigious detached houses, and the first plots were entered in the land register in 1871. After 1920 mainly standard-design family houses were built, and after the Second World War many large sanatoria and rest homes. For tourist accommodation, the ECDC recommends keeping hot water circulating through the whole system at 50–60 °C and cold water below 20 °C; this also suits a large building with long hot water pipes.

The Dzintari Concert Hall at Turaidas iela 1 opened in 1936, and the summer hall was added in 1960. In an LSM report on the storm of 29 July 2024, Dzintari is named among the places where streets and yards flooded. Jūrmala’s regulations (point 61) require the user to protect their basements from flooding by water and waste water, by keeping the networks watertight in their own zone of responsibility.

Timber villas in monument district No. 6084 and fire safety

“Dzintaru–Bulduru vasarnīcu kvartāli” is a separate urban planning monument (No. 6084). The Morberg summer house at Dzintaru prospekts 52/54 (1883) is a monument of neo-Gothic timber architecture, restored by the University of Latvia in 2007–2008. In a timber villa, pipework with soldering is fire-hazardous work.

The fire safety regulations count work with an open flame or sparks as fire-hazardous work, and the work area within a 5 m radius must be cleared of combustible materials or the materials protected. A temporary work site must be supervised for at least four hours after the work ends, and hollow structures must not be heated without making sure what is inside (points 306, 310, 311 and 314.1.5). In a timber wall or an intermediate floor, this means choosing a joint without a flame wherever possible.

Fire Safety Regulations (likumi.lv, id 281646), points 2.13, 306, 310, 311, 314.1.5, checked on 19 November 2026.

Often asked in Jūrmala

What does a pipe with a plug by my Dzintari fence mean?

It is usually the phase IV water connection up to the property boundary, while the inspection chamber opposite the property is the sewer connection. The next step is a request to Jūrmalas ūdens for technical conditions, and only after them does digging in the yard begin.

Does nanofiltration at the Dzintari station mean the water heater no longer needs cleaning?

No. The total hardness of Jūrmalas ūdens water is 3.21–4.25 mmol/l, and according to a WHO document, water above 1.8 mmol/l counts as very hard. Jūrmalas ūdens does not plan to supply moderately hard or soft water, so limescale builds up in a water heater even after the membrane replacement.

Can the old well in a Dzintari villa stay if I connect to the town water supply?

If a building has two water sources, its own well and the town water supply, Jūrmala’s regulations (point 22) require an air gap of at least 50 mm or a special non-return valve at the joint. After construction the owner also confirms that their own well is not connected to the centralised system.

Plumber in Buļļuciems - risers and yard connections by the river mouth

A plumber in Buļļuciems works at the eastern end of Jūrmala, where the Lielupe flows into the Gulf of Riga and where a fishing village grew up.

The street water supply and sewerage here are from phase IV, and they were built only up to the red line, the street boundary. So the service pipe from the street to the house and the house outlet to the street are the owner’s job.

The first phase IV section was in Buļļuciems

In the 2012 city plan there were no centralised water supply and sewer networks in Buļļuciems. On 7 July 2016 Jūrmalas ūdens (Jūrmala’s water utility) signed a contract for networks on Vikingu, Matrožu, Lašu and Jūrnieku iela, and this was the first section of the whole of phase IV. Until the networks were handed over, houses on these streets managed with their own systems, so an old holding tank or septic tank that is no longer needed after connecting may be left in the yard.

On the Buļļuciems section, 3.5 km of water main, 2.6 km of sewer, 0.9 km of rising main and two sewage pumping stations were built. The network was put into operation on 16 June 2017. On 31 March 2020 the next contract, “Buļļuciems–Bulduri”, was signed for the streets where there had been no network before.

The technical conditions for a connection from Jūrmalas ūdens are free of charge, are prepared in 10 working days, and are valid for two years from registration. After connecting to centralised sewerage, Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 (point 15) require you to remove your own decentralised structures in which waste water was stored until then.

Two pumping stations and 0.9 km of rising main mean that the waste water of Buļļuciems is pumped for part of its way. On the house side it matters that the system is closed: if the user’s network lacks shut-off fittings, the rodding eyes are not closed airtight or non-return valves are not installed, the user is responsible for any consequences (regulations No. 38, point 53). So in a new outlet the rodding eyes must be closable, and the place for the non-return valve is worth agreeing already at the design stage.

From the open-air museum on Tīklu iela to Ragakāpa

The Jūrmala Open-Air Museum, with a 19th-century coastal fisherman’s farmstead, is at Tīklu iela 1a and was established in 1970. Towards the Lielupe stretches the urban planning monument “Stirnu rags”: the site of an old fishing village between Vikingu iela and the Lielupe, with an irregular street pattern. On an irregular plot the service pipe from the red line to the house may not run straight, so its length is measured along the real route, not on the map. In the form it helps to write how many metres it is from the house to the fence on the street side and whether there are trees, a shed or paving on the way.

Summer-house building between Loču, Vikingu and Tīklu iela took shape as early as the start of the twentieth century, when in 1910 the owner of the Buļļi manor, J. Erhards, divided part of the seaside forest into plots. While such a house is not connected to the street sewer, its holding tank, septic tank or local treatment unit must be entered in the register of SIA “Jūrmalas ūdens”. This is required by Jūrmala City Council binding regulations No. 37 (25 July 2024), points 28 and 29.

In the eastern part, block building appeared in the 1960s (20th century), along with the development of the “Uzvara” fishing collective farm. Behind them, a large part is taken by the Natura 2000 nature reserve “Lielupes grīvas pļavas”, the Lielupe port and moorings, and in the north rises Ragakāpa (18.2 m), where some pines are close to 300 years old. In a block of flats, work usually starts not in the yard but at the riser.

A riser in a 1960s block and a pipe to the red line

Riser replacement. “Water riser replaced on one floor” and “Soil stack replaced on one floor” each cost from €120 per floor, labour price, materials not included. In January 2024 one Jūrmala block of flats was left without cold water for 10 days, because part of the frozen water pipe ran under the floor of an unheated, uninhabited flat. So before riser work it is worth knowing which flats it passes through and whether they are warm. In a hot water riser the temperature is not low: since 2018 Cabinet Regulation No. 906 has required the building manager to provide hot water at the heat exchanger outlet of not less than +55 °C. So before the work the riser must be shut off and drained together with the building manager.

Replacing drain pipes in a private house in Buļļuciems can mean a new yard outlet up to the street inspection chamber. “Replacing waste pipe” costs from €25/m. An 18 m outlet from the house to the red line is at least €450 per job. After construction the owner submits an as-built survey plan and, if the gravity network is 200 mm in diameter or more, also a CCTV drain survey. The trip to Buļļuciems is included in both prices.

Often asked in Jūrmala

My house is not on Vikingu, Matrožu, Lašu or Jūrnieku iela. Are there networks there?

The first section in 2016–2017 covered these four streets. The contract “Buļļuciems–Bulduri” of 31 March 2020 provided networks on streets where there had been none before. Which streets it includes and where the connection already reaches the boundary, Jūrmalas ūdens can say exactly.

What should you know about the meter chamber in a Buļļuciems yard before winter?

Jūrmalas ūdens asks you to check that the chamber lid is intact and closes tightly and, where needed, to insulate the chamber with moisture-resistant materials, keeping access to the meter. If a meter is found frozen and damaged, the replacement and the work are paid by the user (regulations No. 38, point 26).

In Buļļuciems, can the yard trench be backfilled right after the pipes are laid?

No. In the phase IV area Jūrmalas ūdens requires the yard connection to be shown to a company specialist in the open trench before backfilling. The owner builds the connection alone or with a builder, and connecting is not compulsory.

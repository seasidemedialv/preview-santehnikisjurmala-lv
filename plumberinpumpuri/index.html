On the sea side of the railway there is dense detached-house building, and summer houses were built in dune hollows as early as the second half of the 19th century. In the dips between dunes in Jūrmala, a high groundwater level is typical, 0.5–2.0 m, so groundwater can be close to a basement and to a pipe laid under the floor.

A basement in a hollow and the water supply of an old villa

Water in a basement. In the storm of 29 July 2024, streets in Asari, Pumpuri and Melluži were like rivers. If after such a downpour water stands in the basement of a house in a hollow, it is pumped out by a drainage pump. Repairing a drainage or basement pump: the price depends on the pump type, the fault and access to the chamber. The pump’s outlet must not be led into the domestic sewer, because Jūrmala’s regulations forbid discharging rain, snowmelt and drainage water into it. More about pumps: basement pumps.

Replacing pipes in a private house. In the first phase of water management, the water supply on the Pumpuri-Jaundubulti section was reconstructed. Jūrmalas ūdens' responsibility nevertheless ends at the last gate valve before the property boundary; the networks on the plot and in the house belong to the owner. Replacing water pipe is from €10/m, and a 20 m yard section is at least €200 of work. Repiping a whole flat or house is from €500 per property, labour price, materials not included. In Pumpuri the trip is included in both labour prices.

From the Karlsbāde halt to the Rainis Pines

Pumpuri station began in 1877 as the Karlsbāde halt on the Riga-Tukums railway; in 1922 it was named Melluži I, and in 1939 it was renamed Pumpuri. Later the surrounding part of Jaundubulti and Melluži also took this name. So in a request it is safer to give the street and house number, not only the name of the district.

The street now called Upes iela used to be the boundary between the Jaundubulti land of the Sloka forestry and the Melluži land of Firks. Pumpuri belongs to an urban planning monument of national importance, whose value is the historic street network and the division of land units. So even an ordinary, unprotected house here stands in a monument area. Ask the National Heritage Board before the work whether your work needs permission, especially if it is a new ventilation riser through the roof or an outlet through the facade.

Rainis and Aspazija lived at Poruka prospekts 61 in 1904–1905, and in the dunes by the sea grow the so-called Rainis Pines. Until the First World War Pumpuri kept a rural character, with far fewer places of entertainment than in Dubulti or Majori. In a house built for summer, the water supply and drains may have been installed later than the building itself, so before opening walls it is worth finding out where the pipes run.

7.8 km of water main on streets that had no network

The phase IV contract “Dubulti-Pumpuri” (2020) provided for 7.8 km of water main, 11.6 km of sewer, 1.5 km of rising main and 6 new pumping stations on streets where there had been no network until then. After phase IV, centralised sewerage is available to 99% of Jūrmala’s residents.

After construction, a house that connects submits an as-built survey plan and two statements. One says that its own water source is not connected to the town system, the other that rain and drainage water is not discharged into the sewer (regulations No. 38, point 13). Gravity networks of 200 mm diameter and above also need a CCTV drain survey. For a plumber this brings one practical task: to separate the house’s indoor and yard sewer from everything that collects rain or groundwater.

A basement with a shower: shut-offs under point 34.4

If a basement has sanitary appliances (sinks, toilets, showers, floor drains) or drainage, Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 (point 34.4) require automatic shut-offs or gate valves on the sewer. If the shut-off fittings are missing from the user’s system, the rodding eyes are not closed airtight or non-return valves are not installed, the user is responsible for any consequences (point 53). In a house in a hollow with a basement shower, this is the first thing to check before another water point is added there.

Checked on 19 November 2026: Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 of 25 July 2024, points 13, 34.4 and 53.

Often asked in Jūrmala

How can you tell groundwater from a burst pipe in a Pumpuri basement?

Close all taps and look at the water meter. If it stands still but the damp in the basement grows after rain, it is most likely groundwater or rainwater, because in dune hollows groundwater in Jūrmala can be 0.5–2.0 m deep. If the meter turns, a water pipe is leaking somewhere.

What kind of pump suits a basement after a summer downpour?

One for clean water. For example, the manufacturer Pedrollo states that TOP drainage pumps are intended for clean water without abrasive particles, at temperatures up to +40 °C and with solid particles up to 10 mm. They are supplied with a float switch, and the smallest models (TOP 1-3) pump water out down to 14 mm above the floor.

How many pumping stations did the phase IV contract provide for Pumpuri streets?

The contract “Dubulti-Pumpuri” (2020) provided for 6 new pumping stations and 1.5 km of rising main on streets where there had been no network. What may be discharged into this network is set by Jūrmala’s regulations: the temperature of waste water must not exceed +40 °C, and its pH must be 6.8–8.0 (regulations No. 38, point 40.4).

The village has centralised water supply and sewerage, but for the new detached houses in the “Kosas” detailed plan, local water and a sealed wastewater holding reservoir are for now allowed.

The village network and the new house: two jobs in Spuņciems

Drain flushing. Spuņciems was the only village in Sala parish for which the regulator approved centralised water supply and sewerage tariffs in 2020. In the post-war years the village grew as the centre of the collective farm “Padomju Latvija”: from 96 residents in 1970 to 600 in 1989. In houses from that time, the indoor sewer may be just as old. Pressure flushing clears deposits from the whole pipe wall, where a drain rod only drills a way through. Flushing the drains with water pressure costs from €60, and Clearing a blocked indoor drain from €40.

Plumbing a new house. In the “Kosas” detailed plan, new detached houses may use local water supply and a sealed holding reservoir until the centralised networks are built along Dambja ceļš. If you build the indoor plumbing now, it is worth leaving room for an outlet through which the house can later be joined to the street network. One plumbing point in a new house costs from €55.

Spuņciems lies outside Jūrmala, 29 km from the centre of Riga and 27 km from the municipal centre, Mārupe; Jūrmala is on the other bank of the Lielupe. Getting to Spuņciems is part of these labour prices.

Along the spit from Sala church to the former Spuņupe

The centre of Spuņciems has the Sala parish administration, Sala primary school, a library, a sports and culture centre, a post office and the Sala St John Lutheran church. The village grew up by the former Sala manor, and its earlier name, Sala, was first mentioned in 1225. The parish administration is the nearest place to ask about local rules before a connection or a rebuild. In the 20th century the village was called Spuņņciems after the Spuņņupe, and it was legally renamed Spuņciems only after independence was restored, so the name may be written differently on old house plans.

The Spuņupe was dug in 1816 and 1817 to link Lake Babīte with the Lielupe. In the 20th century it was filled in, and its place is now crossed by the A10 Jūrmala bypass. Where the river was filled in, the ground is not natural but made up; if the route of a yard pipe runs through such a place, it needs a firm bed so that the pipe does not settle later.

The village is only 5 m above sea level. In the LVĢMC flood risk plan, flood maps have been prepared for the Lake Babīte polder area for sea surges with a 10% and 0.5% probability; whether a particular plot is in the map zone can be checked on the map itself. For a house with a basement it is worth knowing where its lowest point is and whether there is a pump there. In 2025, 624 people lived in Spuņciems, almost as many as in 1989, and next to the Soviet-era houses new detached houses are now being built, so one street can have both old and quite new plumbing.

Spuņciems networks, from the 2020 tariff to Dambja ceļš

In 2020 the Spuņciems water main and sewer were served by “Babītes siltums”. Now water and sewerage are provided by “Mārupes komunālie pakalpojumi”, and from 1 July 2026 it applies a single tariff across the whole municipality.

The “Kosas” detailed plan was approved by the Mārupe Municipality council on 29 June 2022. It states that local water supply and the sealed holding reservoir are a temporary solution until the centralised networks are built along Dambja ceļš. When that happens, Mārupe Municipality binding regulations No. 16/2025 provide co-funding for a private house’s connection if the main network is in the street by the property.

A house with its own water intake that will later also get a water main has one strict boundary: LBN 222-15 (point 150) forbids joining a drinking water system with a system whose water does not meet drinking water requirements. When switching to the network, the old water intake must be separated, not connected in parallel.

A sealed holding reservoir means that all the water used in the house ends up in the tank. A running toilet or tap fills the tank sooner. The Mārupe Municipality regulations calculate the emptying frequency of a holding tank from water consumption and tank volume.

Often asked in Jūrmala

Our house is in the “Kosas” detailed plan. Can the plumbing be built now?

Yes. For new detached houses the detailed plan for now allows local water supply and a sealed wastewater holding reservoir until the centralised networks are built along Dambja ceļš. The indoor plumbing can be built now, and the outlet can be positioned so that the house can later be joined to the network.

Is the water tariff in Spuņciems the same as elsewhere in Mārupe Municipality?

From 1 July 2026, “Mārupes komunālie pakalpojumi” applies a single water and sewerage tariff across the whole municipality where it provides the service. For the actual amount, see the company’s information.

My house in Spuņciems has a basement with a shower. How can it be protected from sewage backflow?

If a basement has a sink, shower or floor drain, a backwater valve or a lifting unit is used for protection. For example, ACO states the standard EN 13564 for its valves, and the Quatrix is a type 3 device for grey and black wastewater. For a Saniflo lifting unit, a loop in the rising main above the maximum backflow level is required, which is street level unless local requirements say otherwise.

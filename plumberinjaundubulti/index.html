Plumber in Jaundubulti - the water plant and large villa plots

A plumber in Jaundubulti works in the part of Jūrmala between Dubulti and Pumpuri, where it is about 2.3 km from the left bank of the Lielupe to the sea and the dune strip along the beach is wider than elsewhere.

Jaundubulti developed precisely for summer houses on large, regular plots, so the way from the street to the house tap tends to be longer here than in the old fishing villages.

One of four water plants and 4.0 mmol/l in one sample

Jaundubulti has one of the four Jūrmalas ūdens treatment plants, at about 1,923 m³ a day. The iron removal plant was reconstructed in the first phase of water management, and in the second phase nanofiltration was added to it to remove sulphates. In the 2014 contract, trunk water mains joined the Dzintari-Jaundubulti and Kauguri-Sloka systems, which had been separate until then.

In Jūrmalas ūdens' 2026 average values (as of 19 August 2026), sulphates at the Jaundubulti plant are 170 mg/l against a limit of 250 mg/l. In one sample of 22 May 2026, total hardness ranged from 2.33 mmol/l in Ķemeri to 4.0 mmol/l in Jaundubulti. According to a WHO document, water above 1.8 mmol/l counts as very hard, and Jūrmalas ūdens does not plan to supply moderately hard or soft water. In plumbing it shows as limescale on a water heater’s heating element, in tap cartridges and in aerators.

In the phase IV contract “Dubulti-Pumpuri”, new water and sewer networks were built in Jaundubulti too. If you connected afterwards, keep the documents for your service pipe: Jūrmala’s regulations (points 32 and 33) require you to keep water and sewer diagrams and as-built drawings with pipe diameters, depth, fittings and meter assemblies. If there are none, inventory drawings must be drawn up.

Along the former Karlsbādes iela to the tower house and the dunes

Dubultu prospekts used to be Karlsbādes iela. In 1848, 100 plots for summer houses were allotted on Sloka forestry land west of Dubulti, and the street was built by soldiers. The plots stayed large, and the service pipe from the boundary to the house can be long: the town networks are built up to the property boundary, but everything beyond it is the owner’s section. On a long service pipe, every joint is one more place where a leak can start.

At Strēlnieku prospekts 58 stands a residential building with an Art Nouveau tower-like extension and wood carving, and it is an architectural monument. In an old villa the bathroom or kitchen is often fitted later than the building itself, and the pipes run through places not originally meant for them. Before opening a new hole in a wall or facade, it is worth knowing the monument rules, described below.

The dune strip along the beach in Jaundubulti is wider than elsewhere in Jūrmala. If a villa near the dunes has a garden tap or an outdoor shower, they freeze first in winter. For example, the City of Toronto advises disconnecting garden hoses before frost, closing the separate valve of the outdoor tap and opening the outdoor tap so that the remaining water drains out.

A monument area: when you need permission before the work

Jaundubulti belongs to the urban planning monument of national importance “Vaivaru-Asaru-Mellužu-Pumpuru-Jaundubultu vasarnīcu rajons”. Article 21 of the law “On Protection of Cultural Monuments” provides that cultural monuments of national and regional importance may be repaired only with the written permission of the National Heritage Board, and monuments of local importance with municipal permission. Whether replacing a pipe needs permission, ask the National Heritage Board before the work, especially if the work touches the facade or a historic interior.

Law “On Protection of Cultural Monuments”, Article 21; checked on 19 November 2026.

Limescale in a water heater and taps in villa kitchens

Water heater cleaning. With hardness around 4.0 mmol/l, limescale builds up at the bottom of the water heater and on the heating element, and the water heats more slowly. A water heater clean-out with an anode check is from €75 per water heater, labour price, materials not included. During cleaning the anode is inspected, and a worn anode has to be replaced (the anode is a material); the manufacturer Lochinvar warns in its instructions that the anode must be in the water heater at all times.

Mixer taps. In hard water, cartridges and aerators clog up, and the tap starts to drip or the flow becomes uneven. Tap replacement (your own tap) is from €25 per tap, and replacing two taps is at least €50. Stopping a dripping tap: the price depends on the tap model, the spare part and access. In Jaundubulti the trip is included in each of these labour prices.

Often asked in Jūrmala

Why does limescale build up in a water heater in Jaundubulti if the plant has nanofiltration?

Nanofiltration was added to the Jaundubulti plant to remove sulphates. Since 2023 Jūrmalas ūdens has been gradually changing its nanofiltration membranes to reduce hardness, but in 2024 the hardness of the treated water was still 3.5–4.0 mmol/l. Extra softening is up to the consumers themselves.

Is the water in Jaundubulti the hardest in Jūrmala?

One sample does not prove it. The 4.0 mmol/l in Jaundubulti is from the sample of 22 May 2026, while Jūrmalas ūdens as a whole gives a total hardness of 3.21–4.25 mmol/l. The company publishes its latest measurements on its drinking-water quality page.

How deep should a yard water pipe go in the dune sand of Jaundubulti?

The LBN 003-19 table has no separate station for Jūrmala. For the nearest one, Riga, the standard for clay ground is 59 cm (recurring once in two years) and 91 cm (once in ten years). In sand this figure is multiplied by a coefficient of 1.2, which gives about 71 cm and 109 cm. The exact depth is set by the connection design.

Vaivari has wide forests and large plots, so the service pipe from the street to the house tends to be long, and a longer service pipe means more metres to protect from frost in winter.

The meter chamber in winter and point 26

Jūrmalas ūdens reminds users that a water meter in a chamber must be protected from frost for the winter: if at an inspection a meter is found frozen and damaged, the replacement and related work are paid by the user (regulations No. 38, point 26). The company asks you to check the chamber lid before winter, that it is intact and closes tightly, and, where needed, to insulate the chamber with moisture-resistant materials, keeping access to the meter. If the chamber stands by the street, far from the house, it is easy to forget in winter.

Checked on 19 November 2026: Jūrmala City Council binding regulations No. 38 of 25 July 2024, point 26; Jūrmalas ūdens recommendations on meter chambers.

From the Asari II halt to Alkšņu iela and the forest plots

Vaivari station opened in 1927 as the Asari II halt and was renamed Vaivari in 1938, like the surrounding part of Asari. Vaivari lay on the Firks land of Nurmuiža, the residents fished, and plots began to be allotted to summer visitors at the end of the 19th century. The older houses here were built for summer; if the water supply was added later, it tends to run through the crawl space or along an outer wall, where it is cold in winter.

Deep in Vaivari, by Alkšņu iela, there is a small group of detached houses with small plots. There the service pipes are short, and the houses are close to each other. It is worth knowing where your shut-off valve is: Jūrmalas ūdens advises a private house owner, in an emergency, to shut off the water to the particular room, or at the meter assembly for the whole building.

From Vēju iela to Kaugurciems, Vaivari has wide undeveloped forest areas, large plots and only a few large complexes. On a long yard service pipe a leak can stay unseen for a long time: if all the taps are closed but the meter turns, water is going somewhere on the way. Tracing a hidden leak: the price depends on the leak location, access and the testing method.

A service pipe to the street and a pipe that has frozen

Connecting to the networks. In phase IV the networks are brought to the plot boundary; the owner builds the yard connection alone or with a builder and, before backfilling, shows it to a Jūrmalas ūdens specialist in the open trench. Connecting is not compulsory, and the meter is installed by Jūrmalas ūdens after construction (regulations No. 38, point 11). Yard connection to the town network: the price depends on the length, the depth, restoring the surface and permits. “On-site inspection” (from €40 per visit) is the price when the plumber comes, looks and does no other work on that visit.

Frozen pipes. On a long service pipe and in a house built for summer, frost first finds exposed sections. For example, the City of Toronto advises insulating exposed pipes in basements, crawl spaces, garages and attics with foam sleeves. Thawing out frozen pipes: the price depends on the length of the frozen section, access and splits. In Vaivari the trip is part of the labour price.

A rising main from 1974 and the contracts of 2014 and 2020

In 1974–1976 an 11.5 km sewage rising main was built from Kauguri to Vaivari, through which waste water reached the Sloka treatment plant. On 21 January 2014 the phase II contract was signed for extending the water supply and sewerage in Kaugurciems, Vaivari, Asari and Melluži. On 25 September 2020, in phase IV, the contract “Extending the Water Management Networks in Sloka and Vaivari” was signed.

Whether the network is also on your street, ask Jūrmalas ūdens. The company prepares the technical conditions in 10 working days free of charge, and they are valid for two years from the date of registration. With them you can plan the yard service pipe and outlet, which on a large Vaivari plot is the larger part of the work.

If a house still uses its own holding tank or septic tank, Jūrmala City Council binding regulations No. 37 (25 July 2024) require it to be entered in the register of SIA “Jūrmalas ūdens” (points 28 and 29). They also require a septic tank to be emptied no less than once a year (point 8). After phase IV, centralised sewerage is available to 99% of Jūrmala’s residents, while the rest use such systems, so on a large forest plot both situations are possible. When the street connection is ready, the house outlet is redirected to the new branch.

Often asked in Jūrmala

In Vaivari, can rainwater collected in the yard be pumped into a sewer chamber?

No. In the storm of 29 July 2024, streets and yards flooded in Vaivari, but Jūrmala’s regulations forbid discharging rain, snowmelt and drainage water into the centralised sewer (regulations No. 38, points 45.8 and 45.9). In most of Jūrmala rainwater has a separate sewer, and the municipality is responsible for it.

What should not be used to warm a frozen pipe in a Vaivari house?

An open flame. The City of Toronto advises a heating pad, a hair dryer or warm towels, but never a blowtorch or a propane heater, because it creates a risk of fire and carbon monoxide. The City of Winnipeg also advises against a hair dryer and portable heaters. The advice differs, so in a timber house it is safer to call in a plumber.

We are buying a house in Vaivari without sewer documents. What should we know?

Jūrmala’s regulations (point 57) provide that when ownership changes or during reconstruction, if there is no as-built documentation for the sewer outlet, the owner must carry out a CCTV survey of the pipeline at their own expense and submit it to Jūrmalas ūdens. Before the deal you can request a plumbing check before buying: the price depends on the size of the house, the number of systems and the depth of the check.

Plumber in Ragaciems - homes between the Gausā jūdze and Lake Kaņieris

Ragaciems is a village of Lapmežciems parish in Tukums Municipality, south of Ragaciems cape (Ragaciema rags), between the Gulf of Riga and Lake Kaņieris by the P128.

It is our farthest village along the coast, 52 km from Riga, but here too getting there is already included in the published labour prices.

For a Ragaciems house: connecting a water heater and servicing before the season

Water heater fitting. A new water heater must be filled completely before the power is switched on, by opening the hot tap: this is what Lochinvar requires for its electric water heater. Wall-mounting a water heater: from €65 per water heater, labour price, materials not included.

Plumbing service. If a toilet or shower in the house is lower than the drain and a lifting unit pumps the water away, Saniflo recommends checking it visually once a month and having a specialist inspect it once a year. During a power cut, do not use the connected appliances. The service also looks at taps, traps, the water heater and shut-off valves. Seasonal plumbing service: the price depends on the size of the property, the number of points to check and the defects found.

For the rising main of a lifting unit, Saniflo requires an anti-backflow loop above street level, and at the service this spot is checked together with the inlet shut-off valves.

Ragaciems lies outside Jūrmala, in Tukums Municipality. “On-site inspection” (from €40) is the price here only when the plumber comes, inspects and does no other work.

From the lighthouses on the cape to the Starpiņupīte

The village’s name comes from Ragaciems cape, and the village has an old and a new lighthouse and a fish market. Fish cleaned in a house kitchen can leave scales and fat in the sink trap. A trap that can be unscrewed by hand is the first place to check if water drains slowly. Fish guts and scales are better put in the rubbish than washed down.

Along the coast there is an official bathing place by the “Aizrags” car park and an unofficial beach at the end of Zvejnieku iela. If the yard has an outdoor shower for rinsing off sand, its pipe usually runs above ground or shallow. Before frost it must be shut off with a separate valve in the house and drained, because that is where water freezes first. In spring it is opened last, when frosty nights are no longer expected.

In the south of the village flows the Starpiņupīte, the outflow of Lake Kaņieris into the Gulf of Riga, and it is 3 km to Lapmežciems. The two villages have in effect merged and are in one parish, to which the “Tukuma ūdens” tariffs apply from 1 December 2026. For a house connected to the network, connection and meter questions are for “Tukuma ūdens”, while the pipes beyond the meter are the plumber’s work.

Ragaciems in summer and winter: the Gausā jūdze and 600 residents

The coast between Ragaciems and Klapkalnciems is known as the Gausā jūdze, one of the most popular access points to the sea in the municipality. In summer swimmers and holidaymakers come here. If the house is a summer house and is not heated in winter, the Saniflo SANICUBIC instructions require the toilet, pipes and macerator pump to be prepared with antifreeze made for such systems or drained completely.

In 2025, 600 people lived in Ragaciems in an area of 1.44 km², and 662 in 1989. In houses lived in all year, water and heating work in winter too. If the heating loop is protected with antifreeze, the manufacturer Sentinel states for X500 that it must not be used in drinking water systems, the dose is at least 20%, and at 20% the solution freezes at about −6 °C. At 50% the manufacturer states about −29 °C, so the dose is chosen by how cold the house may get.

Tukums regulations No. 16 and the Ragaciems village boundaries

The requirements of Tukums Municipality binding regulations No. 16 (23 February 2022) for decentralised sewerage apply in villages whose boundaries are set in the territorial plan. If your plot is within these boundaries and the house has a septic tank, it has to be emptied at least yearly. Whether a plot falls within the village boundaries can be seen in the Tukums Municipality territorial plan, and you can ask the municipality.

Tukums Municipality binding regulations No. 16 (23 February 2022) on the procedure for providing and recording decentralised sewerage services (likumi.lv), checked 19 November 2026.

Often asked in Jūrmala

After the beach by Zvejnieku iela, sand collects in the shower floor drain. What should I do?

Sand settles in the water seal of the floor drain and narrows the path of the waste pipe. The grate and the bucket of a floor drain can usually be taken out and rinsed by yourself. If the water still drains slowly afterwards, the sand is further along the pipe. Clearing a blocked indoor drain: from €40 per job.

In winter we keep the Ragaciems house at a low temperature. What should we do with the radiator thermostats?

For example, on an Oventrop thermostatic head, position 0 shuts the valve, while the snowflake position opens it if the room cools below 6 °C. So in an empty house the heads are left on the snowflake, not on zero. This does not protect pipes under the floor or in an unheated room; they must be insulated or drained.

The house has a septic tank. What do you do, and what does the pumping-out service do?

We do not pump out septic tanks; in Tukums Municipality a septic tank has to be pumped out at least yearly, and this is done by a septage service provider. We take care of the pipe running from the house to the septic tank, and of the sewerage inside the house. Cleaning the yard drain line: from €50 per job.

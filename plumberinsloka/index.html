Plumber in Sloka - connections, drains and the historic centre

A plumber in Sloka works in the western part of Jūrmala, on the left bank of the Lielupe, about 2 km from the Gulf of Riga, where the river turns sharply east.

In 2010 only 52% of residents here used town water, and the networks were extended in 2015 and under the phase IV contracts. So plumbing in a Sloka detached house also covers the yard: the service pipe, the outlet and the old wastewater system.

From Sloka station to the paper mill landing on the Lielupe

Sloka railway station opened in 1877, and the territorial plan lists it among the notable sites of the Kauguri and Sloka part. Sloka is mostly dense detached housing, standing next to large industrial areas. For a detached house, everything beyond the last gate valve before the property boundary is the house’s own network. Jūrmalas ūdens (Jūrmala’s water utility) is responsible for the water main up to that valve and for the sewer up to the last inspection chamber before the boundary.

Further on is the historic centre, with the Lutheran church built in 1851 and the market square. Buildings here typically stand along the street frontage, and the urban planning monument protects the street layout and the central square, not every house separately. For plumbing, this means that changing a tap, toilet or water heater in an ordinary house is usually not tied to the monument status, but some buildings have their own rules (more below).

The road ends at the Lielupe, where the plan mentions the former Sloka paper mill site with its landing, an island in the river and the Duke Jacob’s Canal. Sloka-Kauguri is one of the nine land drainage districts of Jūrmala, and ditch water from Sloka flows to the Vecslocene and the Lielupe; a ditch is a land drainage system, not a place for house waste pipes. Jūrmala’s wastewater treatment plant also operates in Sloka: biological works handed over in 2009, with a capacity of 9,050 m³ a day, treating about 70% of the city’s wastewater. Everything that enters the drains has to be separated out there, so Jūrmalas ūdens asks people not to flush wet wipes down the toilet (see the questions below).

The Kauguri-Sloka system and streets that got the network later

In 2010 Sloka was in the Kauguri-Sloka water supply system, and 3,099 of 5,923 residents used centralised water. In December 2015, 7 km of water main, 13.2 km of gravity sewer and 531 sewer branches were handed over in Kauguri and Sloka. At the opening a Jūrmalas ūdens representative said that cesspits are not the safest solution for the city, especially by the river, where groundwater is high.

For phase IV, networks were designed in 2018 for further Sloka streets, for example Dīķu, Litenes, Dzirnavu, Krasta, Fabrikas, Varoņu and Leona Paegles iela. In 2020 the contract “Sloka and Vaivari” was signed for construction, in 2022 “Kaugurciems - Sloka”: almost 7 km of water main, about 6.4 km of gravity sewer and 2 pumping stations in the Varoņu iela area in Sloka and in Valteri.

In phase IV the network is brought to the plot boundary. The owner builds the yard section, alone or with a builder, and shows it to a Jūrmalas ūdens specialist in the open trench before backfilling; connecting is not compulsory. If you connect, your own wastewater storage structures must then be closed down (binding regulations No. 38, point 15). Jūrmalas ūdens can say for certain whether the network already reaches your section of the street.

Yard service pipe and drains in a Sloka detached house

Connection to the town networks. For the line “Yard connection to the town network” (€/m), the price depends on length, depth, restoring the surface and permits, so it is set after an on-site inspection. If the house needs its waste pipes re-routed to the new outlet, each “Extra water or waste outlet” costs from €35 per point.

Clearing drains. “Clearing a blocked indoor drain” costs from €40 per job, “Cleaning the yard drain line” from €50 per job; both are labour prices, materials not included. Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 (point 34.5) require sewer networks in their service zone to be cleaned as a precaution at least twice a year. For a detached house with a long yard outlet, this means a blockage can be found in the inspection chamber before water starts to rise through the floor drain.

The church, six buildings on Raiņa iela and monument No. 6089

Sloka historic centre is an urban planning monument of national importance, No. 6089, approved in 1998, and its protection zone is 100 m. Sloka Lutheran church is under state protection, and the municipality has given six buildings on Raiņa iela the status of protected development. Article 21 of the law “On Protection of Cultural Monuments” says that a monument of national importance may be repaired only with written permission from the National Heritage Board.

If work in a protected building touches walls, floors or foundations, for example the route of a new sewer outlet, ask about permission before it: the National Heritage Board for the church, the municipality for the Raiņa iela buildings. In the LVĢMC flood risk plan, the Sloka historic centre is named among Jūrmala’s cultural and historical sites exposed to flood risk. If a basement has a sink, toilet, shower or floor drains, regulations No. 38 (point 34.4) require automatic shut-offs or gate valves on the sewer.

The law “On Protection of Cultural Monuments”, Article 21; Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38, points 15, 34.4 and 34.5. Checked 19 November 2026.

Often asked in Jūrmala

Does plumbing work in Sloka historic centre need monument permission?

Monument No. 6089 protects the street layout and the central square, not every building, so changing an ordinary tap or water heater is usually not tied to it. The Lutheran church is under state protection, and six buildings on Raiņa iela have the municipality’s protected development status. Before work that touches the structure, ask the National Heritage Board for the church and the municipality for the Raiņa iela buildings.

May a Sloka detached house's drainage be connected to the new sewer?

No. Jūrmala binding regulations No. 38 (points 45.8 and 45.9) forbid sending rain, melted snow and plot drainage water into the domestic sewer, and when connecting to the networks the owner confirms this in writing (point 13.4). Plan the outlet of a drainage pump separately from the domestic waste pipes.

What do the Sloka treatment works ask people not to put down the drains?

Jūrmalas ūdens reports that the Sloka works treat about 2.7 million m³ of wastewater a year and screen out 14 tonnes of waste. Wet wipes belong in the rubbish, even when the packaging says “flushable”. In a house they can catch at the first bend behind the toilet, and then a blockage has to be cleared.
